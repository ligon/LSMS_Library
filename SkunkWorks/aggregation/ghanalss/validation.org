* Published partition

The country =Aggregate Label= column combines four groups.  The 215
Preferred labels delivered across seven waves map to 209 groups; every
other label, tobacco included, retains its exact Preferred name.  This is
a conservative semantic partition passed through the beta-spread gate, not
an estimate of an optimal aggregation.

* The gate

The rule is the parent skill's: for every multi-member bucket,
=max(beta) - min(beta) < 1.348 * sigma_min= (a 50% two-sided normal
interval), beta and sigma from ONE CFE fit containing every member --
CFE's beta is a rank-1 factor identified up to a per-fit scale, so loadings
from two fits are not comparable.  Where every member survives the full
fine preparation the fine fit is used; otherwise the members are fitted
TOGETHER beside the eight densest non-member foods and Salt (PR #937's
probe basket), and that one probe supplies beta and sigma.  Sigma is
CFEDemands 0.10.0's resampling bootstrap
(=Regression.get_beta(compute_se=True)=, >= 30 draws of the residual matrix
within Region, IQR-based), seeded so a rerun reproduces it.  Implementation:
[[file:../ghana_gate.py][ghana_gate.py]]; outputs =gate_*/gate.csv=, =loadings.csv=,
=probe_loadings.csv=, =gate_specification.yml=.

Populations.  Primary: 2016-17 purchased amounts summed over the six
recalls, Region markets, PR #937's controls, on its seed-20260916 60%
training split (8,397 households; 86 goods survive the fine preparation).
CFE's =prepare_data= keeps a pair of goods only if at least =min_obs=30=
households report both, so a sparse pair cannot be jointly identified on
the split (Macaroni x Spaghetti: 45 households in the whole wave); such
groups are gated on the full 2016-17 wave (14,009 households, 93 goods),
which is the stricter population, not a relaxation.  A group must ALSO
pass in every other wave where two or more of its members are delivered:
2012-13, 2005-06 and 1998-99 were gated on their full waves (16,772 /
8,687 / 5,998 households; 83 / 76 / 58 goods).  1991-92 is not gated: its
rural households are asked over 14 days and its urban ones over 30, and a
Region market does not absorb that.  Sensitivity runs -- a second split
seed (20260917), the total recorded-value basis -- are reported and are
not decisive.  Scoring in the validation below uses =min_goods=1=
explicitly (PR #937 =e70b8df=); whether the ranking is stable in
=min_goods= is GH #939 and was not attempted.

Borderline verdicts a human may want to revisit: =Goat/Mutton= (passes
every 2016-17 run, fails 2012-13 at 1.08, sits at 1.00 in 2005-06),
=Crab and shrimp= (the split and the full wave disagree), the sugar
group's total-basis 1.87 and the malt group's full-wave 1.13.  All four
are documented with the conservative choice: the primary decides for
2016-17, and a group has to be sound in every wave it merges.  Not
gateable by construction, and singletons: =Margarine= / =Margarine/Butter=
(never co-occur; no =Butter= label exists), =Potato= / =Sweet Potato=
(Sweet Potato is own-production only from 1991-92 on), =Onion= / =Shallot=
(Onion is a reserved target).

Loadings in Salt units on the primary population, for the selected groups:
Sugar (cubed) 1.87 +- 0.85 and Sugar (granulated) 2.03 +- 0.13; Malt Drinks
(bottle) 1.46 +- 0.25 and (canned) 1.73 +- 0.32; Macaroni 0.90 +- 0.26 and
Spaghetti 0.98 +- 0.20 (full wave); Millet 1.60 +- 0.71 and Guinea
Corn/Sorghum 2.19 +- 0.89.  Note what the rule does and does not say: a
sparse member with a wide sigma passes easily (the skill's own instruction
is to place sparse items by conceptual fit), so a pass is "not
distinguishable at 50% confidence", never "equal".

* Held-out validation

Design as PR #937: fixed 60/20/20 household split within Region x Rural
(seed 20260916), the CFEDemands 0.10.0 preparation and frozen scoring with
=min_obs=30=, =min_prop_items=0.1=, =alltm=False=, =min_goods=1=, Salt as
the singleton anchor, Onion and Egg reserved as prediction targets, survey
weights in coverage, normalisation and the prediction diagnostics.  The
fine map and the four-group map (=draft= in the CSVs) are compared on the
validation split on both bases and once on the final test split; the
memberships were frozen before the test split was examined.

The four-group map admits one more fitted good than the fine map on each
basis (the merged sugar / pasta / millet items clear the pairwise support
they lacked separately); fitting and scoring coverage are unchanged to four
decimals; every reserved-food difference is within its conditional
interval, on the test split slightly against the aggregation (Onion +0.2%,
Egg +0.02%) and on the validation split slightly for it.

As with PR #937, the group support gains do not translate into scored
coverage gains (frozen scoring already reaches almost every household), and
the reserved-food prediction differences are within their conditional
intervals.  These checks support a limited practical partition; they do not
establish a welfare-precision gain.  The fine Preferred output remains the
default.

* Publication and verification

=publish_ghana.py= wrote the =Aggregate Label= column after =Preferred
Label= for all 219 rows (215 distinct Preferred labels; the four extra rows
are the per-wave spelling rows of one label and carry the same Aggregate);
it appended NO rows -- post-#935 the served axis is the country table --
and the writer now refuses an append by default.  Wave and FCT cells are
untouched.  =nutrition.py= recognises =YYYY-YY= wave columns explicitly
(PR #937's change) so the new column is not read as a survey round.

Tests: =tests/test_ghanalss_aggregate.py= (configuration: selection
disjoint and on the country axis, publication universe = country axis,
every wave label named in its country column, the column complete and
unambiguous and equal to the selection; delivered: =food_acquired= is a
row-preserving rename and =food_expenditures= a sum of levels on both
bases, all seven waves), plus the canonical-label, nutrition, label,
schema and derivations suites and the four =food_prices= /
=food_quantities= pins, which the default =j= does not read.  Results are
in the PR.

* Reproduction

From the worktree root, with CFEDemands 0.10.0 shadowed ahead of the venv:

#+begin_example
export OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 MKL_NUM_THREADS=1 LSMS_BUILD_WORKERS=1
export PYTHONPATH="<cfe010>:$PWD:$PWD/SkunkWorks"
G=SkunkWorks/aggregation/ghanalss

python -m SkunkWorks.aggregation.ghana_inventory
python -m SkunkWorks.aggregation.ghana_gate --wave 2016-17 --basis purchased --output $G/gate_2016-17_purchased
python -m SkunkWorks.aggregation.ghana_gate --wave 2016-17 --basis purchased --seed 20260917 --output $G/gate_2016-17_purchased_seed2
python -m SkunkWorks.aggregation.ghana_gate --wave 2016-17 --basis total --output $G/gate_2016-17_total
for w in 2016-17 2012-13 2005-06 1998-99; do
  python -m SkunkWorks.aggregation.ghana_gate --wave $w --basis purchased --population all --output $G/gate_${w}_purchased_all
done
for basis in purchased total; do
  python -m SkunkWorks.aggregation.ghana_validation --basis $basis --names fine draft \
    --specification $G/selected_groups.yml --output $G/validation_selected_$basis
done
python -m SkunkWorks.aggregation.ghana_validation --names fine draft --evaluation test \
  --specification $G/selected_groups.yml --output $G/test_selected
python -m SkunkWorks.aggregation.publish_ghana          # refuses an append
#+end_example

PR #937's other outputs (=validation_purchased=, =validation_additional=,
=bootstrap_purchased=, =probes_purchased=, =simulation_selected=) are kept
as the record of the pre-#935 run and were not regenerated; their
=specification.yml= files name the revision they were made from.
