* Result

This records the inventory that the 2026-09-17 selection
([[file:validation.org][validation and publication report]]) was made from.  It supersedes the
2026-09-16 inventory of PR #937, which measured the WAVE vocabulary that
leaked through =food_acquired= before PR #935 applied the wave->country
crosswalk (233 delivered labels, 47 absent from the country table).  That
run's numbers are retained in git history only.

GhanaLSS delivers *215* distinct Preferred labels across its seven waves,
and the country =harmonize_food= table has *215* distinct Preferred labels:
the two sets are identical (=inventory.yml=:
=delivered_labels_absent_from_country: []=,
=country_labels_not_delivered: []=).  Every proposed member is delivered.
The [[file:proposed_groups.yml][candidate specification]] names 18 groups to gate plus six deferred
alternatives; applied all at once it would map the 215 labels to 188
groups ([[file:draft_partition.yml][draft partition]]).  Three tobacco labels are inventoried and
excluded from food support.  Nothing in this file is a live mapping: the
published column is the [[file:selected_groups.yml][selection]].

* Observed support

Counts use positive monetary amounts.  Zero and unavailable amounts both
contribute zero; a price-only row does not establish positive expenditure.
The denominator is the entire API =sample= for the wave, including
households with no food records.  Tobacco is excluded.  The two bases are
purchased expenditure and total recorded acquisition value, using the
library's existing monetary derivation without additional valuation.

Only the per-wave label counts moved against the pre-#935 inventory
(2005-06 111 -> 113, 2012-13 112 -> 114, 2016-17 176 -> 178, the early
waves unchanged): the crosswalk un-merges items previously conflated under
one label (mutton under =Goat=, pito under =Beer=/=Wine=), and the
medians and densities are unchanged to the third decimal.

Pair support is the binding constraint on the gate.  Across the 178 food
labels in 2016-17, the median number of households reporting both members
of an unordered pair is 36 for purchases and 56 for total recorded value,
and only 52.6% / 59.3% of pairs have at least 30 such households.  CFE's
=prepare_data= keeps a pair of goods only if at least =min_obs=30=
households report BOTH, which is why several candidates cannot be gated on
the 60% training split at all (=Macaroni= x =Spaghetti=: 45 households in
the whole wave) and why five can be gated on no population (see
=validation.org=).

  =Sugar (cubed/granulated)=, =Malt Drinks=, =Macaroni/Spaghetti= (label

  (imported/local), =Gari= (white/yellow), =Fish (smoked)= (river/sea),
  =Millet/Sorghum= (millet, millet flour, guinea corn/sorghum; and a

  Butter=, =Cocoa Powder= (with the malted-drink group), =Goat/Mutton=,
  =Beer/Pito=.
- *PR #937's additional semantic candidates* re-keyed where a member was a
  leaked wave spelling (=Milk (tinned, condensed)= -> =Milk (tinned,
  condensed/unsweetened)=; =Pepper (dried, red)= -> =Pepper (dried)=).

Three pairs are listed as =not_gateable= and default to singletons:
=Margarine= / =Margarine/Butter= (never co-occur; no =Butter= label
anywhere to test against), =Potato= / =Sweet Potato= (Sweet Potato is

reserved prediction target).

* Survey qualifications

The [[file:../../../lsms_library/countries/GhanaLSS/_/CONTENTS.org][country notes]] document different recall designs.  GLSS7 has six
five-day food recalls; GLSS3 uses seven rural and ten urban asks with
different recall lengths, which is why 1991-92 is not used to gate anything
here (a Region market does not absorb an urban/rural exposure gap).  The
table above is an inventory of observed support, not evidence that all
waves measure a common consumption period.

Total recorded value includes the API's existing own-production amounts
(=8h-farmgate= for 1998-99..2016-17, =12b-fortnight= for GLSS1/2; GLSS3
own-production expenditure is not wired).  Two households with positive
food amounts lie outside =sample= in each of 1987-88 and 1988-89 and are
reported separately; there are none in the other five waves.

* Reproduction

From the worktree root, with CFEDemands 0.10.0 shadowed ahead of the venv
(=pip install --no-deps --target <dir> 'CFEDemands==0.10.0'=; the mirror
venv carries 0.8.2, which has no =score_w=):

#+begin_example
export OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 MKL_NUM_THREADS=1 LSMS_BUILD_WORKERS=1
export PYTHONPATH="<dir>:$PWD:$PWD/SkunkWorks"
python -m SkunkWorks.aggregation.ghana_inventory
#+end_example

=inventory.yml= records the label reconciliation (empty both ways), the
non-foods, the configuration revision (=ff4c00f2b=, the =development= merge
of #935) and the API warning counts (3 =NullReadWarning=, 5
=QuantityImplausibleWarning=, 1 =NullIndexKeyWarning=, all pre-existing
data audits).  Saved outputs contain only item, group, wave and visit
summaries; no household identifiers.
