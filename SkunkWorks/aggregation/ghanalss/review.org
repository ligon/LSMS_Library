* Result

GhanaLSS delivers 233 distinct Preferred labels across its seven waves.
The [[file:draft_partition.yml][draft partition]] maps these to 225 groups: five proposed merges
cover 13 labels, and the other 220 labels remain singletons. Four tobacco
labels are retained in the inventory but excluded from food support
calculations. No live =Aggregate Label= column has been added.

The country =harmonize_food= table has 195 distinct Preferred labels, but
47 delivered labels are absent from that axis. Nine country Preferred
labels are not delivered. The country table's wave columns sometimes
record broader crosswalks; they cannot be applied as spelling corrections.
The [[file:item_review.csv][item review]] preserves the exact delivered identities, their raw-code
and raw-label evidence, documentary crosswalk targets, draft assignments,
and reasons. Every delivered label has a corresponding wave Preferred
label. Historical wave Aggregate assignments impose no constraint on this
draft: the API reads a country-level column when =labels='Aggregate'= is
requested.

* Observed support

Counts use positive monetary amounts. Zero and unavailable amounts both
contribute zero; a price-only row does not establish positive expenditure.
The denominator is the entire API =sample= for the wave, including households
with no food records. Tobacco is excluded. The two bases are purchased
expenditure and total recorded acquisition value, using the library's
existing monetary derivation without additional valuation.

Summing visits substantially increases apparent coverage. In 2016-17 the
median purchased-food count among households reporting positive purchases
is 11 at visit 2 and 10 at each of visits 3--7. On the total basis the
corresponding medians are 13 and 12. These visit-level medians condition on
positive reporting; they are not the full-sample medians in the table.
The [[file:visit_support.csv][visit report]] gives counts without assuming that every non-reporting
household completed every visit.

Pair support is also limited. Across the 176 food labels in 2016-17, the
median number of households reporting both members of an unordered pair is
36 for purchases and 56 for total recorded value, even after pooling visits.
Only 52.6% and 59.3% of pairs, respectively, have at least 30 such households.
These are wave-wide counts, before any market/time conditioning or training
split; 30 is a descriptive threshold, not a certification rule. Full counts
and weighted prevalence are in [[file:profiles.csv][profiles.csv]] and [[file:item_support.csv][item_support.csv]].

* Five candidate groups

The [[file:proposed_groups.yml][specification]] records exact members and source-table evidence. These
are semantic candidates; similar loadings or error variances have not been
established.

- Rice: generic, imported, and local unprepared rice. Excludes prepared
  dishes. Origin may still distinguish quality and household selection.
- Sugar: cubed and granulated sugar only. Generic =Sugar= remains separate:
  the 1987-88 source combines "Sugar, candy, honey and sugarcane". A single
  country-wide assignment cannot treat that label as pure refined sugar
  just because later instruments use it more narrowly.
- Malt drinks: bottled and canned forms in 2016-17, with overlapping
  brands. Earlier source rows say "malta and malt drinks" without naming
  packaging. Packaging can still distinguish serving size and purchasers.
- Pasta: macaroni, spaghetti, and the earlier combined label. Excludes
  instant noodles, couscous, and other cereals.
- Gari: =Cassava (flour)= and its white/yellow variants. The consumption
  source labels explicitly say Gari/Garri. Excludes fresh cassava, dough,
  and dried cassava/Kokonte.

Each candidate has two delivered members in 2016-17. The purchased-basis
gains are 3279 for rice and 313 for gari; the other three are unchanged.
Rice also combines distinct labels in 2005-06 and 2012-13. Earlier generic
rice, pasta and gari labels are renamed by the all-wave draft, but this
does not combine distinct items within those earlier waves. See
[[file:group_support.csv][group_support.csv]] for all comparisons.

Rice and malt drinks supply the largest increases in group support. Pasta
has only 45 households reporting both members over the whole wave, so a
loading-comparison rule must distinguish weak evidence from similarity.
The already well-supported granulated sugar dominates its candidate's
coverage; a plausible semantic merge need not offer much statistical gain.

The specification also records four deferred alternatives: chicken cuts,
pork cuts, smoked fish, and sausages. They are not applied to the draft.
Cuts, species, processing, and formulations may matter. Other broad source
bundles remain separate: modern =Beef= includes other cow products, =Goat=
includes mutton, and =Chicken (frozen)= is sourced from "Other chicken
product". Delivered Preferred labels are already a coarsening; this step
cannot recover their components.

* Survey qualifications

The [[file:../../../lsms_library/countries/GhanaLSS/_/CONTENTS.org][country notes]] document different recall designs. GLSS7 has six
five-day food recalls; GLSS3 uses seven rural and ten urban asks with
different recall lengths and imperfect agreement between field schedules
and the analysis Rural flag. The table above is an inventory of observed
support, not evidence that all waves measure a common consumption period.
The CFE evaluation must specify its household/time cell before fitting.

Total recorded value includes the API's existing own-production amounts.
Modern Section 8H derives amounts from quantity times a reported unit
value. Its hypothetical selling-price wording and its internal
=8h-farmgate= derivation key do not establish a farmgate economic price:
the later country note, "What food_acquired.Price on produced rows
MEASURES", reports strong agreement with purchase unit values. GLSS1/2
use a different construction. GLSS3 own-production expenditure is not
wired, so the two reported bases coincide there.

Two households with positive food amounts lie outside =sample= in each
of 1987-88 and 1988-89. Their labels and expenditure totals are retained
in the inventory, with separate counts and amounts in the reports. They
are excluded from sample prevalence calculations; the discrepancy remains
to be resolved before using those waves for estimation. There are no such
households in the other five waves. Every sampled household has a positive
finite weight in this run.

* Reproduction and next evaluation

From the LSMS_Library root:

#+begin_example
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 MKL_NUM_THREADS=1 \
LSMS_BUILD_WORKERS=1 PYTHONPATH="$PWD" \
.venv/bin/python -m SkunkWorks.aggregation.ghana_inventory

OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 MKL_NUM_THREADS=1 \
PYTHONPATH="$PWD/../CFEDemands:$PWD" \
.venv/bin/python -m pytest --confcutdir=SkunkWorks/aggregation \
  SkunkWorks/aggregation -q
#+end_example

The driver uses the Country API and its normal caches. Saved experiment
outputs contain only item, group, wave, and visit summaries; they contain
no household identifiers. [[file:inventory.yml][inventory.yml]] records label discrepancies,
nonfoods, the extraction/configuration revision, and API warning counts.
The [[file:../../../.coder/ledger/ghanalss-aggregate-curation.md][ledger]] records reuse decisions and checks.

The next experiment should start with 2016-17 and compare the fine map,
each merge separately, and the combined draft using the existing
[[file:../evaluation.org][partition evaluator]]. Fix expenditure basis, time cells, training and
validation households, normalization, and evaluation population in advance.
Report changes in preparation retention and held-out score coverage, then
evaluate the approximation and measurement-error costs. Real-data stability
or prediction checks cannot establish MSE for unobserved true \(w\);
simulations using Ghana's observed masks can examine specified mechanisms.

Only after that comparison should accepted groups become a country-level
=Aggregate Label= column. Publication must first cover the actual delivered
axis, including the 47 labels absent from the current country Preferred
column, and preserve the separate nutritional crosswalk. Agreement among
the unused wave Aggregate columns is not a prerequisite.
