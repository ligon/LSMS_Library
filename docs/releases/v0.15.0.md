# v0.15.0

Draft prepared 2026-10-14 against integration commit
`7fa05179c8519f44ea329ba6387f5d6d6441a8ea`, with the separately reviewed
historical #837 disposition. This is the full change set since v0.14.0,
including work merged in September.

**Release validation is still pending.** The integrated local suite passed
4,951 tests with no failures. The separate cache-presence follow-up passed
2,416 checks and exposed the one held nutrition baseline failure. Installed
native Windows validation also passed. The cold
corpus completed 549 declared requests: 530 succeeded and 19 retain documented
source or wiring gaps. The Feature audit completed with findings, including
five pre-existing tenure-vocabulary failures. Controlled credentialed CI stopped
on a GhanaLSS build timeout before a suite summary; a test-environment leak was
subsequently reproduced and corrected in a candidate now undergoing full CI. Uganda's historical
nutrition baseline has an explicit scientific approval hold; its fixture has

described below. These draft notes do not declare the release ready.

## New survey coverage and discovery

**GhanaAHIES adds the Ghana Annual Household Income and Expenditure Survey,
2022Q1–2024Q4.** Eight tables cover all twelve quarters: `sample`,
`household_roster`, `individual_education`, `food_security`, `housing`,
`cluster_features`, `people_last7days` and `anthropometry`.
`household_characteristics` derives from the roster. The three annual source
folders retain quarterly `t` values and weights, with a stable household key
constructed from cluster and household number; the source `hhid` changes by
quarter and is not used as the panel identity. [PR #944](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/pull/944),
[PR #950](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/pull/950).

The survey's consumption, expenditure, income and asset modules are not
available as complete tables in the distributed files, so the library does
not claim those features. Source caveats remain visible: 2023Q3 household
weights have a half-scale anomaly, and some quarters contain nonnegative
anthropometric values that may encode nonmeasurement. They are recorded rather
than corrected without a decision. GPS is not supplied. See the
[country documentation](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/blob/development/lsms_library/countries/GhanaAHIES/_/CONTENTS.org).

**Wave discovery now represents multiple holdings and catalog relationships.**
Provenance can record several catalog IDs, constituent coverage, evidenced
aliases and derived releases without treating them as additional acquired
microdata. Tanzania's four constituent NPS releases can report `covered` by
the 2008–15 holding, and Nigeria's derived catalog entry can report `derived`;
the existing Boolean `local` remains false for those statuses. Existing scalar
catalog-ID and constructor behavior is retained. Missing or incomplete
provenance remains uncertain. [PR #992](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/pull/992).

- **Niger 2014–15 households include `EXTENSION` in their identity.** This
  restores 59 distinct households and their person and agriculture records.
  Exact all-nines missing-value codes are decoded before arithmetic. Crop sales
  carry `Unit_sold`, repeated sale exports are removed before summing, and
  selected food/region spelling variants are harmonized. Seed-slot collisions,
  orphan harvest records and the cross-wave split-off namespace caveat remain
  documented. [PR #993](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/pull/993),
  [#679](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/issues/679),

  [#824](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/issues/824).
- **Nigeria W1/W2 crop sales retain their recorded sale unit.** This does not
  supply the missing plot link for W3–W5 household-crop sales or recover other
  buyer channels. W5 now supplies cluster geography in both 2023Q3 and 2024Q1,
  using household-specific keys for EA=0 movers. The repository's v01 extract
  lacks GPS; the available v02 geovars are a separate acquisition tracked in
  [#978](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/issues/978). The old claim that
  the survey supplies no W5 geovariables has been corrected.
  [PR #994](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/pull/994),
  [PR #995](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/pull/995).
- **Malawi preserves the sale's own shelled/unshelled basis and distinguishes
  boiled groundnuts.** Registered derivations disclose the sale-basis ladder,
  including price-supported overrides and a separate no-reference outcome;
  null-unit sales remain in basis references. This does not resolve every
  crop-to-food identity or processing comparison. [PR #966](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/pull/966).
- **Uganda reads the second season-B harvest-condition slot for 2018–19 and
  2019–20.** The pending food-acquisition candidate distinguishes source-confirmed money-only
  rows, unknown units, residual codes and the recorded fish-unit label instead
  of converting all 7,359 old blank-unit rows through fabricated factors.
  Quantities and expenditures at acquisition grain are preserved; inferred kg
  and nutrition outputs change. [PR #972](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/pull/972),
  [PR #967](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/pull/967).
- **Burkina Faso 2014 preserves missing quantity.** The four visits remain
  separate seven-day recalls; only passage 1 has a quantity grid. Exactly
  449,033 manufactured zero quantities become missing, while expenditure,
  other quantities and row coverage remain unchanged in the cold comparison.
  Food-label crosswalk parsing is repaired. Price diagnostics distinguish
  money-only groups, with the limitation that an all-null quantity column
  alone cannot prove questionnaire coverage. [PR #981](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/pull/981).
- **Peru 1994 gains `sample` geography with consistent padded cluster IDs.**
  All 3,623 sample households and 19,285 roster records join the 364 clusters.
  The export's constant-one `_weight_` is not presented as a survey weight;
  the other Peru sample waves remain unwired. **Panama 1997** code 77
  (`valor`) no longer supplies 7.70 as a physical quantity, and the raw
  expenditure total is preserved. [#700](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/issues/700),
  [#777](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/issues/777),
  [PR #996](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/pull/996).
- **GhanaLSS** food scripts use script-relative imports, its live 1998–99
  Price reducer is corrected, and curated C1-control punctuation in labels is
  repaired. Region spelling and unit-label repairs also cover Ethiopia, Mali,
  Malawi, Niger, Guinea-Bissau, Burkina Faso and Senegal. The explicit-metric
  parser now reads fractions such as `1/2 kg` as fractions.
  [PR #947](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/pull/947),
  [PR #952](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/pull/952),

## Portability, readers and diagnostics

Script-path builds can invoke the wave script when GNU make is unavailable;
Windows conda setup, UTF-8 file handling, installed-wheel identity checks and
manual CI dispatch support accompany this path. Wave builds run serially where
process forking is unavailable, including native Windows. Linux cold tests produced
matching Uganda shocks/assets results with and without make. The installed
native Windows cold-build/comparison job passed at `74c9398ecf3c` on October 14
(Pacific time). That result covers the reviewed portability head; the final
release record must identify its inclusion in the integrated source.
[Windows validation](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/actions/runs/38683117467/job/116251866840).
Importing the package no longer eagerly imports GitPython.
[#964](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/issues/964),
[#968](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/issues/968).

The reader dispatches SAS XPORT by magic bytes, including the verified Peru
SSP files, and rejects contradictory or undecodable claimed Stata files before
a permissive text fallback can turn binary bytes into a misleading empty
frame. Peru 1990 table configuration remains work to do. Reader fallbacks and
ID crosswalk reads no longer use cwd-rooted `dvc.api.open`; JSON cache reads no
longer invoke make. [#699](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/issues/699),
[#712](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/issues/712),
[#810](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/issues/810),
[#970](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/issues/970).

- `labels=` normalizes label-column names, reports unmatched labels through
  warnings and `labels_unmatched`, supports strict refusal, and rejects numeric
  targets. Ambiguous one-to-many targets, per-country aggregate curation and
  carrying diagnostics through `Feature` remain open in
  [#787](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/issues/787).
- Monetary scale diagnostics compare neighboring waves and warn at the
  approved fivefold threshold without changing the coverage tier. Currency
  boundaries and support checks remain explicit; warnings are investigative
  signals, not proof of corruption. Persistent shifts, long gaps and other
  blind spots remain in [#830](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/issues/830).
- Country-name typos raise early with suggestions. Existing but unregistered
  directories remain the unresolved half of
  [#915](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/issues/915). Categorical mapping
  strips string values without nulling mixed-column numbers, and illustrative
  housing tables no longer enter the live mappings.
- Reducer conflict reports survive cold/warm cache reads, including explicit
  rounds inside a multi-round folder. Panel diagnostics check roster-cache
  wave coverage before treating missing targets as a data defect.
- `cache clear` reports in-tree source files and preserves tracked configuration
  inputs. The sidecar-less in-tree read policy remains open in
  [#831](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/issues/831).
- Canonical food deduplication and Panama scripts use vectorized
  `sum(min_count=1)`. Summed floats can differ by a few ULP; the regression
  bound is four. The remaining reducer sweep is
  [#905](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/issues/905).

## Upgrade and validation notes

**Expect a cold rebuild of both harmonized parquet tiers across the corpus.**
The build and reader changes share a corpus-wide fingerprint scope. L1 source
blobs remain reusable. A successful warm call is not validation of the new
build. The integrated cold rebuild covered all 549 declared requests across
38 countries: 530 succeeded, with two unavailable Armenian source requests,
eleven unavailable Nepal source requests and six declared but unwired Peru
requests reported as errors. No other build errors occurred. Two Uganda
country caches, `earnings` and `fct`, lose their metadata when sibling scripts
rewrite them; ordinary reads correctly treat them as stale under #809.
The original outputs remain preserved, and follow-up audits use independent
copies. [Corpus evidence](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/blob/development/slurm_logs/release_landing_2026-10-14/corpus/REPORT.md).

The minimum `ligonlibrary` version is now **0.2.3**. Python remains >=3.11,
pandas remains >=3.0, and the existing CFEDemands >=0.10.0 requirement is
unchanged. `poetry.lock` is now tracked; the prior environment was archived
before the fresh resolve. Dependency versions are not part of the data-cache
fingerprint, so the environment change also needs cold validation. Development
installation and image-building guards use `--no-root` and explicit checkout
identity. Multi-node venv ownership and its documentation remain separate
unfinished work.

CI avoids duplicate branch/PR runs, supports merge queues and manual credentialed
validation, and skips known unavailable data at collection rather than waiting
for network failure. Unexpected runtime credential skips warn. Structural tests
enumerate declared tables and make absent cache coverage visible; they do not
turn an unbuilt table into evidence of correctness. The requested sequential
cold selection for [#941](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/issues/941)
passed 451 tests with no skips at `ed1414fb9`, including all EHCVM delivery
assertions. Full credentialed CI remains unresolved: the controlled run stopped
on a GhanaLSS build timeout before a suite summary. The corrected candidate
is being checked after reproducing a collect-only test that leaked cache-bypass
state ([#1010](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/issues/1010)). The historical
#941 failure's cause is not identified, so it stays open.

The full local credentialed suite at `ec1a59767` passed 4,951 tests, with
2,725 skips, four expected failures and one existing non-strict unexpected
pass. None of the skips were credential skips; 2,580 were absent-cache cases.
Their follow-up against the cold-built corpus, with ten additional helper and
identity checks, produced 2,416 passes, 168 classified skips, five existing
tenure-label xfails and the one held nutrition mismatch. Fifteen tests
require the unavailable independent Uganda replication project. The nutrition
fixture comparison skipped in that full run, so its known mismatch remains
open. Some legacy test preconditions require stored derived parquets that the
current API does not write; these and obsolete index/dtype assertions remain
coverage debt in [#1006](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/issues/1006).
[Full-suite evidence](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/blob/development/slurm_logs/release_landing_2026-10-14/full-suite.org),
[cache-presence follow-up](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/blob/development/slurm_logs/release_landing_2026-10-14/cache-coverage.org).

Two helper tests now isolate and restore the cached data-root setting even on
exceptional teardown. Credentialed CI pins build and numerical-library thread
counts and uses a 600-second per-test timeout while retaining sequential test
execution and the same test selection. Neither change establishes the cause
of the historical #941 failure. [PR #1004](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/pull/1004),
[PR #1005](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/pull/1005).

The complete Feature sweep covered 39 features, all 549 declared country/table
pairs, 38 baseline assemblies and 17 configured variants. It retained 19 known
source/wiring errors and five existing tenure-vocabulary failures in Albania,
Cambodia, Kosovo, Tajikistan and Timor-Leste. Its diagnostic warnings remain
visible; completion is not a blanket scientific approval of every warning.
Older saved audits are partial and lack matched source/cache provenance. Their
comparison and the reviewed source/value changes revealed no unexplained
release-specific warning category, but do not prove every diagnostic historical.
[Comparison limits](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/blob/development/slurm_logs/release_landing_2026-10-14/feature-prior-comparison/REPORT.md).
[Feature audit evidence](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/blob/development/slurm_logs/release_landing_2026-10-14/feature-audit/REPORT.md).

Region-market food variants still warn and exclude GhanaSPS and Panama: both
have cluster IDs in `sample()`, but neither exposes `sample.Region` or declares
the `cluster_features` geography fallback. The relevant code and declarations
are unchanged since v0.14.0. GhanaSPS remains covered by open
[#140](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/issues/140); Panama is tracked in
[#1009](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/issues/1009).

**GhanaLSS still loses null-key rows during Feature assembly.** On the rebuilt
corpus, `Feature('food_acquired')(['GhanaLSS'])` deletes 13,472 rows with a
missing cluster or unit key; the country-native output retains them. This is
separate from the legitimate aggregation of visits. Independent replay
reproduced the dropped rows and conserved the retained additive values.
The mechanism is unchanged since v0.14.0; exact counts on rebuilt historical
inputs were not measured. The earlier reporting issues did not fix this
country residual. The measured loss is tracked in
[#1008](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/issues/1008).
[Independent review](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/blob/development/slurm_logs/release_landing_2026-10-14/ghana-feature-review/REVIEW.md).

**Uganda nutrition remains a release gate.** The historical fixture is
23,801 x 15 with hash `fc85fc2e…`; current development before #967 is the same
shape with hash `1131b358…`, and this candidate is 23,777 x 15 with hash
`12d25f6e…`. Historical replay exactly reproduces the first two hashes and
attributes their entire difference to four #850 commits already shipped before
v0.14.0. Their 18.7208% Energy reduction is historical baseline reconciliation,
not a change introduced by v0.15.0.

The pending #967 increment has independent raw-row and nutrient arithmetic
accounting: inferred quantity falls by 849.299850578 kg, 24 household-wave rows
lose their last convertible quantity, and 60 retained nutrient vectors change.
Arithmetic replay does not adjudicate the inherited litre-as-kg and nominal
container assumptions. It also exposed an older food-composition source-note ambiguity:
sweet-potato row 4003 supplies vitamin A at 225 mcg/100 g, while its source note
says vitamin A and carotenoids were assumed zero. An adjacent, more specific
note records beta-carotene derived from three yellow-flesh varieties in Kidmose
et al. (2007), supporting a nonzero value. The follow-up is to verify that cited
derivation and reconcile the generic note; it is not evidence that the density
should become zero. No density or numeric source value was changed. The
maintainer's choice between accepting these documented
limitations and holding for source review is pending. The nutrition-only
manifest patch remains unapplied under the explicit country hold. See the
[historical accounting](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/blob/development/slurm_logs/release_landing_2026-10-14/uganda-nutrition-history.org)
and [PR #997](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/pull/997).

The historical [#837](https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/issues/837) early
cluster-reduction proposal was reviewed and rejected. Current development
already retains all 333 Ethiopia W1 GPS fixes; the proposal would instead blank
364 Ethiopia GPS cells and 119 Tanzania attributes and bypass the existing
household-grain audit. Source behavior stays unchanged. The country notes now
correct the false inference that Ethiopia W1's missing-versus-recorded GPS
conflicts were administrative disagreements. Remaining source/scientific
questions stay open under #837.
