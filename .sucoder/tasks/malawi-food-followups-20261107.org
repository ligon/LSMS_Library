* Item 50: inspect, do not silently choose food or service
The maintainer proposed interpreting =Grain Mill (Gramil)=, code 119, literally
as a milling service and requested comparison with other corpus treatments.
The previous recommendation to map it to Maize is not an approved decision.

Existing precedents:
- Togo =nonfood_items= code 217: =Grain Milling Fees=, used by its section-9
  nonfood expenditure script. Cote d'Ivoire's questionnaire-derived nonfood list
  separately names =Frais de mouture des cereales= at 217.
- Malawi community price codes 3/4 explicitly price grinding a 50 kg bag of
  maize/rice, distinct from maize flour. Tanzania's community price mapping
  likewise keeps Maize Grinding as a service with no consumed-food counterpart.

New contrary evidence requiring reconciliation:
- The Malawi 2024–25 questionnaire (PDF p.49) prints =Grain Mill (Gramil)= at
  119 among maize flours, with food-consumption quantity/unit and source columns.
- The NSO's earlier Malawi dictionary explicitly names community-price item 2
  =Maize flour (Gramil, Rab, or locally milled)=, separately from item 3's charge
  to grind maize and item 4's charge to grind rice:
  https://microdata.worldbank.org/index.php/catalog/2248/variable/F187/V6616?name=com_ck00a
  This is evidence that Gramil can denote maize flour, not proof by itself of
  the current code's meaning. A harmonized =Meal= label is not a primary source.
- UNIDO's 1988 Malawi maize-mill feasibility report lists Gramil flour products
  (historical corroboration, not a 2024 code crosswalk):
  https://www.unido.org/publications/ot/9647091/pdf

Verify the current instrument, raw label and response/quantity/source patterns;
inspect separate household milling-charge questions if held. Preserve service
costs under their actual semantics and do not infer edible kg for a service.
Return a concise source-backed recommendation and any remaining ambiguity before
changing the current classification or guessing a borrowed factor for item 119.
