I briefly argued the GSS/Rwanda waves were exposed because our cache is gated on
a *WB* key while their terms name a different authority.  *EL corrected this and
he is right*: GSS runs the same NADA software as the WB, with the same
self-service API-key generation -- measured, *112 of the catalog URLs are
NADA-style*, WB and GSS and Rwanda among them; only the 13 =doi.org= (Dataverse)
ones differ.  So the population that can obtain GSS data is the same population
that can obtain WB data, and the six GhanaLSS waves are symmetric with the 63 WB
boilerplate licences.  Accept both or reject both; we accept both.

*Design consequence*: the permission record's key field is the ACCESS MECHANISM,
not the authority --
- =self-service= (NADA registration, or a public licence like CC0) -> discharged
  by the existing WB-API-key gate;
- =bespoke-agreement= (Novissi; un-displaced GPS) -> NOT discharged; this is the
  only category needing (A)'s default-deny.
=PROVENANCE_SOURCE= still cannot be borrowed for this: it records where we GOT a
wave, not what we may DO with it (6 GhanaLSS waves are =external= yet fine).

EthiopiaRHS x8 have no clear term.  Only GhanaSPS 2013-14 is substantively
