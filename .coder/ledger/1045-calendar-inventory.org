Gregorian from values):

  the citation is given.

  civil calendar this is written =unknown (no cite; non-ET)=: it is *very
  probably* Gregorian, but nothing in the repo says so, and Nepal (Bikram

Findings that change the Phase-2/3 plan (each detailed in its section):
1. *Every string-typed served date traces to one of two mechanisms*: a
   formatter returning =datetime.date= (Mali =mali.py:85=, Nigeria 2018-19
   =interview_date.py:12,23=, Timor-Leste =mapping.py:25= / =:21=) or a raw
   ISO string kept with no formatter (Ethiopia W4/W5, Tanzania 2019-20/2020-21
   =hh_a18=).  One string wave is enough to turn the whole L2-country column
   into =large_string=.  The #798 loss counts are *exactly* the minority string
   form in each country (Mali 12,745 ='YYYY-MM-DD'=; Tanzania 5,893
   ='YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS'=).
2. *Mali 2018-19/2021-22*: a =household_roster= myvar named =interview_date=
   forced a pass-through formatter of the same name, which silently disables
   the =interview_date= table's df_edit hook in those waves (1b).  Mali also
   serves only visit-1 start of the six EHCVM visit timestamps (source sweep)
   and drops EACI passage 2 at the (t, v, i) collapse.
3. *=crop_production= "months" are three different kinds*: month-of-year
   integers (Ethiopia -- converted from the Ethiopian calendar by a lossy fixed
   relabel, Malawi, Mali, Tanzania, Uganda, Niger 2014-15), *year-month strings*
   (Nigeria ='YYYY-MM'=), and *sets of months* (GhanaSPS ='08 11'=, 6,356 cells).
   Six of the seven EHCVM =harvest_month= columns are 100% null (not in the
   instrument); Niger's is harvest *start* month where others use end month.
4. *Uganda =shocks.Year= 2009-10..2011-12 is imputed*, not reported
   (=shock_yr()=); =shocks.Onset= is a month count computed from two
   day-padded year-months (31 negatives); Albania =shocks.Years= is a
   comma-joined *set* of years.
5. *DOB*: in Uganda (7 waves) and Nigeria (5) the day/month of birth are read
   but cannot affect =Age= (no interview date reaches =age_handler=);
   =age_handler= imputes a missing day as *15*, not 1; Ethiopia 2013-14 is the
   only conversion through =lsms_library.calendars=; Ethiopia 2015-16's DOB is
   unwired and its calendar is documented both ways.
6. *Calendar is uncited almost everywhere* (=unknown=): no questionnaire was
   read in this pass.  Ethiopia W4/W5 interview dates stay open (charter §5).
