## §3 Definitions & conventions in force

- Derived values: `CLAUDE.md` "Derived Values" (fit rule: "a value served under a name implying a different window -- the function is the identity and the registry is the point"); stamp after any collapse; `inputs` must join to the served rows; drift test both ways (`tests/test_derivations.py`).
- Categorical mappings: `CLAUDE.md` "Categorical mappings resolve wave -> country -> global" (whole-table override at a wave level; ~40 modules read `categorical_mapping.org` BY PATH, bypassing the cascade -- fact 2).  Ethiopia's crop decode is one of them.
- No silent aggregation / vectorised reducers: `CLAUDE.md` "Grain Collapse" (GH #871: per-group callables deepcopy `attrs`).
- A missing answer is not "No": #1250's rule (zero only where the flag positively says No).
- Ethiopia idiosyncrasies (`Ethiopia/_/CONTENTS.org`): `household_id` (W1, W4, W5) vs `household_id2` (W2, W3) as `i`; `ea_id` -> `ea_id2` re-identification at W1 -> W2 (`v` wave-scoped, GH #942); the SALES GRAIN CAVEAT (§11/§12 are holder-crop grain, attached only to a crop's single §9 plot); #852/#931 kg factors decode `j` through the SAME `_eth_crop_label_map` / `_clean_unit_label` as `crop_production`, so a `j` change must reach `_crop_cf_table` too.

## §4 Invariants & assumptions

- §11 "No" (W1-W3 Q1 / W4 Q7 = 2) skips quantity and value: blank by design.
- W1/W2 §11 Q3 is KG by the instrument; W3-W5 record a coded sale unit.
- No §11 line in any wave carries a positive quantity with a blank unit (measured).
- `_eth_crop_label_map` is consumed by `crop_production_for_wave`, `plot_inputs_for_wave` (seed `j`), `_crop_cf_table` (shipped kg factors) and `inputs_sect12_sale`; all four must decode a wave the same way or joins break.
- W4/W5 PP §4 `s4q15` is the survey's own per-crop-row permanent/tree flag (W4 "IS THIS CROP A FRUIT TREE CROP?", W5 "IS THIS CROP A Permanent/TREE CROP?").
