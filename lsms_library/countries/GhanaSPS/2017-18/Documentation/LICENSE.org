** The 100 pre-existing files -- terms NOT RECORDED

The files this repository already held for wave 3 did *not* come from the
Dataverse distribution and their licence is *unknown*.  =SOURCE.org= records
=PROVENANCE_SOURCE: external= with no source URL.  No licence document for
wave 3 exists in this repository other than this file.

*This is not a statement that they are unrestricted.*  It is a statement that
nobody recorded the terms.  Two facts make that worth taking seriously:

1. The wave-1 =LICENSE.org= (the only other GhanaSPS licence in the repo)
   carries the clause: "Users who download the data may not pass the data to
   third parties."  If wave 3 came through a comparable channel, a similar
   restriction may apply.
2. *30 of those files carry variables the published release removed.*
   Measured 2026-08-19 against Dataverse v1.2: row counts are identical in
   every case, but our copies carry extra columns and never fewer.  The
   stripped variables are consistently disclosure-sensitive -- free-text
   ="...other"= responses, sub-district geography (=d_code=, =district=,
   =comm_code=, =*_community=, =plotlocation_comm=, =schoolcommunity=) and
   consent/admin flags (=consented=, =main_consent=, =audio_consent=,
   =checkpoint1..3=).  In other words our copies are *pre-anonymization*
   relative to what the producers chose to publish.

Anyone redistributing this wave, or deriving a public product from it, should
resolve the terms of the 100 pre-existing files first.  See
=slurm_logs/ghana_audit/FINDINGS_ghanasps_provenance.org= for the full
file-by-file evidence.
