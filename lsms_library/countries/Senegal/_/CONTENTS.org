* Household Presence / MonthsSpent

The EHCVM questionnaire (used in the 2018-19 and 2021-22 waves) does
NOT ask "How many months did [NAME] live here in the past 12 months?"
(the continuous MonthsSpent/MonthsAway variable available in Uganda,
Ethiopia, Tanzania, Malawi, and many other LSMS countries).  Instead,
EHCVM uses two binary eligibility filters:

- =s01q12= :: "Has [NAME] lived continuously in this household for at
  least 6 months?"
- =s01q13= :: "Does [NAME] intend to stay at least 6 months?"

These determine membership eligibility but do not give a continuous
duration measure.  Downstream analysts who need a presence filter for
=household_characteristics= should use these binary variables directly
rather than expecting a =MonthsSpent= column in =household_roster()=.

Senegal has no pre-EHCVM waves in the library, so no continuous
months-of-presence variable is available for any wave.

* household_roster 2018-19: cp1252 roster labels read as Latin-1 (GH #801)

=s01_me_sen2018.dta= is Stata format 115 with no declared encoding, and its
value-label bytes are Windows-1252: code 7 of =s01q02= is stored as
=Fr\xe8re, s\x9cur= (0x9C is the oe ligature in cp1252).  =get_dataframe=
reads the file through pandas =StataReader=, which decodes every pre-118
file as Latin-1, so 0x9C came back as the C1 control U+009C and the wave
served =Frere, S<U+009C>Ur= (2,749 rows -- the =Ur= because the wave's =Relationship= formatter =.title()=-cases the label and =str.title= treats the control byte as a word break).  pyreadstat's default, or
=encoding='cp1252'=, reads the same file correctly -- the defect is the
reader's Latin-1 fallback, not the file (bytes and decoder matrix in
=slurm_logs/gh797/fix_801/REPORT.org=, measured 2026-09-07).

Fix (2026-09-07): =2018-19/_/mapping.py::_decode_cp1252= re-encodes the label as Latin-1 and decodes it
as cp1252, and =_fix_relationship= re-title-cases the result; the =household_roster= hook applies it to =Relationship=
only.  Served label is now =Frere, Soeur= with the ligature restored,
the label the 2021-22 wave and Niger 2018-19 serve; kinship tuple [0, 1, consanguineal] unchanged; 2,749 rows changed,
nothing else.  The C1-control spellings were deleted from
=lsms_library/categorical_mapping/kinship.yml= at the same time, so that
file no longer carries the artefact and a regression would surface as an
=Unknown relationship labels= warning instead of resolving silently.

Same defect and same fix in Niger 2018-19, Benin 2018-19 and Togo 2018 (all format-115 cp1252 EHCVM-1
roster files).  See GH #801 and the #797 vocabulary sweep
(=slurm_logs/gh797/vocab_sweep/REPORT.org= section 2.4).

* Sampling universe, exclusions and sub-samples

Evidence for what each Senegal wave *claims* to cover, kept deliberately
separate from what the data look like.  Every statement below is quoted
verbatim from a named document at a named location; anything not quoted is
labelled as editorial.  Nothing here is inferred from the =.dta= files.

Both Senegal waves are EHCVM (=Enquête Harmonisée sur les Conditions de Vie
des Ménages=), the WAEMU/UEMOA harmonized series run by ANSD with the World
Bank and the WAEMU Commission.  See also =Household Presence / MonthsSpent=
above, which documents the EHCVM 6-month *membership* rule (=s01q12= /
=s01q13=) -- that rule decides who counts as a member of a sampled household
and is a different question from the survey's declared universe.

*** Sub-sample structure: the two =vagues= are halves of every EA

This is the sub-sample fact a pooling analyst needs.  The wave is not a
single fieldwork pass: each EA was *split in half*, and the halves were
interviewed in two seasons.  DDI PDF page 4, =SAMPLING PROCEDURE=, verbatim
(identical text in the local BID, =Sample=, and in WB catalog 4297):

#+begin_quote
The total survey sample size is 7156 households - 3941 from urban areas and
3215 from rural areas. After that, the survey design randomly divided each
enumeration area into two equal groups. The survey team interrogated the
first group in wave 1 and the other in wave 2. Finally, for various reasons,
including availability and quality monitoring, the final sample size
comprises slightly more households (twenty) in round 2 than in round 1. In
wave one, the survey teams interviewed 3568 households (1961 in urban areas
and 1607 in rural areas. In wave two, the teams interviewed 3588 households
(1980 in urban areas and 1608 in rural areas).
#+end_quote

(Quoted as printed, including the unclosed parenthesis after "1607 in rural
areas".)

Same PDF, page 2, =ABSTRACT=, verbatim: "The surveys took place in two waves
with each wave covering half of the sample. The first wave was fielded
between September 2018 and December 2018, while the second wave occurred
between April 2019 and July 2019. The two-wave approach was chosen to
account for seasonality of consumption."

Consequences for anyone pooling this wave:

- The =vague= split is *seasonal*, by design.  Any within-wave comparison
  across =vague= is confounded with season, deliberately so.
- Because each EA is split rather than each EA being assigned wholly to one
  =vague=, the design intent differs from the library's operating
  assumption for EHCVM countries.  =CLAUDE.md= records the convention that
  "each =grappe= is visited in exactly one =vague=", hence =v: grappe= and
  =i: [grappe, menage]= rather than composites including =vague=.  The
  documentation above says the *households* within an EA were split between
  =vagues=, i.e. an EA appears in both.  Flagged, not adjudicated -- this is
  a discrepancy between a documented design statement and a library
  configuration convention, and resolving it needs someone to look at the
  Senegal identifiers directly.

* WARNING: the =u= crosswalk is keyed on MOJIBAKE, and that is load-bearing

=_/categorical_mapping.org= table =#+NAME: u= maps unit labels to preferred
labels.  Its *Original Label* column does not hold the unit names as a human
would write them -- it holds them *double-encoded*:

: | unitÃ©                    | Unité   |
: | BoÃ®te                    | Boîte   |
: | cuillÃ¨re Ã &nbsp; soupe    | Cuillère à soupe |

On disk those keys are the UTF-8 encoding of =Ã©= (U+00C3 U+00A9), i.e. bytes
=c3 83 c2 a9=, *not* of =é= (U+00E9).  This is deliberate and it is *correct*,
because of how the source file is read:

- =Data/s07b_me_sen2018.dta= is Stata *format 115*.  ~pandas.io.stata.StataReader~
  chooses its string encoding purely from the format version
  (~_set_encoding~: =< 118 -> latin-1=, =>= 118 -> utf-8=).  Format 115 is
  therefore decoded as *latin-1, silently -- no ~UnicodeWarning~ is raised*.
- The file's value labels are actually UTF-8 bytes.  Decoding them as latin-1
  yields =unitÃ©= for =unité=.  That mojibake is what reaches the =u= index.
- The =u= crosswalk keys on exactly that string and maps it to the clean
  =Unité=.  *The crosswalk is the repair.*

*Consequence: "fixing" the encoding would BREAK this table.*  Measured on
2018-19 (=s07bq03b=, 214,528 rows): 41,000 rows carry a mojibake unit label,
and every one of them matches a =u= crosswalk key.  Re-decode them correctly
(e.g. by passing =encoding== through to the reader) and all 41,000 stop
matching -- =unité= is *not* a key in the table, =unitÃ©= is.  The API
currently returns 0 mojibake =u= values precisely because the two wrongs
cancel.

*The same pattern keys =j=, and it is six times larger.*  =harmonize_food='s
*Original Label* column carries *51 mojibake rows*, and =data_info.yml= maps
=j= through it (=mappings: ['harmonize_food','Original Label','Preferred Label']=).
Measured on 2018-19: *63,989 of 214,528 rows* key on a mojibake
=harmonize_food= entry, against 41,000 for =u=.  Corpus-wide this is an EHCVM
*cohort* property -- Benin, Burkina Faso, Guinea-Bissau, Niger, Senegal and
Togo all do it, ~278,777 rows across the six waves that are cached (GH #794).

*The cohort figure is a LOWER BOUND.*  The census that produced it keyed on
the =Ã=/=Â= (double-encoding) signature only, so it misses the cp1252-C1
subclass entirely -- Burkina Faso's 2014 wave contributes *0* to the 278,777
under that predicate while actually carrying 34,133 crosswalk-matched
non-ASCII rows.  Widening the predicate to "any non-ASCII" raises every
country's count.


*There is already a better answer in-repo, and Senegal does not use it.*
CotedIvoire, Guinea-Bissau and Niger each carry a =_decode_mojibake= helper in
=<wave>/_/mapping.py= that repairs the *data* instead of the crosswalk:

: return s.encode('latin-1').decode('utf-8')

which is exactly the correct reversal of pandas' latin-1 decode.  Those three
countries' =harmonize_food= key columns are consequently *clean*.  Niger even
carries a =_LOST_PREFIX_REPLACEMENTS= dict for the unrecoverable case --
including =bÅ__uf -> bœuf=, which is *precisely* the damage Senegal's own
=produitID= label 23 carries (=Viande de b= U+00C5 U+005F U+005F =uf=; the
UTF-8 continuation byte was replaced with literal underscores upstream of the
library and no decode recovers it).  Senegal has the same corruption and no
such dict.

*Updated at the merge with =development= (2026-09-07; GH #801 / #805).*
Since GH #801 Senegal *does* carry a decode hook -- but not for this table,
and not for this defect.  =2018-19/_/mapping.py::_decode_cp1252= (with
=_fix_relationship=) repairs the *roster* file =s01_me_sen2018.dta=, whose
value labels are Windows-1252 bytes read as Latin-1 (sweep record: =nort 60,
c1 3=; =Frere, s<U+009C>ur=), and the =household_roster= hook applies it to
=Relationship= only -- see the =household_roster 2018-19= section near the
top of this file.  That is a *different* encoding class from this table's:
=s07b_me_sen2018.dta= holds UTF-8 bytes read as Latin-1 (sweep record:
=double 67, nort 2=, no =c1=), which =_decode_cp1252= cannot reverse -- the
double-encoded pair U+00C3 U+00A9 carries no codepoint in U+0080-U+009F, so
that helper returns it unchanged.  One wave, two files, two encodings.  The
sentence above ("Senegal does not use it") therefore still holds for
=food_acquired=: no decode hook touches =j= or =u=, and the crosswalk is
still the repair.

*GH #805 does not unwind this section either.*  The reader-level fix it
proposes (cp1252-first for format < 118) changes only strings carrying a
codepoint in U+0080-U+009F, because Latin-1 and cp1252 agree on every other
byte.  The =s07b= value labels carry none (the sweep signature above; =str=
columns were not probed, per the sweep README), and the crosswalk keys carry
none (measured on this tree: 0 of 315 =harmonize_food= keys, 0 of 120 =u=

* 2026-09-12 -- =food_acquired=: one row, two windows (GH #876)

=food_acquired.Expenditure= used to be wired straight to =s07bq08=, whose
Stata label is "7B.08. Valeur (en FCFA) du [PRODUIT] achete la derniere
fois" -- the value of *one* purchase, which =s07bq06= places up to 30 days
back -- sitting on the same row as =Quantity=, a *7-day* consumption figure.
Section 7B is headed "Consommation alimentaire des 7 derniers jours et achat
des 30 derniers jours"; the row carried both clocks and nothing in the frame
said so.  =s07bq05=, the quantity received as a GIFT, was never read at all.

The served =Expenditure= is now DERIVED, on the survey's own accounting
identity (construction "C", @ligon 2026-09-12):

  : Quantity     = (s07bq03a - s07bq04 - s07bq05).clip(0)
  : Expenditure  = Quantity * (s07bq08 / s07bq07a)

-- the 7-day consumption net of own production AND of gifts, times the unit
value of the last purchase -- so =Expenditure / Quantity= is exactly that
unit value and the two numbers on the row refer to the same thing.  =NA=,
never a guess, where =s07bq07a= is not positive or where the purchase was
made in a different unit (or a different SIZE of the same unit) from the
consumption: no EHCVM wave ships a unit-conversion table, so there is no
exact conversion to apply.

And the gift quantity is now SERVED, on its own =s = 'inkind'= rows in the
consumption unit =s07bq03b=, with =Expenditure= *NA*.  EHCVM asks no value
for a gift; UEMOA's =depan(Don)= is that quantity priced at the same
last-purchase unit value -- verified below, and therefore an imputation, not
an answer -- so the library checks it and does not serve it.  Own production
is unchanged: =s = 'produced'= from =s07bq04=, =Expenditure= NA, which was
already the rule.

One function for all eight EHCVM countries (=lsms_library/ehcvm.py=,
=derive_ehcvm_purchase_value=), registered once per country in
=_/derivations.yml= and stamped on every purchased row in the =Derivation=
column:

  : Senegal::food_acquired::7day-purchase-at-last-purchase-unit-value

Raw answers come back by their original names from
=Country('Senegal').derivation_inputs(key, wave=...)=.
