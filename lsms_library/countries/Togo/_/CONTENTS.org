* Overview

Togo EHCVM (Enquete Harmonisee sur les Conditions de Vie des
Menages), the harmonized West African household-conditions survey
template.  Two waves: =2018= and =2021-22= (EHCVM-2, wired 2026-11-04, see "2021-22
wired" below, and its 2026-11-12 sequel at the end of this file).

* Sampling Design

Standard EHCVM convention: =grappe= is the cluster identifier (=v=);
each grappe is visited in exactly one =vague=, so household
identifier is =(grappe, menage)= without a vague level.  See
CLAUDE.md "EHCVM countries" for the cross-country pattern.

* Sampling universe, exclusions and sub-samples

Evidence for what the Togo wave *claims* to represent, kept deliberately
separate from what the data look like.  Nothing here is inferred from any
=.dta=; every claim is a quotation with a citation.  Companion to
"Sampling Design" above (which records the =grappe=/=vague= identifier
convention) and to "Household Presence / MonthsSpent" below (which
records the 6-month residency filters =s01q12= / =s01q13= that operationalise
"de jure" membership at the person level).

Design representation is out of scope here --- GH #603 is deciding how the
library should *encode* this.  This section is evidence only.

*** WARNING: this universe sentence is NADA boilerplate, not a Togolese claim

The sentence quoted above is *World Bank NADA template text*.  It appears
verbatim in 15 waves across the corpus --- all eight WAEMU/EHCVM countries
(Benin, Burkina Faso x2, Cote d'Ivoire, Guinea-Bissau, Mali, Niger, Senegal,
Togo) and also Ethiopia 2018-19 / 2021-22 and Nigeria 2015-16 / 2018-19 /
2023-24, which are not EHCVM at all.  Its presence attests *which metadata
template was used*; it does **not** independently attest that INSEED made
that claim about this survey.  Do not treat it as a statistical office's
statement about Togo.

Corroborating this reading: the Togo Basic Information Document (INSEED /
World Bank, June 2021; catalog download id 52590) contains **no** universe
statement and **no** exclusion clause at all --- greps for =universe=,
=exclud=, =prison=, =barrack=, =nomad=, =homeless=, =institution= over the
full 20-page text return only an unrelated hit ("access to financial
institutions", Section 6).  The exclusion of collective/institutional
dwellings is asserted *only* by the catalog template.

*** Geographic coverage and the Lome Commune sentence (quoted, NOT corrected)

Catalog 4298, Sampling > Sampling Procedure, opening sentence --- identical
text in the Basic Information Document, section "Sample", *PDF page 10*,
first sentence (both verified 2026-07-21):

#+begin_quote
The Togo EHCVM 2018/19 sample covers all regions with urban and rural areas
surveyed in all regions except Lome Commune where all respondents are from
rural areas.
#+end_quote

*Read that twice.*  It is quoted exactly as printed in both sources.  It is
internally implausible --- Lome Commune is the wholly urban capital district
--- and reads like an urban/rural transposition in the source document
(compare Guinea-Bissau's structurally identical sentence, which says the
autonomous sector of Bissau is the one where "all respondents are from
*urban* areas").  It is reproduced here uncorrected on purpose.  **Do not
silently "fix" it**, and do not treat the corrected reading as documented:
what is documented is the sentence above.

Practical import either way: *Lome Commune is a single-milieu stratum*.
Whichever milieu the source meant, one of urban/rural is absent from Lome
Commune by design, so a rural/urban contrast computed within Lome Commune is
not estimable, and pooled urban/rural comparisons carry a design asymmetry
there.

*** Oversamples and sub-populations: NONE FOUND

No booster, no ethnic or programme sub-sample, no refresh/extended split, no
module subsample, and no cross-section-plus-panel combination is documented
for this wave.  The design as stated is a plain stratified two-stage draw.

"Stratified" is measurable, and the weights confirm it (GH #812, 2026-10-14):
=Country('Togo').sample(waves=['2018'])= -- 6,171 households, 540 clusters,
weight constant within cluster -- puts *66.5% of the variance of log
cluster weight between the 6 =strata= (regions)*, with sample share over
weighted population share from 0.54x (Lome commune, 1,034 households / 98
clusters) through Plateaux 0.85, Maritime 0.89, Centrale 1.45, Kara 1.55 to
1.90x (Savanes, 1,144 / 96).  All six regions get 72-98 clusters each
regardless of size.  Treat the design as region-stratified for inference;
no source consulted states the allocation rule.  (=sample.strata= is wired
from the cover-page region code, =2018/_/data_info.yml:16=.)  The twin
measurements for Guinea-Bissau (76.8%), Benin (31.4%) and CotedIvoire
(36.5%) are in their =CONTENTS.org=.

The one structure that could be mistaken for a sub-sample is the *two-wave
seasonal split*, which is a fielding split of one sample, not two
populations.  BID p.4, "Survey Characteristics" (verified verbatim):

#+begin_quote
The surveys took place in two waves with each wave covering half of the
sample. The first wave was fielded between September 2018 and December 2018,
while the second wave occurred between April 2019 and June 2019. The
two-wave approach was chosen to account for seasonality of consumption.
#+end_quote

The library folds both fielding waves into the single wave directory =2018=
(=t = "2018"=), so the seasonal half-samples are pooled by default.  Anyone
computing consumption levels should know they are averaging a
Sept--Dec 2018 half and an Apr--Jun 2019 half.

Sample-design magnitudes --- *context, not universe* (catalog 4298 Sampling
Procedure; identical in BID p.10): two-stage design; 540 EAs at stage 1;
12 households per EA at stage 2; total 6,171 households (2,270 urban,
3,901 rural); each EA split into two equal halves, one per fielding wave;
wave 1 = 2,931 households (1,034 urban, 1,897 rural), wave 2 = 3,240
households (1,236 urban, 2,004 rural).

*** Universe vs. what the weights represent

*Unresolvable from the record.*  The DDI entry for 4298 carries **no**
=weight=, no =sampling_deviation= and no =response_rate= field, and the BID

** The window is carried, NOT annualised

=Expenditure= is the FCFA figure the household reported, and =RecallWindow=
names its window verbatim, so the raw number and its period both stay
recoverable.  Annualising is a *transformation*, and REPORTED-values-only is
the rule this country already applies to =crop_production= and =plot_labor=.
One defensible scaling, spelled out so nobody has to guess:

: annual = Expenditure * 365.25 / {'7 days': 7, '30 days': 30,
:            '3 months': 91.3125, '6 months': 182.625,
:            '12 months': 365.25}[RecallWindow]

=RecallWindow= is a COLUMN, not an index level, because EHCVM gives each
sub-module a *disjoint* item-code block (201-217 / 301-322 / 401-418 /
501-512 / 601-653), so =j= already determines the window.  Making it a level
would bake a functional dependency into the key.  The wave script asserts the
dependency (=nunique() == 1= per =j=), so a future release that reused a code
across modules would fail the build rather than mislabel a window.

Sanity, measured: median expenditure rises monotonically with the window --
500 / 900 / 1,200 / 3,000 / 3,500 FCFA.  A 7-day median at 14.3% of the
12-month median is the shape a correct window mapping produces.

** Partie A (=s09a=) is EXCLUDED — and that has a cost, stated

=s09a= is *not* wired into =nonfood_expenditures=.  Three reasons, in order
of weight:

1. *Different grain.*  It is keyed by EVENT (13 fêtes / cérémonies: Fin du
   Ramadan, Tabaski, Noël, Pâques, Nouvel an, Mariage, Baptêmes,
   Communion/Confirmation, Funérailles, ...), not by product/service, with
   five FIXED amount columns per event rather than one amount per item.
2. *Two of those five columns are food* -- =s09aq03= alimentation and
   =s09aq04= boissons.  They do not belong in a table called
   /non-food/ expenditures at all.
3. *Replacement parity.*  The five retired CSVs were the five recall
   windows, i.e. s09b..s09f.  s09a was not in them either, so excluding it
   loses nothing that was previously delivered.

Folding it in would require either summing over the 13 events (destroying
the event dimension) or minting 39 compound =event × category= item labels
(polluting the =j= axis).  Neither is obviously right, and neither should be
decided silently.

*THE COST, because it is real and easy to miss.*  Sheets =S9e=/=S9f= carry a
standing instruction in the item-label column:

#+begin_quote
(NE PAS INCLURE LES PAGNES, VETEMENTS, CHAUSSURES ET BIJOUX ACHETES A
L'OCCASION DES FETES ET CEREMONIES COMME LA TABASKI, LA FIN DE RAMADAN,
NOEL, PAQUES, NOUVEL AN, MARIAGE, FUNERAILLES, BAPTEMES, ETC. QUI ONT ÉTÉ
INSCRITES DANS LES SECTIONS PRÉCÉDENTES)
#+end_quote

So ceremony clothing, footwear and jewellery were deliberately routed to
=s09aq05= and are therefore *absent from 9E/9F by design*.
=nonfood_expenditures= consequently *UNDERCOUNTS clothing, footwear and
jewellery*.  It is not a bug in the wiring; it is what excluding 9A means,
and anyone using these categories must know it.

*Follow-up (not done here).*  A separate =ceremony_expenditures= feature
keyed =(t, i, event)= with the five reported category columns is the honest
shape for =s09a=: it keeps the event, keeps food and non-food apart, and
does not distort the =j= axis.  =s09a= is a complete grid -- 6,171
households × 13 events = 80,223 rows, of which 13,921 have the gate
=s09aq02 == 1= and carry amounts.

** Grain, keys, and what the framework adds

Declared =(t, i, j)= in =data_scheme.yml=; DELIVERED =(i, t, v, j)=, because
=_join_v_from_sample= adds =v= at API time (540 grappes, no nulls).  Do not
declare =v= here -- =nonfood_expenditures= is not in
=lsms_library/data_info.yml='s =index_info=, so the join fires, and emitting
=v= from the wave script would duplicate it.

No =vague= level: each grappe is visited in exactly one vague, so
=(grappe, menage)= identifies a household uniquely (the standard EHCVM
convention recorded under "Sampling Design").  =i= is the composite id
=format_id(grappe) + '0' + format_id(menage, zeropadding=2)=, inlined
verbatim from =togo.i()= exactly as =livestock.py= does.

=(t, i, j)= is unique *in the source* -- the wave script asserts it -- so
nothing is reduced and =LSMS_GRAIN_STRICT=1= produces zero
=GrainCollapseWarning=.

** Idiosyncrasies worth knowing

- *The modules ship PRE-FILTERED to the gate.*  Every row of s09b..s09f as
  distributed has =s09?q02 == 1= ("Oui"); the "Non" rows are not published.
  So the absence of a =(household, item)= pair means /did not buy/, NOT
  /not asked/.  Do not read a missing pair as missing data.  (=s09a= is the
  opposite: a complete 6,171 × 13 grid with both gate values present.)
  The wave script keeps the gate filter anyway, so a future re-release that
  ships the "Non" rows cannot leak them in as zero-value purchases.
- *A typo in the shipped Stata labels.*  =s09aq02='s variable label reads
  "9A.02. 12 derniers *jours*, avez-vous effectué des dépenses pour les
  fêtes?" where the instrument says "Au cours des 12 derniers *mois*".  The
  instrument is right; the =.dta= label is wrong.  This is exactly why the
  window question was settled against the questionnaire and not against the
  variable labels.
- Relatedly, the =s09aq01= value label for event 02 says "Tabaski *2018*"
  where the questionnaire prints "Tabaski *2017*".  Recorded, not corrected.
- *The item labels come from the INSTRUMENT, not the =.dta=.*  Unlike
  =s07bq03b= (whose value labels were lost -- see "food_acquired units"
  above), the s09 code variables *do* carry Stata value labels.  But those
  are truncated at ~110 characters, so the questionnaire sheets are the
  source for =nonfood_items= in =categorical_mapping.org=.
- *Two declared items are never reported*: 627 "Vignette automobile/moto"
  (Vehicle Road Tax) and 649 "Taxes d'habitation ... taxes de voiries"
  (Property and Municipal Taxes).  120 of 122 items are delivered.  They are
  kept in the table because they are on the instrument; their absence is a
  fact about Togolese households, not a wiring defect.
- *12 of the 6,171 sample households report no section-9 purchase at all* in
  any of the five modules, so =nonfood_expenditures= covers 6,159 households
  (99.81%).  There are zero orphans in the other direction.
- *121 rows report a purchase with no amount* (gate = Oui, =s09?q03= null).
  These are kept: "bought it, amount not recorded" is a reported fact and is
  not the same as "did not buy".

** The =#+name:= trap (if you add or rename an org table)

=df_from_orgfile= (=lsms_library/local_tools.py:1636=) locates a table by
*exact string equality* against ~f'#+name: {name}'.lower()~ after
=.strip().lower()=.  Case is free -- this file mixes =#+name:= and =#+NAME:=
and both work -- but *the single space after the colon is mandatory*, and
nothing may follow the table name on that line.  A miss raises =KeyError=
(GH #461) rather than silently returning the file's *first* table, which is
the behaviour that once labelled every crop with a unit.  Note also that a
bare =get_categorical_mapping(tablename=...)= with no value column used to
return an *empty dict*, so every lookup silently yielded =pd.NA= (GH #372 /
#377 / #348); since GH #674 it raises =TypeError= instead.  Prefer
=code_label_map=.

* food_acquired units (Benin<->Togo codebook link)

Togo's =s07bq03b= unit variable stores bare numeric codes (100-662): its
=.dta= lost the Stata value labels, so =convert_categoricals=True= still
returns codes.  The labels survive in the *sibling EHCVM countries* whose
=.dta= kept them --- chiefly *Benin*, which uses the identical standardized
coding (=100=Kg=, =101=Litre=, =107=Boite=, =124=Gousse=, ...).

The decode therefore borrows the sibling labels: the shared codebook
=lsms_library/categorical_mapping/ehcvm_units.org= (=#+name:ehcvm_units=,
built by unioning =s07bq03b= value labels across all 8 EHCVM countries ---
see =slurm_logs/ehcvm_unit_codebook_2026-06-07.org=) is applied to =u= via
=mappings: ['ehcvm_units','Code','Preferred Label']= in
=2018/_/data_info.yml=.  Result: code leaks 25 -> 4.

Residual codes (=114, 659, 660, 662=) are *item-specific local units* --
659 banana/plantain, 660 meat, 662 yam/cassava/plantain portions, 114
tomato -- with the size in =s07bq03c= (Petit/Moyen/Grand).  They have no
label in any *2018-19* EHCVM source =.dta=, and the household-questionnaire =Unites=
sheet is the generic 1--57 scheme, NOT the data's 100--700 coding (whose
neighbours -- 663=Agoua, 664=Kpogban -- are specific local names).

=Quartier= and =Tête=.  The evidence is Togo's own 2021-22 files: =s07b_me_tgo2021.dta=
labels =s07bq03b= 659 =Main= (on Plantain, 60 rows), 660 =Quartier= (other meat, 5), 662

* Household Presence / MonthsSpent

(the continuous MonthsSpent/MonthsAway variable available in Uganda,

* 2026-09-12 -- =food_acquired=: one row, two windows (GH #876)

=food_acquired.Expenditure= used to be wired straight to =s07bq08=, whose
Stata label is "7B.08. Valeur (en FCFA) du [PRODUIT] achete la derniere
fois" -- the value of *one* purchase, which =s07bq06= places up to 30 days

identity (construction "C", @ligon 2026-09-12):

  : Quantity     = (s07bq03a - s07bq04 - s07bq05).clip(0)
  : Expenditure  = Quantity * (s07bq08 / s07bq07a)

-- the 7-day consumption net of own production AND of gifts, times the unit
value of the last purchase -- so =Expenditure / Quantity= is exactly that
unit value and the two numbers on the row refer to the same thing.  =NA=,
never a guess, where =s07bq07a= is not positive or where the purchase was
made in a different unit (or a different SIZE of the same unit) from the
consumption: no EHCVM wave ships a unit-conversion table, so there is no
exact conversion to apply.

consumption unit =s07bq03b=, with =Expenditure= *NA*.  EHCVM asks no value
for a gift; UEMOA's =depan(Don)= is that quantity priced at the same

already the rule.
