* Sampling universe, exclusions and sub-samples

Evidence gathered for GH #601 (population / universe statements).  This section
records *what the survey documentation claims* about the population each wave
represents.  It is deliberately separate from the =sample= notes elsewhere in
this file (the "DONE sample" subsection), which describe the *data* -- realized
household and cluster counts, weight variables, strata columns.  Nothing here
was inferred from the =.dta= files; every claim below is quoted from a document,
with a citation.

No schema is proposed here.  How the library should *represent* this is being
decided in GH #603.

Transcription conventions.  French quotations are reproduced with their original
accents and punctuation -- fidelity beats the ASCII house preference here, since
a de-accented quote is no longer verbatim.  The source PDFs are justified text,
so pdfminer emits runs of multiple spaces between words; that whitespace has
been collapsed to single spaces, and the typographic apostrophe (U+2019) is
rendered as ASCII ='=.  Words the PDF hyphenates across a line break (e.g.
"sous-\nechantillons", "EHCVM-\n1") are rejoined to their printed form.  Wording,
accents and punctuation are otherwise unaltered.  =[...]= marks the elision of a paragraph break between two
consecutive paragraphs.  Quotes were re-checked against the PDFs when this
section was written.

Headline findings:

- *No Burkina Faso wave excludes a region, and none is rural-only.*  All three
  declare the whole national territory, urban and rural, all 13 regions.
- *No oversample or booster is declared in any wave.*  The specialization in this
  country is sub-sampling, not oversampling.
- The thing a pooling analyst must know is the *2021-22 panel frame*: a strict
  half-sub-sample of the 2018-19 households, with no tracking outside the EA of
  origin and no top-up when an EA fell below 6 available households.

** 2014 -- Enquête Multisectorielle Continue (EMC 2014)

Source: =2014/Documentation/Report.pdf= (in-repo, DVC-tracked) -- INSD,
« Rapport -- Enquête multisectorielle continue (EMC) 2014 : Profil de pauvreté
et d'inégalités », Novembre 2015, section 1.2.1 « Champ de l'EMC », p. 20.
WB catalog id 2538.

*** Declared universe (verbatim, French)

#+begin_quote
Pour des besoins de conformité au principe de comptabilité nationale, le champ
social est constitué de l'ensemble des ménages, toutes catégories confondues,
nationaux ou non, résidant sur le territoire national. [...] Le champ
géographique de l'EMC est le territoire national burkinabè. Le niveau spatial de
représentativité des données collectées concerne les milieux de résidence
(urbain et rural) et les 13 régions administratives du pays.
#+end_quote

TRANSLATION (not source text): "To comply with the principle of national
accounting, the social scope consists of the totality of households, of all
categories, nationals or non-nationals, residing on the national territory.
[...] The geographic scope of the EMC is the Burkinabè national territory.  The
spatial level of representativeness of the data collected concerns the areas of
residence (urban and rural) and the 13 administrative regions of the country."

Sont exclus de ce champ, les ménages collectifs (camps militaires, casernes,

#+end_quote

TRANSLATION (not source text): "Excluded from this scope are collective

*** Sample design (context, not the universe)

Report.pdf section 1.2.2 « Plan de sondage », p. 20, VERBATIM: « Le plan de
sondage adopté est celui d'un sondage aréolaire stratifié à deux degrés. La
stratification est faite avant le tirage des unités primaires et basée sur
l'urbanisation des agglomérations. »  Frame, same page: « La base de sondage des
unités primaires ou zones de dénombrement de l'EMC est constituée de la liste
des 13 821 ZD définies lors de la cartographie du RGPH réalisée en 2006. »
Method: 900 ZD drawn with probability proportional to size at the first stage,
12 households per ZD systematically at the second -- « Ce qui a donné un
échantillon de 10 800 ménages. »

- The catalog DDI's =sampling_procedure= instead gives 905 EAs / ~10 860
  households.  Recorded as a design-figure discrepancy; *not adjudicated*.
- The realized counts recorded in the =sample= section of this file (10,224
  households, 900 clusters) are data-side counts, a different kind of fact from
  these design figures.  No claim is made here about which is "right".

*** Sub-samples / oversamples

None declared.  The EMC's four quarterly passages are *repeat visits to the same
households* -- Report.pdf p. 20, VERBATIM: « Le dispositif de l'EMC a prévu la
reconduite de l'ensemble des ménages enquêtés au premier passage aux trois
passages suivants (deuxième, troisième et quatrième). »  They are not distinct
sub-samples.  This matters when reading the =food_acquired= notes elsewhere in
this file (only passage 1 records quantities): that is a module-coverage fact,
not a sampling fact.

They ARE, however, four independent recall occasions, and =food_acquired=
carries them on a =visit= level for exactly that reason -- see the section
"2014 EMC =food_acquired=: FOUR PASSAGES, and 82.5% of them used to be
deleted" at the end of this file.

Pour des besoins de conformité au principe de comptabilité nationale, le champ

*** The English "UNIVERSE" field is WB NADA boilerplate -- read it accordingly

=Documentation.pdf=, p. 3, heading "UNIVERSE", VERBATIM:

#+begin_quote
The survey covered all de jure households excluding prisons, hospitals, military
barracks, and school dormitories.
#+end_quote

The same string appears byte-identically in the WB catalog DDI export

But *this exact sentence is the single most repeated statement in the whole

not word-for-word equivalent: the French additionally excludes
diplomatic-status households and the homeless, which the boilerplate does not
mention; the boilerplate names prisons and school dormitories, which the French
does not.

*** Sub-sample structure: two vagues, each a nationally representative half

This is the wave-internal specialization a pooling analyst needs.  Rapport
général, p. 16, VERBATIM:

#+begin_quote
Le dispositif de l'enquête harmonisée a prévu une répartition aléatoire de
l'échantillon des ZD en deux sous-échantillons représentatifs au niveau
national, en milieu urbain, en milieu rural et dans chacune des 13 régions
administratives.
#+end_quote

And p. 17, « Méthode de sondage », VERBATIM: « L'enquête devant se dérouler par
vague, l'échantillon des ZD est réparti de façon aléatoire en deux sous
échantillons. Chaque sous-échantillon devrait être déployé pour une vague de
collecte. »

So each *cluster* (=grappe= / ZD) belongs to exactly one vague -- consistent with
the library-wide EHCVM convention =v: grappe= (not =[vague, grappe]=).  Vague 1
ran September--November 2018, vague 2 April--June 2019 (rapport, section 1.3).

*DOCUMENTED TENSION, not adjudicated.*  The English Basic Information Document
=basicinformationdocument_bfa.pdf=, "Sample", p. 10, describes the split
differently, VERBATIM: "After that, the survey design randomly divided each
enumeration area into two equal groups.  The survey team interrogated the first
group in wave 1 and the other in wave 2."  That reads as a *within-EA household*
split -- both vagues visiting every EA -- whereas the French report splits the
*ZD sample* between vagues.  Both quotations are recorded; the repo's existing
EHCVM handling follows the French reading, and the 2021-22 BID's "half (293) for
the first wave and half (292) for the second" (quoted below) corroborates it.
This note does not settle the point.

*** THE THING TO KNOW: this wave is a panel sub-sample, not a fresh national draw

=bid_bfa_ehcvm2.pdf=, "Sample", p. 4, VERBATIM (three separate passages from that
page):

#+begin_quote
The panel sample of the EHCVM-2 2021/22 corresponds to half of the households
interviewed in EHCVM-1 2018/19 Households were tracked within the EA. In other
words, EHCVM-1 households that have left their EA of origin were not surveyed.
#+end_quote

(The missing sentence break after "EHCVM-1 2018/19" is in the source; quoted as
printed.)

#+begin_quote
Household members in 2018 who have since left the household were not followed up
physically. The survey collected some information on these individuals in one of
the modules of the EHCVM-2 2021/22 questionnaire.
#+end_quote

#+begin_quote
Sampling only 6 households out of the 12 surveyed in 2018/19 allowed for the
possibility of replacements in cases where a previously sampled household was
unavailable for the new round of surveys Additionally, if the number of available
households in a given Enumeration Area (EA) fell below 6, no additional
households were included in the survey.
#+end_quote

Consequences a user pooling this wave must weigh:

1. The *declared* universe is inherited template text describing a national
   household population.  What the 2021-22 sample can represent is bounded by a
   frame that is half of the 2018-19 households, restricted to households still
   in their EA of origin.
2. *Attrition by migration is structural, not accidental.*  Households that left
   their EA were by design not surveyed, and individuals who left the household
   were not followed physically.
3. There is *no replacement floor*: EAs that fell below 6 available households
   were not topped up, so realized cluster sizes vary in a way correlated with
   local mobility.
4. The distinct panel weight (=hhweight_panel=, see the =sample= section of this
   file) exists because of exactly this; =hhweight2021= and =hhweight_panel= are
   not interchangeable.

*** Sample design (context, verbatim from the same page)

#+begin_quote
The sampling strategy used is a two-stage sampling with stratification in the
first stage. The first stage sampling frame consists of enumeration areas (EAs)
or clusters. A study area is a portion of the population for which meaningful
results are sought, i.e., separate estimates with sufficient precision. Each
region is considered a study area, as is the entire urban environment and the
entire rural environment. The different strata result from the combination of the
13 regions with the two areas of residence (urban, rural).
#+end_quote

VERBATIM, same page: "A total of 585 EAs were selected (half (293) for the first
wave and half (292) for the second wave).  Within each EA, 6 households out of
the 12 surveyed in 2018 were surveyed."

Planned vs. realized, VERBATIM: "The sampling plan stipulated the selection of
3,510 households, with 1,758 to be surveyed during the initial wave and 1,972
during the subsequent wave.  The final actual size of the panel sample is 3,227
households, of which 1647 were interviewed in the first wave and 1,580 in the
second wave."

- The BID's own arithmetic does not close: 1,758 + 1,972 = 3,730, not 3,510.
  Quoted as printed, not corrected.
- The =sample= section of this file records 3,218 households across 550 clusters
  in the data -- a data-side count, neither confirming nor refuting these design
  figures, and not used here to adjudicate anything.

*** 2014 wave (EMC)
The 2014 survey has 4 passages (=emc2014_p{1..4}_conso7jours_*.dta=).  Only passage 1 records food quantities (=qachat=), units (=uachat=), and auto-consumption quantities (=qautocons=).  Passages 2--4 record only monetary values: purchase expenditure (=achat=), auto-consumption value (=autocons=), and gifts (=cadeau=).  All 4 passages are included; =Quantity= and =Produced= are NaN for passages 2--4.

CORRECTION (2026-09-09).  This entry used to end "... and =u= are NaN for
passages 2--4", and *"all 4 passages are included" was the intent, not the
behaviour*: a NaN on the declared =u= index level is deleted by
=groupby(dropna=True)=, so 460,438 of 557,822 rows -- every passage-2/3/4 row
-- were silently thrown away, and the served wave was passage 1 alone.  =u=
now carries the =Unknown= sentinel, and the four passages carry =visit= 1..4
so their independent 7-day recalls are not summed into one figure.  Full
write-up, with before/after numbers, in the section "2014 EMC =food_acquired=:
FOUR PASSAGES, and 82.5% of them used to be deleted" at the end of this file.

Variable names differ from EHCVM waves: cluster is =zd= (not =grappe=), food item is =product= (not =s07bq01=), etc.

*** 2018-19 and 2021-22 waves (EHCVM)
Standard EHCVM format, nearly identical to Mali.  Variable names: =s07bq01= (food item), =s07bq03a= (quantity CONSUMED over the last 7 days), =s07bq03b= (its unit) / =s07bq03c= (that unit's size), =s07bq04= (of which own-produced), =s07bq05= (of which received as a gift -- still unread), =s07bq06= (when the item was last BOUGHT: hier / 7 jours / 30 jours), =s07bq07a/b/c= (the quantity of that last purchase, its unit, its size), =s07bq08= (the VALUE OF THAT ONE LAST PURCHASE -- *not* an expenditure over the 7-day window; this line used to call it "expenditure", which the variable label refutes; corrected 2026-09-12, GH #876), =vague= (visit/passage).

=Burkina_Faso/2021-22/_/food_acquired.py= read the =.dta= with
=encoding='iso-8859-1'=.  Neither script is the builder: both waves declare

=_/data_scheme.yml= does *not* mark the table =materialize: make=.  The wave

still expects the *legacy* column names, so it could not consume the current
wave parquets either.)

*Why this matters beyond tidiness.*  =encoding== activates
=ligonlibrary.dataframes._coerce_label=, which is
~str(v).encode(enc, errors="ignore").decode("utf-8", errors="ignore")~.  For a
*format 118* file -- which =s07b_me_bfa2018.dta= is -- pandas has *already*
decoded the labels correctly as UTF-8, so the "repair" is not merely
unnecessary, it is destructive: the =errors="ignore"= on the *decode* leg
silently deletes any character that encodes into latin-1 but is not a legal
UTF-8 start byte.  Measured on that file:

: 'Boîte de tomate' -> 'Bote de tomate'   (5,235 rows)
: 'Boîte de lait'   -> 'Bote de lait'     (  326 rows)

=î= (U+00EE) encodes to the single byte =0xEE=, which is not valid UTF-8, and
is dropped without an exception.  Were these scripts revived as-is, those
5,561 rows would stop matching the =Boîte de tomate= / =Boîte de lait= keys in
=_/categorical_mapping.org= =#+NAME: u= and the unit harmonization would fail
*silently*.

*=u= is immune; =j= is NOT.*  For the reason recorded above under GH #223
Layer 2, =u= is decoded from the *numeric* =s07bq03b= code via
=mappings: ['ehcvm_units', 'Code', 'Preferred Label']=, so value labels never
enter *that* key path.  But =j= is mapped via
=mappings: ['harmonize_food', 'Original Label', 'Preferred Label']=, and the
*Original Label* column of =_/categorical_mapping.org= =#+NAME: harmonize_food=
carries *46 mojibake rows* -- it is keyed on =Ã=-form strings exactly the way
Senegal's =u= table is.  Measured on 2018-19: *47,240 of 136,888 rows* key on a
mojibake =harmonize_food= entry.  So Burkina Faso's =j= *is* load-bearing
mojibake; the immunity is only on =u=.

** The idiosyncrasy

The EMC 2014 is a *continuous* survey.  =Report.pdf= p. 20, quoted verbatim
under "Sub-samples / oversamples" above: « Le dispositif de l'EMC a prévu la
reconduite de l'ensemble des ménages enquêtés au premier passage aux trois
passages suivants ».  The 7-day food module is *re-asked at every passage*, so
the wave delivers *4 x 7 = 28 days* of exposure in four INDEPENDENT recalls.
A passage-3 row is a different measurement of the same (household, item), not
a second transaction inside one 7-day window.

*The passage number is in the FILENAME and in no source column.*  Measured on
all four =emc2014_p{1..4}_conso7jours_16032015.dta=: none carries a passage /
vague / round variable.  The only per-passage marks are *suffixed* copies of
household attributes (=hhweight1= / =hhsize1= in p1, =hhweight2= in p2, ...),
which is not a variable you can key on.  So =visit= must be *stamped per file*.

*Passage 1 is the only one with a quantity grid.*  p1 fields =qachat= /
=uachat= / =qautocons=; p2-p4 record money only (=achat=, =autocons=,
=cadeau=).  Row and CFA totals as extracted:

|       1 |     171,236 |        152,589 | 4,208,738 |        93,383,167 |

** What was wrong -- TWO mechanisms, and only one of them was the visible one

1. *No =visit= level.*  All four files were declared under one
   =(t, v, i, j, u, s)= index.  =food_acquired= is in
   =feature._ADDITIVE_MEASURE_COLUMNS=, so four passages landing on one tuple
   are *SUMMED* into a single figure served as a 7-day number.
2. *=u= arrived NaN for every p2-p4 row* (no =uachat= column exists in those
   files), and for 55,205 p1 rows whose =uachat= is blank.  =u= is a DECLARED
   index level and pandas =groupby(dropna=True)= *deletes* a row with a NaN
   key, so =_normalize_dataframe_index= threw those rows away *before* the sum
   could happen.

*Mechanism 2 is why mechanism 1 was invisible.*  The passages were not being
summed -- they were being *deleted*.  Measured on the pre-fix build:

: 557,822 canonical rows extracted -> 460,438 DELETED (82.5%) -> 97,361 served
: CFA 342,110,515 extracted        -> CFA  80,286,096 served  (76.5% lost)

so =Country('Burkina_Faso').food_acquired()= at =t='2014'= was *passage 1's
unit-bearing rows and nothing else*, presented as the wave.  This is the worst
instance in the corpus of the NaN-index-key class (CLAUDE.md "Grain Collapse"
§3b names this exact cell; =.coder/ledger/323-framework.md= §7 D2 deferred the
framework-level change).  The cell nonetheless graded =sane= throughout.

*The prior review's framing -- "132,860 index groups hold >1 row, group sizes
2/3/4 tracking the passage count" -- reproduces exactly but does NOT mean the
passages were summed.*  Those groups are an artefact of grouping with
=dropna=False=: they pool p2/p3/p4 (plus p1's unit-less rows) on a shared NaN
=u=.  The real collapse runs with =dropna=True= and never reaches them.  Among
the rows that DID survive, only 22 groups were multi-row, all inside passage 1.

** The fix

- =2014/_/data_info.yml= stamps =visit= per file through the framework's
  per-file override block, using the only constant-stamp spelling config has:
  an always-present column (=zd=) mapped through =visit_p{n}= in
  =2014/_/mapping.py=, four functions that ignore their argument and return
  the passage number.  =zd= carries no information here.  =visit= is declared
  as a *myvar*, not an idxvar, because =df_data_grabber= runs =format_id= over
  every idxvar and would serve the passage number as a *string*.
- =2014/_/mapping.py::food_acquired= reshapes each passage separately and
  re-stamps =visit= onto the index as an =int= with
  =build_transforms.add_visit_level= -- which was written for this wave (its
  docstring names it) and until now had zero production callers.  It RAISES if
  the frame reaches it with no =visit= column, so a future config edit cannot
  silently re-merge two recall occasions.
- The same hook fills a missing =u= with the corpus-canonical =Unknown=
  sentinel (GH #842; the Niger pattern, =Niger/_/niger.py::fill_missing_u=).
  *Without this the =visit= fix would be cosmetic* -- p2-p4 never reach the
  index at all.
- =2018-19= and =2021-22= ask the module once, so their =mapping.py= appends
  =add_visit_level(..., visit=1)=: one index shape across the three waves, no
  number changed.  EHCVM's =vague= is a *sample split*, not a repeated
  measure, and is dropped upstream by =food_acquired_to_canonical= -- reusing
  it as =visit= would falsely imply a second recall.
- =_/data_scheme.yml= declares =index: (t, v, i, j, u, s, visit)=.

*Why config and not a =materialize: make= script.*  A script is the repo's
usual answer to per-file constants, and =add_visit_level= is script-shaped.
It was rejected here for three reasons: the section above records that 2014 is
built through the YAML path and its =j= is keyed on *load-bearing mojibake* in
=harmonize_food=, so a script would have to re-implement that crosswalk and
would drift from the open GH #794 / #805 fix; script-path L2-wave parquets are
written hashless and cannot self-invalidate; and the alternative core edit (a
constant primitive in =Country.column_mapping=) sits in
=build_transforms_fingerprint= for *every table in every country* and would
cold-rebuild the corpus.

* 2026-09-12 -- =food_acquired=: one row, two windows (GH #876)

=food_acquired.Expenditure= used to be wired straight to =s07bq08=, whose
Stata label is "7B.08. Valeur (en FCFA) du [PRODUIT] achete la derniere
fois" -- the value of *one* purchase, which =s07bq06= places up to 30 days
back -- sitting on the same row as =Quantity=, a *7-day* consumption figure.
Section 7B is headed "Consommation alimentaire des 7 derniers jours et achat
des 30 derniers jours"; the row carried both clocks and nothing in the frame
said so.  =s07bq05=, the quantity received as a GIFT, was never read at all.

The served =Expenditure= is now DERIVED, on the survey's own accounting
identity (construction "C", @ligon 2026-09-12):

  : Quantity     = (s07bq03a - s07bq04 - s07bq05).clip(0)
  : Expenditure  = Quantity * (s07bq08 / s07bq07a)

-- the 7-day consumption net of own production AND of gifts, times the unit
value of the last purchase -- so =Expenditure / Quantity= is exactly that
unit value and the two numbers on the row refer to the same thing.  =NA=,
never a guess, where =s07bq07a= is not positive or where the purchase was
made in a different unit (or a different SIZE of the same unit) from the
consumption: no EHCVM wave ships a unit-conversion table, so there is no
exact conversion to apply.

And the gift quantity is now SERVED, on its own =s = 'inkind'= rows in the
consumption unit =s07bq03b=, with =Expenditure= *NA*.  EHCVM asks no value
for a gift; UEMOA's =depan(Don)= is that quantity priced at the same
last-purchase unit value -- verified below, and therefore an imputation, not
an answer -- so the library checks it and does not serve it.  Own production
is unchanged: =s = 'produced'= from =s07bq04=, =Expenditure= NA, which was
already the rule.

One function for all eight EHCVM countries (=lsms_library/ehcvm.py=,
=derive_ehcvm_purchase_value=), registered once per country in
=_/derivations.yml= and stamped on every purchased row in the =Derivation=
column:

  : Burkina_Faso::food_acquired::7day-purchase-at-last-purchase-unit-value

Raw answers come back by their original names from
=Country('Burkina_Faso').derivation_inputs(key, wave=...)=.

the canonical reshape and the =(t, v, i, j, u, s)= collapse).

The three candidate quantities, and why C:

- 2018-19: A (gross =s07bq03a=) 54,075,976, B (net of own production) 53,497,881, *C (served)* 52,466,280 FCFA; raw =s07bq08= was 65,315,308.
- 2021-22: A (gross =s07bq03a=) 29,381,603, B (net of own production) 29,220,369, *C (served)* 27,888,221 FCFA; raw =s07bq08= was 39,905,158.

The three are within 1-9% of each other and all far below the raw total, so
the large move is the #876 fix itself, not the choice among them.  The choice
is settled by =ehcvm_conso=, not by the size of the number.

  The product-level check against =ehcvm_conso= does not run for this wave --
