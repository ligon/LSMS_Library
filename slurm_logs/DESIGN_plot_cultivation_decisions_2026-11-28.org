#+TITLE: Plot cultivation: preserve reported decision roles by plot and season
#+DATE: 2026-11-28

* Status and recommendation

Design proposal for GH #862 under #167 and #439, not a registration or a
commitment to ship the table. Keep the after-defect-queue implementation
order. Review the proposed contract first. If accepted, Uganda 2015–16
is the first candidate, conditional on its own release-specific person,
plot and branch validation. No country is cleared for implementation by
this note.

Keep the plot-season as the parent and use a *separate actor relation*.
The older =DESIGN_erhs_plot_features_2026-05-20.org= reserves
=plot_cultivation= for seasonal plot detail and illustrates crop rows
with =crop_seq=. A crop occurrence and a reported decision reference are
not the same grain. Recommend reserving =plot_cultivation= for seasonal
plot/crop detail and explicitly naming the actor relation
=plot_cultivation_decisions=. This is a naming proposal, not a second
registration. If review instead assigns =plot_cultivation= to the actor
relation, give any future crop occurrence relation its own name and retain
the same separation. Do not retrofit =plot_features=, repurpose Niger's
=cultivation_records= (raw household/observation grain), or multiply
each actor by every crop on that plot.

Use the owner-expansion precedent from
=DESIGN_lsms_plus_individual_disaggregation_2026-06-14.org= only for
preserving distinct people and roles. It does not make an interviewer-
selected plot respondent the decision-maker, turn a slot into a rank,
or imply private self-report. This proposal is not an ownership,
sex-of-decision-maker, consent, legal-rights or input-allocation output.

* Proposed source and serving contracts

** Observation accounting before serving

Start from one verified native agricultural observation key per
country, edition, physical source block and household/plot. Bind the
section's slot fields to that observation and record the release, source
block, visit, branch, role, slot and decision domain as separate operands.
Do not infer identity from interleaving, stable-looking row numbers,
DDI extrema or coincident numeric codes. Preserve raw rows and slots
losslessly in private reconciliation. An exact-file row ordinal can
locate evidence in the same bytes, not identify an episode across
versions. Repeated native parent keys are an unresolved serving problem
unless an authoritative stable source coordinate distinguishes them.

Account for every candidate slot before projection, including not asked
by the printed route, eligible blank, unresolved route, nonblank off
branch, unresolved identity/season, verified identity and explicit
source conflict. Do not make totals from public DDI marginals or equate
blank, unchecked and No. A nonblank slot with a null, nonmatching or
ambiguous roster bridge is unresolved source evidence. It is not a
person key or a synthetic household-head row. Keep its source evidence
in bounded private reconciliation and report counts by approved
country/wave/module/status. Public actor rows cover verified reported
participants only, not all decisions, all plots or everyone responsible
for cultivation. A plot with no actor row is not a known negative.
Before serving any partial pilot, review which unresolved statuses may
remain and how the resulting coverage is disclosed. Distinguish a
reference proven absent from its applicable roster from an unverified
namespace, unavailable roster or missing response. Absent review, a
source error or unresolved release mapping keeps the affected scope held.

** Proposed actor grain

=(t, v, i, plot_id, season, decision_domain, pid, role, role_slot)=
for the Country result, with =country= prefixed by Feature. Retain =v=
under the ordinary sample-join policy, using the canonical plot axis
=plot_id=, not an automatic textual translation of any source plot number.
Do not use =v= as a roster-identity operand. Bind =pid= to its verified
same-wave roster namespace before the normal API-time alignment. If the
ordinary sample join cannot supply required =v=, treat the scope as held
instead of dropping actors or borrowing a cluster.

The substantive relation in #862 is plot × season × person × role.
=decision_domain= and =role_slot= preserve observations lost by that
projection. A reported person can occupy two slots with the same role,
or contribute to both crop choice and input use. Keep both reports
until an explicitly requested analytic projection. Slots are literal
reported positions, not ordered shares, scores or priorities. The role
says what the question means, for example sole decision-maker, joint
decision-maker, primary decision-maker or consulted member. A domain
may be a combined management question rather than a single action.
Never manufacture distinct decision-domain rows from one combined
answer. Check source and public uniqueness before reduction, even if repeated
rows agree. The current =most_complete_row= reducer can still discard
a distinct report if its identity level is lost. Do not depend on a
post-projection row count to recognize this.

A =season= is a supported wave-local agricultural reference key, not a
physical module, recorded interview date, elapsed period or inferred
season from another table. Uganda can retain A/B as local encoded season
keys only alongside the documented reference mapping for the two
management blocks. Module and visit also travel separately. Preserve
reference year and season meaning as explicit provenance, not a
cross-country calendar category. If a season cannot be resolved,
do not publish an actor row in this first *seasonal* schema. A later
non-null unknown-season sentinel or a separate unseasoned observation
product would require its own review and cannot be an equivalence join
key. Avoid null index coordinates and don't let a default sentinel
supply missing identities.

** Disclosure and joins

Roster enrichment is a validated left many-to-one join on the proven
in-scope person namespace, with counts for unmatched, multiply matched
and out-of-universe references. Use native interview scope where source
IDs require it and check the final bridge to the served roster
separately. Retain =t= and =i= even if a file claims globally unique
person IDs. Keep household head, plot respondent, consulted person,
owner and external network role in their actual domains. A later
gender-summary transform may specify a fallback if approved and labelled
as derived. It is not a stored actor or evidence for who decided.

An identical spelling of =plot_id= or a shared A/B token is not a
cross-table join contract. A lasting plot may need a many-to-one
own-wave bridge from a subdivision to the library's parcel key.
Another plot/season/crop table can have multiple outputs, conditions
or observations per native plot-season. Verify the keys, temporal
reference and intended many-to-many cardinality before making any
explicit actor-to-crop link. Do not assume that crop-level costs, area,
amounts or yield are person-level quantities. The first pilot supplies
no default join or imputation.

* Evidence-bound starting points

** Uganda 2015–16: first candidate, not yet admitted

The [[https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/3460/download/46595][UBOS agriculture form, PDF pp.7 and 15]] prints AGSEC3A
as visit 1, second cropping season of 2014, and AGSEC3B as visit 2,
first cropping season of 2015 for these management questions. The
[[https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/3460/download/46598][producer BID, PDF pp.15–18]]
corroborates the visit/file separation and the intended season windows
in its main agricultural descriptions. Localized inconsistent year or
season wording in the BID and the separate Section 5 crop-production
form must not be normalized away. Existing library A/B tokens are
source-letter conventions, not universal calendar seasons.

Q3.1 is the respondent. Q3.2 codes 1 as Single and 2 as Multiple.
Single selects Q3.3, the sole primary report, while Multiple selects
the unranked Q3.4a/Q3.4b references. This is one combined plot-management
domain spanning timing, crop choice and input use. Do not take the union
of all populated columns. The [[https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/3460/data-dictionary/F9][public B dictionary]]
shows 3,886 Multiple answers but 3,888 reported-valid first multiple
slots. If those summaries refer to the same cases, at least two valid
cells cannot be in the Multiple branch. This is a bound on card
marginals, not an attribution to specific records or proof about
valid person IDs. Resolve branch/carrier, missing codes and off-branch
reports from the exact release before serving.

Use current native household, parcel and plot fields
(=HHID=, =parcelID=, =plotID=) on the two core files. Verify exact
case grain, version and lineage to the existing Uganda agricultural
plot key. It uses the native household inside the plot key even when
the public household walk changes =i=. Do not reconstruct that key from
walked =i=. The existing =plot_features= uses AGSEC2A/B parcel suffixes
for owned or use-rights land, not for cropping seasons. Its plot axis
is not the agricultural subdivision key. Existing crop-producer A/B and agricultural key
recipes are useful join candidates, not verification of cross-module
membership or the Section 5 reference year. The person dictionary and
BID describe a dynasty =PID=, while a visit roster has its own member
reference scope and update. Test the actual Q3 reference namespace
against the same-release, same-household roster applicable to the
visit. A visit update is not proof of membership during every earlier
reference season. Neither
=h2q1= nor =gsec2.pid= is an approved direct mapping merely because it
exists. Keep household panel walking distinct from these person IDs.

** Tanzania 2019–20 and 2020–21: later, separate approvals per block

Both [[https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/3885/download/54805][SDD form]] and
[[https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/5639/download/60750][Wave 5 form]]
distinguish A long rains and B last completed short rains. The intended
A windows are Masika 2018 and Masika 2020 respectively. Short-rains
reference year is a reported choice, not the wave start or the A year.
SDD's public B decision-card labels repeat A's long-rains wording and
the printed SDD year choices also disagree in separate places. Wave
5's B block corroborates the short-rains frame but does not supply a
uniform year. In that release =ag_filters.ag2a_11=, =hh_sec_u2.ag2a_11=
and A's Q75 are candidate year carriers with different scopes or
provenance. Show which carrier applies to each B observation and
reconcile any overlaps. A proved season key includes both kind and
year (for example =vuli:2019= versus =vuli:2020=), not B alone. In both forms the B plot already listed in A follows a
printed Q1b skip towards Q40, not another Q8/Q9 answer by assumption.
Distinguish an unavailable B block or missing B parent from a verified
structural skip, asked-but-unanswered, or explicit No. A short-rain
cultivation screen is not the Q1b repeat-list flag. Do not infer B
values from A.

Treat Q8 crop choice and Q9 input decisions as separate domains.
In both waves the household person slots are =08b_1= through =08b_3=
and =09b_1= through =09b_3= within the A or B prefix. The =a_1/a_2=
fields indicate household or outside-household participation. They
are not person IDs. The corresponding =c1/c2= network report and
location, and the separate plot respondent, are not household person
slots and do not become actors by casting to strings or numbers.
An affirmative participation indicator without a linkable reference
does not identify a person.

Bind each field to the exact released A/B and same-wave roster
namespaces, with the current plot keys and the actually applicable
interview scope. Do not borrow previous-round plot IDs, a reused
plot number, or a household key from a split-off as a person bridge.
The historical Tanzania Section 2-to-3A plot cross-check is A-only.
The B stage needs admitted bytes for the exact already-declared
operands and version-bound evidence for the source, routing, year
and roster. Count by exact block and domain, including shared, A-only
and B-only plot classes and unresolved routes or keys. A proof for A
or one wave cannot clear B or the other.
See the public [[https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/3885/data-dictionary/F41][SDD B]] and
[[https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/5639/data-dictionary/F42][Wave 5 B]] dictionaries.

** Ethiopia ESS4 2018–19: preserve role evidence, defer seasonal serving

The [[https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/3823/download/49204][post-planting field form, PDF pp.6–8]]
and [[https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/3823/data-dictionary/F47][public v05 dictionary]]
identify Q13 as one primary actor for the combined management
question on the applicable cultivated field, Q14 as the household
consultation gate, and Q15.1/Q15.2 as up to two consulted household
members, unranked. Consultation is not joint decision-making; Q14 No
does not prove there was no outsider consultation. Preserve the
holder-qualified field key already used by Ethiopia. Resolve
references against the same post-planting agriculture roster first,
starting from the candidate household/holder/member-reference tuple.
Then independently verify any bridge to the main household roster.
The printed prefill intent and similarly numbered person fields do
not establish identity. Keep an unmatched PP-only household
unresolved rather than using its holder, contact or head.

Post-planting dates, the wave range and nearby questions about
Meher/Belg do not bind a role-specific season. Do not call the wave,
post-planting or the interview month a season. A later unseasoned
source-observation view could preserve role/slot evidence without the
claim, but is not part of the first public seasonal contract. This
issue neither implements that view nor changes Ethiopia source versions.

* Discriminating acceptance cases (invented)

| Situation | Required result |
|-----------+-----------------|
| Uganda A Single, Q3 points to P, Q4a contains Q | Serve only the verified sole report. Account for Q as off-branch, never as joint. |
| Uganda B Multiple, Q3 contains P, Q4a and Q4b refer to Q | Preserve two distinct reported positions for Q, not two unique people. P remains an off-branch observation. |
| Same Uganda current plot and person answer in A and B | Keep both seasons even if every payload cell is equal. |
| Missing/unknown Uganda route with a populated slot, or multiple roster matches | Count unresolved evidence. No synthetic or arbitrarily selected person row. |
| Tanzania same verified person at Q8 and Q9, and an unrelated numeric network code | Preserve both domains. Network code is not a third household person. |
| Tanzania B prints short rains but its export label says long rains, or the year is not bound | Do not assign A's window or publish a claimed B season without independent authority. |
| Ethiopia Q13 refers to P, Q14 Yes, Q15 refers to Q and R | Keep the roles distinct in reconciliation. No seasonal rows on the current evidence. |
| Same printed person number or plot number in two households or releases | Prove the correct scope. Never use a generic lookup on the number alone. |
| One plot-season has two crops and two actor slots | Preserve two crop occurrences and two actor reports independently, not four default records. |
| A populated household-head identifier is the only available person | No reported actor. A separately reviewed derived rule may be labelled as such. |

* Review gates and staging

1. Review naming and grain, with the long actor relation and separately
   reserved crop detail as the recommendation. Keep GH #862 open.
2. After the defect queue and design approval, admit the exact Uganda
   2015–16 A/B management files, declared roster/cover and necessary
   native key operands for a private bounded projection. Verify both
   branch marginals and their joint row membership, every eligible
   slot's lineage, repeated keys, missing/special codes, within-
   household roster cardinalities, source-only vs served plot keys,
   and cold/warm and coverage behavior. Report aggregate discrepancy
   counts and reason categories without publishing keys. A source
   discrepancy or an irresolvable grain means revise or hold the
   relevant scope, not automatic source repair.
3. A later reviewed implementation should preserve native observations
   through extraction, use approved schema and =index_info= with the
   canonical level names, and verify Country and Feature on invented
   mixed-slot cases plus the admitted pilot. Check distinct-season,
   slot and role preservation, Country-to-Feature row conservation,
   named levels, absence of hidden collapse, valid coverage for
   one versus both seasons, separate bridge cardinality checks, and
   unchanged existing plot, roster, input and crop outputs. Use
   =bench/feature_audit/= only within the admitted feature/country/data
   scope. Include no unsupported registered schema or missing-country
   default rows.
4. Treat later Uganda waves, Tanzania and Ethiopia as separate source
   and contract reviews. Do not extend this proposal to Niger's
   existing household/observation table, an imputed gender statistic,
   or a remote data acquisition task.

This design uses public form/dictionary evidence and tracked code
inspected at =d18b47de60e4=. It neither remeasures source rows nor binds
a deployed questionnaire or published export to previously checked
bytes. No library declaration, source input, cache, served data or
analysis default changes.
