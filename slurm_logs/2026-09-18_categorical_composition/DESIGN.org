* 1. The model

** 1.1 Three concepts, three words

The brief is right that one word is doing three jobs. Stated precisely:

- *inherits* --- a relation between two /levels/ (wave, country, global) holding
  a table of the same name. Either the lower level *replaces* the upper, or it
  *extends* it. This is the granularity question of #953.
- *composes* --- a property of two /key columns/: they compose iff they draw on
  the same namespace, so a row of one can override a row of the other. Two
  label columns over the same survey vocabulary compose; a code column and a
  label column do not, /whatever their headings say/.
- *commensurable* --- a property of a /fold/: when a table maps n>1 source
  values to one =Preferred Label=, are the values measured under those n source
  labels safe to *add* (or to convert with one kg factor)? =Kg=+=Kilogramme=
  yes; =Lb=+=Kg= no; =bidon de 5 litres=+=bidon de 200 litres= no.

The third is asserted *by the fold itself*, not by any cross-level merge. It
applies to a purely country-level table that the =_ADDITIVE_CATEGORICAL_TABLES=
frozenset never touches, which is exactly why #833 is an open defect in five
countries on the =j= axis with no mechanism watching it.

** 1.2 Composable columns are label-keyed

Measured over 312 tables, keys classified by /content/ (every non-blank key
value numeric -> code; none numeric -> label; otherwise mixed):

| key namespace | tables |
|---------------+--------|
| label         |    148 |
| code          |    142 |
| mixed         |     22 |

Label-keyed is the composing namespace. A country's label key and the global
table's label key name the same thing (a survey's own spelling of a unit, a
school level, an asset) and a row of one legitimately overrides a row of the
other. Two code keys do *not* compose across levels even though both are
"codes": Nigeria's =1 = Kg= and Uganda's =1 = Kg= agree by coincidence, and
nothing in the corpus guarantees the next pair will.

*Therefore: only label-keyed tables compose, and only within one country's
level stack or against the global level.* Measured, the global level is
already almost there: of its *5 live tables* (the other two global files,
=canonical_education_labels.org= and =canonical_housing_labels.org=, declare
none), =u=, =harmonize_assets= and =harmonize_education= are all
=Original Label=-keyed and =u_kg= keys on the canonical =Unit=. The single
exception is =ehcvm_units=, which is =Code=-keyed --- and it is consumed
exclusively by explicit =mappings: ['ehcvm_units','Code','Preferred Label']=
list forms in three wave =data_info.yml= files, never by the merge. So no
global table composes on a code namespace today; open decision 5 asks whether
=ehcvm_units= should stay there.

** 1.3 Additional columns do not compose; they are level-scoped

The brief's point 1. Measured: *91 distinct headings*, *812 (table, heading)
pairs*. Classified by role:

| role | distinct headings | (table,heading) pairs |
|------+-------------------+-----------------------|
| =canonical= (=Preferred Label=)                  |  1 | 280 |
| =key= (=Code=, =Original Label=, =Alternate Spelling=, =Text=, =Unit=, =Aggregate=, =GD Category=, =proizvod=) |  8 | 306 |
| =label= (alternate output: =Aggregate Label=, =Label=, =Comparable Label=, =Nutrition Label=, =Aggregate (short)=) |  5 |  49 |
| =wave-scoped= (=2019-20=, =Code_8h=, =Label_12B=, =Wave=, =File=, ...) --- *and this class is two things, see §1.6* | 52 | 134 |
| =extra= (local evidence: =FCT ID=, =Basis=, =Note=, =Food=, =Species=, ...) | 25 |  43 |

So: *14 headings carry 635 of 812 pairs* and are the only ones that need to
mean the same thing at two levels. The 25 =extra= headings are level-local by
construction and are exactly what outer-joining on merge (2026-06, :117)
handles correctly. The model does not change what happens to them; it lets a
file /say/ that is what they are. *The 52 wave-scoped headings are NOT all
level-local, and §1.3b is that correction.*

The =extra= tail also contains a legible cost of never having said so: the
food-composition-table identifier is spelled *eight* ways in eight files ---
=FCT ID= (3), =FCT Code=, =FCT code=, =FTC Code=, =FDC ID=, =Food Codes=,
=FCT Label=, =FTC Label=. Under the model these stay level-local and their
spelling stops mattering, because nothing tries to compose them.

** 1.3b A wave-named column is usually a KEY, not a presence mark --- and the model has no spelling for it

The =wave-scoped= class above is two different objects sharing one shape, and
separating them is the one place the corpus refuses to fit the model as briefed.
Measured over all 134 (table, heading) pairs in that class, by whether the
column's non-blank values are drawn from {=x=, =X=, =yes=, =✓=, =1=, =*=}:

| what the column holds | pairs | tables |
|-----------------------+-------+--------|
| a presence *mark*     |  *12* | 5 (*all Mali*) |
| free strings --- i.e. *that wave's raw label* | *122* | *28*, in 14 countries |

Uganda's =u= is the clearest instance:

: | Code | Preferred Label | 2005-06        | 2009-10  | ... | 2019-20        |
: |    1 | Kg              | Kilogram (kg)  | Kilogram | ... | Kilogram (kg)  |
: |    0 | (blank)         |                |          | ... |                |

That is *three* namespaces in one table --- an instrument =Code=, a canonical
=Preferred Label=, and eight per-wave *label keys* --- and the positional rule
picks the =Code=. GhanaLSS's =harmonize_food= is the same shape with the key
column entirely absent from the =Code= slot (§3.1). The affected tables are the
ones the =u= and =j= axes care most about: GhanaLSS =harmonize_food= (35 wave
columns), Nigeria =harmonize_food= (8), Uganda =u= (8), GhanaLSS =nonfood_items=
(6), Ethiopia / Malawi =harmonize_food= (5 each), Nigeria =u= (4), Tanzania =u=
(3), Niger =u= (2), ...

*Consequence for §1.3.* A per-wave key column *must not* be outer-joined across
levels: it is blank for six of seven waves by construction, and unioning it
against another level's table produces a column whose blanks and values are not
distinguishable from real absences. Only Mali's 12 presence marks are safe to
outer-join, and they are 12 of 134.

*Consequence for the sentinel.* =#+key:= as a single column name cannot express
"the column named after the wave being built". Two options, both cheap:

- =#+key: {wave}= --- a template value the resolver substitutes with the wave
  label at read time, falling back to =#+key:='s other value (or the positional
  rule) when no such column exists. ~one branch in the resolver; expresses the
  28 tables exactly.
- Declare these tables *out of model*: =#+column: 2016-17 = wavekey= and no
  cross-level composition for the table at all (whole-table override, i.e.
  today's behaviour). Honest, zero new machinery, and gives up composing the
  28 tables that most need it.

*Recommend =#+key: {wave}=, and note it is inseparable from §7 open decision 1*:
the 28 per-wave-key tables and the only country with intra-country multi-level
tables (GhanaLSS) are the same population, so "does the wave rung extend or
replace?" and "can the key be wave-templated?" are one decision.

** 1.4 A code-keyed table is a terminal decode

The brief's point 2, adopted, with one addition: the decode is a /stage/, not a
/rung/. A code-keyed table is applied first and its output --- a label --- is
then the input to the label-keyed fold. Formally, for a given axis in a given
country:

: served = fold_global( fold_country( decode_wave_or_country( raw ) ) )

where =decode= is the identity when no code-keyed table is declared. Composition
of functions, not union of rows. The condition that makes it well-formed is
*totality*: every code the source emits must appear in the decode table, so no
numeric code reaches a label-keyed stage.

*Totality is nearly true, and its violations are cheap to name.* Two
measurements.

/Internal/ (does every code /in the table/ reach a non-blank label?): 142
code-keyed tables, *8 violate*, *153 rows*:

| country | table | unmapped codes |
|---------+--------------------+----|
| Uganda      | =u=                  | *143* |
| Tanzania    | =harmonize_species=  | 3 |
| EthiopiaRHS | =erhs_relation_1989= | 2 |
| EthiopiaRHS | =erhs_sex=           | 2 |
| EthiopiaRHS | =erhs_relation=      | 1 |
| EthiopiaRHS | =harmonize_unit=     | 1 |
| Malawi      | =harmonize_species=  | 1 |
| EthiopiaRHS | =erhs_site_region_1989= | 5 rows whose target is itself numeric |

Uganda's 143 are #928's =---= cells on =u=, i.e. one cell is 93% of the corpus
violation. Separately, *2 identity-map rows* survive D4's "delete identity-map
rows" instruction --- Burkina Faso =harmonize_food=, =11.0 -> 11.0= and
=15.0 -> 15.0= --- and they are the only 2 rows corpus-wide whose
=Preferred Label= is itself numeric.

/External/ (does every code /the data emits/ appear in the table?), measured by
applying each country's own =#+name: u= to the warm
=var/food_acquired.parquet= and counting values that still begin with a digit.
Restricted to the 12 countries that declare =#+name: u=:

| country | food_acquired rows | code-like =u= rows | share | distinct |
|---------+--------+-------+-------+----|
| Togo         | 112,445 | *2,060* | 1.83% | 4 |
| Malawi       | 971,531 | *3,894* | 0.40% | 59 |
| Burkina Faso | 765,816 |    129  | 0.02% | 2 |
| Nigeria      | 595,725 |      1  | 0.00% | 1 |
| Benin, CotedIvoire, Ethiopia, Guinea-Bissau, Mali, Niger, Senegal, Tanzania | -- | *0* | -- | 0 |

*8 of 12 are total; 4 are not.* Togo's four missing codes (659, 660, 662, 114)
are the same gap D4 named in 2026-06 ("Togo: inherit metric units; add the
missing code rows"). Three years on, totality is a real and unmet condition ---
which is the argument for making it a /check/ rather than an /instruction/.

** 1.5 Commensurability is asserted by the fold

Measured over every table on the =u= or =j= axis that has a =Preferred Label=,
counting each =Preferred Label= receiving n>1 source values:

| axis | folds | all sources are spelling variants | all sources numeric (codes) | *distinct labels* |
|------+-------+------+------+------|
| =j=  | 778   | 174  | 149  | *455* |
| =u=  | 223   |  28  |  18  | *177* |

The /spelling/ column is machine-verifiable: after NFKD-stripping,
case-folding, removing non-alphanumerics and a plural/=-mme= suffix, all
sources normalise to one string. Those 202 folds are commensurable by
construction and need no assertion.

The *632 distinct-label folds* are the unguarded surface. #833 names five
countries on =j=; the =u= axis is comparable in kind and concentrated
differently: Senegal 55, Niger 41, Malawi 23, GhanaLSS =harmonize_price_unit=
17, Ethiopia 13, Mali 11, Burkina Faso 10. Examples from Burkina's =u=, all
folding to a single =Preferred Label= that then receives a single =u_kg=
factor:

- =Bidon= <- {=Bidon=, =bidon de 5 litres=, =bidon de 20 litres=, =bidon de 200 litres=} --- a *40x* spread
- =Boîte= <- {=Boite=, =Boîte=, =Boîte de lait=, =Boîte de tomate=, =grosse boite de tomate=}
- =Kg= <- {=Kg=, =kg=, =Avec os au Kg=, =Sans os au Kg=} --- commensurable as a mass, but it folds a /condition/ (with bone / without bone), the =crop_production= =condition= axis of #833

A /machine-catchable subset/ exists and is small: folds whose source labels
carry *conflicting explicit magnitudes*. Corpus-wide there are exactly *2* ---
Burkina =Bidon= (5 / 20 / 200 L) and GhanaLSS =harmonize_price_unit= =Piece=
(325 ml / 330 ml). Two is not a lot, and that is the point: the remaining 630
are /not/ machine-decidable, so the only instrument available is a curator's
signed assertion. That is what the sentinel is for.

* 2. The sentinel vocabulary and its spelling

** 2.1 Spelling: org keyword lines above the table

#+begin_src org
#+name: u
#+key: Code
#+keyspace: code
#+fold: spelling
#+column: Preferred Label = canonical
#+column: 2018-19 = presence
#+column: FCT ID = extra
| Code | Preferred Label | 2018-19 | FCT ID |
#+end_src

All four keywords are *optional*; every one has a default that reproduces
today's behaviour byte for byte.

| keyword | values | default | answers |
|---------+--------+---------+---------|
| =#+key:=      | a column name | first non-=Preferred Label= column | which column is the key |
| =#+keyspace:= | =label= \vert =code= \vert a named namespace | inferred from content | does this key *compose* |
| =#+fold:=     | =spelling= \vert =asserted= \vert =none= | *undeclared* (no check --- today's behaviour) | is a many-to-one fold *commensurable* |
| =#+column:=   | =<Name> = canonical\vert label\vert presence\vert extra\vert meta= | inferred per §1.3 | does this column compose |

=#+key:= is #928's P1 *verbatim*; this design subsumes it rather than competing
with it. #928's P2 (one resolver, all call sites) is the mechanism that reads
all four. #928's P3 =Status= / =Notes= become =#+column: Status = meta= ---
i.e. reservation is /declared/, which answers #928's own open question ("should
=Status= / =Notes= be reserved names, or should reservation be declared?") in
favour of declared. Nothing in #928 is deferred except P4's =pd.NA= collapse,
which is orthogonal.

** 2.2 Why a keyword line and not a header convention or a subdirectory

*Verified, not assumed*: a file containing =#+key:=, =#+keyspace:=, =#+fold:=
and =#+column:= lines between =#+name:= and the table parses today, with the
keywords silently skipped and the table's columns unchanged
(=local_tools.all_dfs_from_orgfile=; its "skip any extra =#+= lines" loop is
=local_tools.py:3062ff=, and is also the #693 bug). So adoption is incremental
with *zero breakage*: old code ignores the lines, new code honours them, no
table must declare anything.

*A header convention (=Code:key=, =FCT ID:extra=) is ruled out by count.* Column
headings are referenced by literal string in *28* places in =lsms_library/*.py=
and =util/*.py=, and in *177* =mappings: [table, key, value]= list entries in
wave =data_info.yml= files (of 189 =mappings:= lines). Decorating the heading
breaks every one of them and forces a lockstep migration of config and code.

*A subdirectory or a new file is ruled out by the cache hasher.* The three
sites that fold mapping files into a build fingerprint all use a
*non-recursive* =glob("*.org")= --- =country.py:943-947= (wave: country =_/= and
the global dir), =country.py:948-952= (the =parent_cmap= single-file fallback)
and =country.py:3565= (country-level build inputs). A =categorical_mapping.d/=
would be invisible to all three, so editing a table there would invalidate
nothing: the #808 / #866 disease introduced deliberately. A keyword line lives
inside an already-hashed =.org= and needs *no hasher change at all*. (If #953
option 1 or @ligon's per-table-file proposal is taken later, the hasher edit is
its cost, not this design's.)

** 2.3 What each sentinel value means

- =#+keyspace: label= --- the key holds survey spellings. *Composes* with any
  other =label= keyspace for the same table name. This is the only value that
  permits a cross-level row-union.
- =#+keyspace: code= --- the key holds instrument codes. *Terminal*: never
  unions with anything; applied as a decode stage before any fold (§1.4). Its
  =Preferred Label= column must be total (checkable, §4.1).
- =#+keyspace: <name>= --- an escape hatch for a shared code namespace that
  genuinely /is/ shared (e.g. =ehcvm-2018=). Two tables compose iff they
  declare the same name. Deliberately available and deliberately not used by
  default; no corpus instance is proposed.
- =#+fold: spelling= --- the curator asserts every many-to-one fold in this
  table is a spelling variant. *Machine-verifiable* (§1.5), so this is a
  cheap, falsifiable claim, not a rubber stamp. *Opt-in, never the default*:
  making =spelling= the default would fail 632 folds on the day it lands, which
  is open decision 3, not a table cell.
- =#+fold: asserted= --- the curator asserts the folds are commensurable on
  grounds a machine cannot check. Requires a =#+column: Notes = meta= column
  populated for each non-spelling fold, on the =absent_verdicts.csv= precedent
  ("an unevidenced negative is unfalsifiable"). This is where #833's five
  countries land.
- =#+fold: none= --- this table's values are never summed or unit-converted,
  so commensurability is not a question (e.g. =relationship=, =region=).

* 3. Resolution under the model

For a table name =T= and an axis value =raw=, in country =C=, wave =W=:

1. *Collect.* Gather every declaration of =T= at the three levels --- wave
   =W/_/categorical_mapping.org=, country =C/_/categorical_mapping.org=, global
   =lsms_library/categorical_mapping/*.org=. Read each one's sentinels.
2. *Partition by stage.* Tables with =keyspace: code= are *decode* tables;
   tables with =keyspace: label= are *fold* tables. A level may declare at most
   one of each for a given name (two of the same stage at one level is an
   error, per #953's comment 2 §3).
3. *Decode (at most one fires).* Apply the *lowest* level's decode table ---
   wave beats country; the global level holds no decode table. Whole-table
   replace: a decode is the instrument's own codebook and a lower level's
   codebook supersedes, it does not extend. *Totality is required* (§4.1); a
   miss is reported, never silently passed through.
4. *Fold (all levels compose).* Row-union the label-keyed tables, *lower level
   wins on key collision*, non-key columns outer-joined --- =_row_union_categorical=
   (2026-06, :113-117), with two changes, now applied to /every/ table whose
   sentinels license it rather than to four hardcoded names. Global is the base
   layer; country overrides it; wave overrides country.

   - *The join key comes from =#+key:=, not from position, and the
     =gk != ck= fallback (=country.py:372=) is replaced by a keyspace check.*
     "Exactly =_row_union_categorical=" would not do: it unions only when the
     two key column /names/ are equal, so Mali's =Code= column of labels could
     never meet the global =Original Label=. Under the model the two sides join
     on their respective declared keys --- renaming one on the way in --- and
     the union is attempted iff both declare =keyspace: label=. A code-keyed
     side is not a fallback; it is scheduled at step 3.
   - *The wave rung changes behaviour.* =Wave.categorical_mapping=
     (=country.py:1160-1168=) is a plain =dict.update= at =:1167=, i.e.
     whole-table override for *every* name, including the four additive ones.
     Under the model a wave's =keyspace: label= table row-unions over the
     country's instead of replacing it. *Blast radius is GhanaLSS alone* --- it
     is the only country in the corpus with a table declared at two levels
     (§7 open decision 1) --- and the same four tables are implicated in §1.3b,
     which is why those two questions are one decision.
5. *Emit.* The =canonical= column (=Preferred Label=) is the served value,
   unless a =labels== kwarg selects a =#+column: ... = label= alternate.

*A code-keyed table meeting a label-keyed one* is therefore never a merge
conflict and never a silent fallback. They are *different stages*. Malawi's
=Code=-keyed =u= decodes =01 -> Kilogramme=; the global label-keyed =u.org=
then folds =Kilogramme -> Kg=. Nothing about Malawi's rows changes; the
=_row_union_categorical= "key columns don't agree -> return country table"
branch (=country.py:372=) stops being reachable for that pair, because the
pair is no longer asked to union.

*The template already exists.* =Country._unit_kg_factors= (=country.py:2013ff=)
already composes two stages --- raw label -> =Preferred Label= via =u=, then
=Preferred Label= -> kilograms via =u_kg= --- and its docstring states the
constraint the decode stage must respect: =conversion_to_kgs= sees the *raw*
=u=, because the derived-food dispatch runs before
=_apply_categorical_mappings= (GH #770). So the decode stage must be available
to =_unit_kg_factors=, not only to the read-time mapper.

*Cache cost of the two-stage read: zero.* =_finalize_result= and
=_apply_categorical_mappings= are read-path; =_finalize_result= is already in
=_build_registry._EXCLUDED_CALLABLES=. Adding a stage there moves no
fingerprint. The cost is entirely in *editing the files* (§7).

** 3.1 The one case where the positional key is provably wrong

GhanaLSS's country =harmonize_food= has columns

: Preferred Label | Aggregate Label | 1987-88 | ... | 2016-17 | FCT Code

so "first column that is not =Preferred Label=" resolves the key to *=Aggregate
Label=* --- an *output* column. The real keys are the seven per-wave columns,
each naming that wave's raw label; the eight wave-level =harmonize_food= tables
have the same shape. Nothing breaks today only because every consumer of this
table names its columns explicitly (=glss_prices.py:116=,
=country._relabel_j=). #928 measured "live tables with a non-conventional
positional key: 0" and that remains true; this is the demonstration that
*positional is not the same as correct*, it is the same as *unused*.

* 4. What becomes checkable, and what each check costs

Today none of these can be asked of a file, because the file states none of it.

** 4.1 Totality --- =keyspace: code= implies every code reaches a label

Two checks, both already written as one-off sweeps for this note:

- /Internal/ --- no blank / NaN / numeric =Preferred Label= in a code-keyed
  table. Pure config read, *no data*. Cost: the full 312-table parse ran in
  well under a second. Current failures: *8 tables, 153 rows* (§1.4).
- /External/ --- every code the source emits appears in the table. Needs one
  warm parquet per (country, axis). Cost: the 19-country =food_acquired= sweep
  above is a parquet index-level read, seconds. This is the reincarnation of
  =diagnostics.food_acquired_u_code_leaks= (=diagnostics.py:611=), which
  already exists and is not wired to any gate; the sentinel is what tells a
  generic checker /which/ tables to run it on. Current failures: *4 of 12
  countries, 6,084 rows*.

** 4.2 Key compatibility --- two levels that claim to compose really do

Today =_row_union_categorical= compares key column *names* and falls back
silently when they differ. That comparison is both too strict and too loose.

- *Too strict*: Mali's =u= key column is headed =Code= and contains *labels*
  (=Avec os au Kg=, =Kilogramme=, =Bidon=). It could compose with the global
  label-keyed =u.org= and does not, purely because of the heading. Same for
  Mali's =harmonize_food=, =harmonize_input=, =harmonize_labor_source=,
  =harmonize_industry= --- *5 tables in one file*.
- *Too loose*: Azerbaijan's and Guyana's =harmonize_education= key columns are
  headed =Original Label= and contain *codes*. The names agree, so the union
  /fires/ --- silently unioning a code namespace with the global label
  namespace. No collision is possible, so nothing warns, and the 37 global
  rows can never match those two countries' data. *This is why the sentinel
  must carry the key's TYPE, not only its NAME: #928's =#+key:= alone does not
  catch it.*

Under the model the check is: a union is attempted iff both sides declare
=keyspace: label=; otherwise the code-keyed side is scheduled as a decode. A
declared =keyspace= that disagrees with the content classifier is a hard
error. Cost: config-only, the same sub-second parse. Current disagreements:
*7 genuine* (Mali x5, Azerbaijan, Guyana).

** 4.3 Commensurability --- =#+fold: spelling= is falsifiable

Run the normaliser over every many-to-one fold. Cost: config-only, sub-second.
Today it would pass 202 folds and *refuse 632*, which is the review queue, not
a failure of the check. The two magnitude-conflicting folds (§1.5) are refused
by a second, stricter regex check that a curator cannot talk past. #833's
instances become a bounded, enumerable backlog with a place in the file to
record the decision.

** 4.4 What is NOT checkable and should not be claimed

Whether =Groundnut= should include =Boiled groundnuts= (#833, Malawi) is a
judgement about the instrument. The design gives it a *place to be recorded and
a name to be cited*; it does not adjudicate it. Do not build a checker that
pretends to.

* 5. Migration, measured

** 5.1 Column headings that must change

*7 headings, in 3 files.* That is the whole of the "composable columns must be
label-keyed" migration:

| file | tables | change |
|------+--------+--------|
| =Mali/_/categorical_mapping.org= | =u=, =harmonize_food=, =harmonize_input=, =harmonize_labor_source=, =harmonize_industry= | heading =Code= -> =Original Label= (contents are already labels) |
| =Azerbaijan/_/categorical_mapping.org= | =harmonize_education= | heading =Original Label= -> =Code= (contents are codes) |
| =Guyana/_/categorical_mapping.org= | =harmonize_education= | same |

The sweep reports 29 tables whose key heading disagrees with its content; *22
of those are a single stray code row in an otherwise label-keyed table* ---
Niger =harmonize_assets= =166 -> Other=, GhanaSPS =AreaUnit= =-999 -> Other=,
Senegal =u= =1.0 -> NA=, Uganda =harmonize_education= four codes -> =Unknown=,
the EHCVM =harmonize_input= / =harmonize_seed_crop= family. *Those are totality
gaps to decode (§4.1), not headings to rename.* Do not report 29.

Note also what is *not* on this list: the 122 per-wave key columns of §1.3b
need no rename --- their headings are wave labels and must stay that way. What
they need is a key spelling that can name them.

Strictly, even the 7 are optional: =#+keyspace:= alone makes the composition
correct without touching a heading. They are recommended because a column
called =Code= holding labels will mislead the next reader, and because the
rename is a two-minute text edit per file.

** 5.2 Sentinel adoption

*47 files, 312 tables.* If every table declares its sentinels: ~312 =#+key:= +
~312 =#+keyspace:= lines, plus =#+fold:= on the ~40 tables on the =u= / =j=
axes and =#+column:= only for the 25 =extra= and 52 =presence= headings that
want documenting. But adoption is *incremental by design*: the inferred
defaults are correct for 290 of 312 tables (the 22 mixed-key tables are the
exceptions), so a staged migration can declare sentinels only where the
inference is wrong or the claim is load-bearing --- the 12 =u= tables, the ~27
=harmonize_food= / =harmonize_crop= tables, and the 8 totality violators ---
which is *~47 tables in ~20 files*, not 312. Add the *28 per-wave-key tables*
of §1.3b if =#+key: {wave}= is adopted --- those cannot be inferred and must be
declared; they sit in 14 countries, so that is 14 country re-warms.

** 5.3 The cache bill, stated

- Editing *any* global =.org= = *full-corpus cold re-warm*. =gorg:<name>.org=
  is in every wave's =_input_hash= for every country (=country.py:943-947=),
  and =Wave.grab_data= folds =build_transforms_fingerprint=, so both L2 tiers
  invalidate. Do the global edits *in one commit*.
- Editing a country's =categorical_mapping.org= = *that country* re-warms.
  Measured precedent: a prose-only GhanaLSS edit moved 17 of 119 table hashes
  (#928). Batch per country; do not dribble.
- =#+keyspace:= / =#+fold:= / =#+column:= lines are *inert to behaviour* until
  the resolver reads them, but they are *not* inert to the hash, because the
  whole file is hashed. So the sentinel-adoption pass and the resolver change
  should land together, per country, or the re-warm is paid twice.

** 5.4 The tool

=util/orgtbl.py:65= =add_or_update_column= writes a complete map into a named
column and preserves alignment. It is the right tool for the parts of a
migration that /add/ a column. It *cannot rename a heading* and its
=source_column= default is itself positional, so §5.1's seven renames are
either a hand edit (3 files) or a new =rename_column= sibling. Say which before
starting; do not discover it mid-pass.

* 6. What to remove

- *=_ADDITIVE_CATEGORICAL_TABLES= dissolves.* Verified: its only code reader is
  =country.py:391= in =_merge_categorical_tables= (every other hit is a
  docstring, a prose comment in a country =.org=, or a test). Under the model
  the merge rule is derived --- a =keyspace: label= pair row-unions, a
  =keyspace: code= table is a decode --- so the four-name allow-list has
  nothing left to decide. Its *intent* survives verbatim (2026-06 :118: "small,
  explicit, auditable"); what changes is that the declaration moves from a
  frozenset in =country.py= to the file it describes, which is also what makes
  a fifth table possible without a framework edit. =tests/test_categorical_merge.py=
  and =tests/test_gh919_country_qualified_units.py= import the name and would
  be rewritten against the sentinels.
- *=_categorical_key_column= becomes a fallback*, not the rule: #928's P2
  resolver order (explicit =idxvars== -> =#+key:= -> positional -> =None=),
  with the two extra sites found here (=local_tools.py:1539=,
  =util/orgtbl.py:65=) folded in --- six sites, one resolver. =get_categorical_mapping='s =idxvars='Code'=
  default is the one that should go: 63 of 71 call sites under =countries/=
  already pass =idxvars== explicitly, so the default serves 8 calls and
  contradicts the other five sites' rule.
- *The cascade's third rung retires* --- #953 option 3. Its own correction
  comment already makes this the strongest option: mechanism 2 reaches the
  global level for every table and has since #168, so teaching mechanism 1 a
  directory would build a second path to the same six files with a different
  merge rule. Under this design the global level's semantics are declared in
  the files, and a build-time reader that ignored those declarations would be
  a third semantics. Retire =dirs[2]= and raise a message that names mechanism 2.
- *Not removed, and a prerequisite rather than part of this design*: #693
  (=all_dfs_from_orgfile= must skip =#+begin_...= blocks). Note the corpus
  instance was closed *this morning* by commit =07c9e697d=, which comma-escaped
  the two illustrative =#+name:= lines in =canonical_housing_labels.org= ---
  i.e. #693's fallback option (b), de-fang the file. *The parser bug itself is
  still live*: re-verified here on a synthetic file, a =#+name:= table inside
  =#+begin_example= parses to a live table. Any sentinel scheme that reads
  keywords above a table inherits it --- and worse, it inherits it at a
  keyword, so a =#+key:= line inside an example block would be read as
  configuration for whatever table follows the block. Option (a) becomes a
  prerequisite rather than a preference.

* 7. Open decisions for @ligon

1. *Does the wave rung extend or replace --- and can a key be wave-templated?
   (One question, see §1.3b and §3 step 4.)* Options: (a)
   whole-table replace, lowest level wins --- what §3 proposes, on the ground
   that a codebook is the instrument's and a wave's instrument supersedes the
   country's summary; (b) row-union like the fold stage. Consequence: only
   GhanaLSS is affected --- it is the *only* country with any table declared at
   two levels (=harmonize_food= x8, =harmonize_education= x4, =region= x5,
   =relationship= x3). Under (a) GhanaLSS 1991-92's =Code=-keyed =relationship=
   fully replaces the country's =Original Label=-keyed one, which is what
   happens today. Under (b) they would have to be reconciled first.
2. *Is =#+keyspace:= inferred-by-default or required?* Inferred is byte-compatible
   and adoptable in 20 files (§5.2); required is honest but is a 312-table edit
   and a full-corpus re-warm. Recommend inferred-with-a-hard-error-on-disagreement,
   and required only for tables on the =u= / =j= axes.
3. *=#+fold: asserted= without =Notes= --- warn or refuse?* The
   =absent_verdicts.csv= precedent refuses. Refusing turns 632 folds into 632
   blockers on the day it lands, so the honest sequencing is: refuse for the
   =u= / =j= axes only, warn elsewhere. Needs a decision, because "warn
   everywhere" is how #323 survived its first fix.
4. *Do the 7 heading renames happen?* They are optional under
   =#+keyspace:= (§5.1). Renaming costs 3 files and 3 country re-warms and
   makes the files legible; not renaming leaves a column called =Code= holding
   =Kilogramme= forever, documented by a keyword two lines above it.
5. *Should the global level be permitted a code-keyed table at all?*
   =ehcvm_units.org= is one today, consumed only by explicit =mappings:= list
   forms in three wave =data_info.yml= files. Under §1.2 the global level holds
   no decode table; either =ehcvm_units= is exempted as "not part of the
   cascade" or it moves. Cheap either way; decide rather than inherit.
6. *Where does =#+fold:= live for a table that exists at two levels?* Per-level
   (each level asserts its own folds) or per-name (one assertion governs the
   composed table)? Per-level is simpler and is what §3 assumes; per-name is
   what a user of the composed result actually needs.

* 8. Deliberately left out

- *Adjudicating #833.* This design gives the =j=-axis folds a declaration and a
  check; it does not decide whether Uganda's =Maize= Aggregate should split.
  The 455 =j=-axis distinct-label folds are a queue, not a bug list.
- *#928's P4* (the =pd.NA= ignorance collapse). Orthogonal, and its own
  warning --- collapsing =Unknown= to =pd.NA= on a declared index level
  re-creates #842/#847 --- stands untouched.
- *Cross-country label harmonization.* Two countries' =Preferred Label= strings
  matching does not make their categories equal
  (=SkunkWorks/cross_country_label_harmonization.org=;
  =semantic_curation_proposal.org= :49). =#+keyspace:= is about a /key/
  namespace, never an output one. Nothing here licenses pooling Malawi's
  =Groundnut= with Nigeria's.
- *#953's file-layout question* (one file per level vs. one file per table,
  =categorical_mapping/= vs =.d/=). The sentinels are layout-independent by
  construction --- they sit above a =#+name:= table wherever it lives --- so
  this design neither needs nor blocks that choice. Its cost (three hasher
  edits at =country.py:943=, =:948=, =:3565=) belongs to that issue.
- *A migration of the 32 =Code= \vert =Label= terminal decodes to
  =Preferred Label=.* Explicitly rejected: =_build_replace_dict=
  (=country.py:2880=) dispatches by table *name*, case-insensitively, so giving
  GhanaLSS 1991-92's =#+name: rural=, =#+name: units= or =#+name: relationship=
  a =Preferred Label= column would auto-apply them to the =Rural= / =u= /
  =Relationship= columns with no other edit. Leaving them as =Label= and
  declaring =#+column: Label = canonical= is *safer than renaming*, and that is
  an argument for the sentinel rather than a limitation of it.
- *Performance.* Every check proposed is a config parse (sub-second over 312
  tables) or a parquet index read; none was measured as needing more.
