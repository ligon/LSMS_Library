#+TITLE: GhanaLSS GLSS1/GLSS2 Section 9: what is there, and what wiring it would take
#+AUTHOR: Sue
#+DATE: <2026-09-16 Wed>

* Why this exists

The GLSS1 -> GLSS2 rotating panel (712 households, 1987-88 <-> 1988-89) is the
only *wired* panel in GhanaLSS, and GhanaLSS declares no =crop_production=, so
=CANDIDATES.org= ruled it out for an agricultural production regression.  This
note asks whether that is a data absence or a wiring gap.

*It is a wiring gap.*  Both panel waves ship the complete Section 9
(Agro-Pastoral Activities) suite -- 22 files each, output, land, labour,
purchased inputs and capital -- and none of it is declared.

For a Mundlak-style specification at *farm* grain (@ligon, 2026-09-16: "we can
get by with farm-level expenses and revenues, don't need plot level") this is
enough.  It is *not* enough for anything plot-level, and that is not a defect
to fix -- see the standing verdict in [[*What must NOT be done][What must NOT be done]].

* The form, read

The GLSS1/GLSS2 questionnaires are image-only CCITT-fax scans with no text
layer.  They were read with the repo's existing renderer,
=slurm_logs/ghana_audit/glss2_questionnaire/render_pages.py=, whose
=PAGE_INDEX.org= locates Section 9 at PDF pp.42-55.  Part B is p.44-45, Part D
p.47-51.

** Part B (Crops), p.44 -- the column-to-variable map

| Q     | question                                                    | variable            |
|-------+-------------------------------------------------------------+---------------------|
|     1 | grown in past 12 months? (cross per crop)                   | row selector        |
|     2 | how many *acres* were harvested                             | =CROPH=             |
|     3 | how many acres not yet in production                        | =CROPNC=            |
|     4 | how much was *sold* (quantity, unit)                        | =CROPS=, =CROPSU=   |
|     5 | if unstarred unit: how many *kilograms per unit*            | =CROPSUK=           |
|     6 | how much was the harvest sold *for* (amount)                | =CROPSP=            |
|   7-8 | kept part for *seed*? / market value of that quantity       | =CROPK=, =CROPKV=   |
|  9-10 | *gave* part to labourers/landowner/gifts? / its value       | =CROPP=, =CROPPV=   |
| 11-12 | *lost* part to insects/rodents/fire/rot? / its value        | =CROPL=, =CROPLV=   |
| 13-14 | intercropped? / which crops                                 | =CROPD=, =CROPD1/2= |

*** =CROPH= is ACRES, not a harvest quantity

This is the single most important finding in this note, and it is the opposite
of what the variable name suggests.  Read as a harvest quantity it would be
wrong by construction and silently so -- 0.2 for a cocoa row is 0.2 acres.

*** There is NO total-harvest quantity anywhere in Part B

The form asks quantity *sold* (Q4) and never quantity *harvested*.  So output
at this grain cannot be a kilogram figure, and =transformations.harvest_kg=
and the whole kg ladder are simply not applicable.  That is a convenience as
much as a constraint: it sidesteps the unit problem that disqualified GhanaSPS.

*Output must therefore be a VALUE*, summing the four disposition values the
form does ask for:

: GVP_partial = CROPSP (sold) + CROPKV (seed) + CROPPV (given) + CROPLV (lost)

*** ...and that sum omits own consumption, which is usually the largest use

The form never asks what the household ate.  Taking the sum above as gross
value of production would understate smallholder output badly and
*differentially* -- households that sell more would look more productive purely
through the disposition margin.

The repo already has the missing piece, for exactly these two waves:
=derive_12b_fortnight_value= (=GhanaLSS/_/ghanalss.py:532=) mints an
=Expenditure= for =s='produced'= rows in GLSS1/GLSS2 from Section 12B, is
registered as =GhanaLSS::food_acquired::12b-fortnight= in =_/derivations.yml=,
and is tested in =tests/test_ghanalss_12b.py=.  See
=.coder/ledger/ghanalss-produced-qp.md= and =585-own-production-valuation.md=
for the valuation machinery generally.

*Open design question, not resolved here*: Section 9 is an annual recall and
Section 12B is a fortnight annualised.  Adding them is a recall-window splice,
and =attrs['recall']= exists precisely to disclose that sort of thing.  Whether
to splice, or to use Section 9 dispositions alone and state the omission, is @ligon's
call.

** Part D (Farm Inputs), p.47 -- expenses, per crop

Seeds (Q1-6), fertilizer (Q7-13) and manure (Q14-19) each follow the same
shape: a crop list with codes, then quantity (fertilizer and manure only, with
a unit code) and *amount spent*.  Chemicals, transport, containers and storage
repeat it on pp.48-51.  All are keyed by crop code, so they aggregate to the
farm by summing -- which is the grain wanted.

*Limitation to record*: each input's crop list is capped at *four* lines
(=01..04=).  A household cultivating more than four crops has its input spend
truncated, and the truncation is invisible in the data.

* What would have to be built

Nothing is declared today: =crop_production=, =plot_inputs=, =plot_labor= and
=livestock= are all =undeclared= for GhanaLSS, and =plot_features= is wired for
GLSS3-GLSS7 only.

The precedent for a plot-less country is *EthiopiaRHS*, which declares

: crop_production:
:   index: (t, i, j, u)

with a comment recording that =u= is degenerate but kept because the canonical
shape carries it, and which puts area on the crop row as =Area_ha= (GH #512,
"documented household-grain coarse table").  GhanaLSS's Part B has exactly that
shape, with acres instead of hectares.

* What must NOT be done

=GhanaLSS/plot_features= for 1987-88 and 1988-89 carries a *closing*
=not-asked= verdict in =.coder/coverage/absent_verdicts.csv=, C4-validated
against the form itself (there is no farm-number column and no per-farm roster
part anywhere in Section 9).  A synthetic single-=plot_id= row would contradict
a permanent, evidenced record.

=CONTENTS.org= adds the specific trap: *do not read =Y09A:IDFARM= as a plot
identifier.*  =Y09A= has exactly one row per household; =IDFARM= is the Section
7 field recording *who answers* the agriculture module.

* Cost, stated before anyone starts

Adding any table to =GhanaLSS/_/data_scheme.yml= moves *every* GhanaLSS table's
cache hash (=Country._table_cache_hash= hashes that file whole), so the bill is
a whole-country cold re-warm including the 5.26M-row =food_acquired=.  Budget
it; do not trigger it casually while exploring.  Use
=LSMS_COUNTRIES_ROOT=<worktree>/lsms_library/countries= to test config under
development against the live package without touching the shared tree.

* Open questions for @ligon

1. *Output measure* -- Section 9 dispositions alone (sold + seed + gifts + losses), or
   spliced with the Section 12B own-consumption value?  The second is more nearly GVP
   and crosses a recall window.
2. *Scope* -- wire only the two panel waves, or attempt GLSS3+ too?  GLSS3+
   have a genuine per-plot roster, so one country table would mix plot-grain
   and household-grain waves and put NaN on a declared index level for GLSS1/2
   (Site I territory; =groupby= then deletes those rows).
3. *Table shape* -- one =crop_production= on the EthiopiaRHS pattern carrying
   acres and the four disposition values, plus a separate farm-level expense
   table?  Or a single wide farm-level table, given that Mundlak wants farm
   aggregates anyway?
4. *Land* -- =Y09A.ACRFRM= (household total) and =Y09B.CROPH= (per crop) are
   both available and should roughly agree.  Which is authoritative, and is the
   discrepancy worth carrying as a check?
