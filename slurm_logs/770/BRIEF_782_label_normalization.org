*Cause*, at =2005-06/_/food_acquired.py:80=:

categorical conversion.  *Verify by CONTENT, not by shape*: confirm =foodcd='s
dtype and a sample of its values before choosing.  The purchased side works ---
understand why before touching it, and do not "fix" it.
