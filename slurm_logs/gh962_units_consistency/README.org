#+title: GH #962 against the corpus's unit conventions
#+date: [2026-09-24]

* Question
Is the second #962 attempt (uncommitted on =development=, job 39195255; the first attempt is =origin/fix/962-uganda-unit-sentinel=) consistent with how the rest of the corpus treats a row with no recorded unit?

* The corpus has two sentinels with two meanings
- =Value= :: money recorded, no physical quantity or unit.
  Framework-reserved (=country._RESERVED_U_SENTINELS=, =transformations._CURRENCY_DENOMINATED_UNITS=); excluded from the kg inference by design (GH #770).
  Convention (=.claude/skills/add-feature/food-acquired/SKILL.md= "LCU-only goods"): =Quantity = Expenditure=.
  Written by GhanaLSS (6 waves), Panama (3 waves), EthiopiaRHS (code 30), Serbia (=dinar=).
  The =Quantity = Expenditure= half is load-bearing: =food_quantities_from_acquired= does =replace(0, nan).dropna()= on =Quantity= before anything else, so a =Value= row with NaN =Quantity= is dropped from =food_quantities= in every mode.
- =Unknown= :: a quantity recorded, no unit recorded.
  =transformations.U_UNKNOWN=; recognised by =_unit_sentinel_mask= and refused by every rung of the kg ladder.
  Written by Niger (=U_NA=), Burkina Faso 2014 (=U_NA=), Ethiopia, GhanaSPS crop, Uganda crop_production; declared in =data_info.yml= =missing_level_sentinels= for crop_production.
  Consumer rule (=niger.py:494=): not a unit; exclude and count.

Everything else that reaches =u= is a real unit and gets a kg minted by the price-ratio inference.
Recognition is a spelling allowlist that fails open -- the issue's own diagnosis.

* Verdict
The draft is consistent on the =Value= label and inconsistent on two points:
1. It leaves =Quantity= NaN on =Value= rows, against the LCU-only convention; the 6,429 rows then vanish from =food_quantities= instead of being carried with =u='Value'= as GhanaLSS's 3.55M and Panama's 17,821 are.
2. It relabels the 930 quantity-without-unit rows to a REAL Uganda unit, =Number of Units (General)=, rather than =Unknown=.
   Every other country writes =Unknown= for that case, and the effect is measurable: all 930 get a minted kilogram again (915 on the u-pooled rung, now pooled with the genuine count rows and doubled to 0.137 kg).
   That is the fabrication #962 is about, under a label that asserts the survey said "count" when it recorded nothing; the issue thread already notes that "a raw cigarette count is not a kilogram either".
   The draft's own test file agrees: its xfail reason records the agreed shape as "a 'Value' unit for the value-denominated items, then U_NA for code 0 only".
3. It also does not touch =unitlabels[int_k] = '---'= (the 143 blank-label codes), so ='---'= can still reach =u= and the xfail stays xfail.

Consistent shape: exp-only -> =Value= with =Quantity = Expenditure=; quantity-without-unit -> =Unknown=; the 143 blank-label codes are a separate question (134 are curated physical units whose Preferred Label is blank -- promote the label, do not sentinel it), and PR #963 lands first because promoting them exposes the fraction parser.
Framework follow-up worth its own issue: extend =missing_level_sentinels= (or a build-time fill in the wave builder) to =food_acquired= so the fill is not re-implemented per country, and a corpus test that every served =u= is a decoded label, a metric-parsable label, or a recognised sentinel -- Nigeria's =None= and Cambodia's ='' = are the next two instances.
