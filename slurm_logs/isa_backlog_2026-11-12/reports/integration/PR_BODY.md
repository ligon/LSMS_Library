# ISA batch 2026-11-12: six reviewed branches, one data gate

This PR assembles six independently reviewed and red-teamed branches into one integration branch, `integration/isa-batch-20261112`. That follows the maintainer ruling "Wait and batch all (Recommended)" (2026-11-12, *Merge order*): one integration PR, one data gate. Each branch was verified against development `e5fdd2fa8` alone. This body adds what only the combined tree can show: the conflicts, the cross-branch effects, the test results and the hash footprint.

- **Head:** `446adc3c27b965a67fa32a9679bc2a2362619523`. **Base:** development `e5fdd2fa831c4d03a247d264be7f339da459e6b0`.
- **Combined effect, in one line:** on what was measured, the six-branch tree equals base plus the sum of the six branches' own effects, with no interaction. "What was measured" is:
  - row counts of `sample`, `food_acquired`, `food_prices()` (default `kgvalue`) and `food_quantities(units='kgs')`;
  - the `kgs` kilogram total;
  - `food_acquired` Expenditure;
  - over 11 countries, cold, in private roots, at five SHAs along the merge line (below).
- **Not measured on the combined tree:** the native-unit modes (`unitvalue`, `units`) on the four new waves.
- **Must state before merging:**
  - Niger 2018-19 served purchase Expenditure falls **12.6%** (53.35M to 46.64M FCFA) under the #1708 ruling.
  - Benin 2018-19 inferred kilograms rise **+11.1%** against base through EHCVM-2's wave pooling. EHCVM-2's final body shows only the round-to-round delta, 0, here.
  - The integrated tree needed three integration-only test commits:
    - the Uganda nutrition baseline (approved);
    - a `test_962` evidence entry, a #1604 defect;
    - a Malawi kg re-pin, also #1604.
  - **Gate status.** The data-gated selection and the full non-S3 suite are green on the six-branch tree.
    - A wider sweep found two `test_table_structure` failures on `Malawi/plot_inputs`. They fail identically on base, so they are pre-existing.
    - One load-induced `test_gh932` failure passes 3 times out of 3 in isolation.
    - `pr-data-validation` itself has not been run.

## Component 4: kg evidence from purchases; native-unit drops (#1509, #1068, #1129)

- **Purchases only.** `food_kg_factors`' price-ratio inference reads only `s == 'purchased'` rows, and every row is still served.
  - A `(j, u)` cell with no priced purchase in any wave falls back, in this order: `item_unit_nonpurchase`, then the pooled purchase `unit` rung, then `unit_nonpurchase`, then `none`. That order is the ruling "Item self-valued first".
  - A cell with any purchase evidence never reads a self-valued rung.
  - Both fallback rungs are disclosed in `kg_factor_sources`, per row, and in `attrs['kg_factor_evidence']['fallback']`.
- **"Make the dependency visible".** Uganda and Ethiopia `nutrition.py` import `food_quantities_from_acquired`, so `framework_imports_fingerprint` sees the kg ladder. Every later kg-factor edit re-warms Ethiopia, GhanaLSS and Uganda cold.
- **"Drop and count".** `food_prices(units='unitvalue')` drops `u='Value'` rows (the tautology 1.0) and missing-unit rows, counted in `price_rows_dropped['currency' / 'unknown']`. `food_quantities(units='units')` drops missing-unit rows into `attrs['quantity_rows_dropped']`.
  - **This is the component's largest change in rows.** GhanaLSS `unitvalue` goes from 1,676,514 to 568,130 rows, and 1987-88, 1988-89 and 1991-92 serve none.
  - Also in `unitvalue`: Uganda −7,329, Panama −7,185, Malawi −1,241. Served `Unknown` rows in `units` are now 0 everywhere measured.
  - These are the branch's measurements.
- **Must state** (branch-alone measurements, cold):
  - `food_quantities(kgs)`: GhanaLSS -7.25%, GhanaSPS +3.93%, Uganda -1.36%, Serbia -2.12%, Cambodia -1.08%. Ethiopia, Malawi, Panama, Burkina Faso, EthiopiaRHS and Niger are unchanged.
  - `nutrition`: GhanaLSS Energy -15.08%, Uganda -1.07%.
  - These are movements, not corrections.
  - Unit-rung eligibility is per `(j, u)` cell ("Keep per food x unit cell", 2026-11-12).
  - The attrs asymmetry is documented in this PR (`6a670ce83`): `currency` counts every expected input row, `unknown` only the rows that would otherwise be served.
