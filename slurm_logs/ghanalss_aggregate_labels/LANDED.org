unmapped label *raises* rather than passing through, because a silent
pass-through is the defect being replaced.

* Still open, for decision

1. *=Millet= absorbs guinea corn and sorghum in GLSS1/2.*  The raw label is
   =Millet, guinea corn or sorghua (grain or flour)= but the Preferred Label
   says only =Millet= -- the =Goat=/mutton pattern again.  The country table
   has separate =Guinea Corn= and =Sorghum= rows, neither of which is ever
   reached.  A compound name (=Millet/Guinea Corn/Sorghum=) would be honest;
   =Millet= alone is not.
2. *RESOLVED (@ligon, 2026-09-16): =Potato/Sweet Potato= folded into
   =Sweet Potato=.*  GLSS1/2 ask one question, =Potato or sweet potato=, so the
   compound label was the INSTRUMENT's conflation faithfully recorded, not ours
   (=Macaroni and Spaghetti= is the same shape and is untouched).  But a
   randomly drawn Ghanaian potato is far more likely to be a sweet potato than
   an Irish one, so the honest reading of that item is sweet potato: the ruling
   is a judgement about Ghana, not about the instrument, and it is recorded
   here as such rather than inferred from the label.

   Done at the COUNTRY level, which is what the crosswalk is for -- the 87/88
   wave tables still say =Potato/Sweet Potato=, and the country table's 1987-88
   and 1988-89 cells carry that spelling against Preferred Label
   =Sweet Potato=.  FCT follows the label: *02_022* (sweet potato, pale flesh)
   rather than 02_009 (=Potato, raw=).

   Measured: =Sweet Potato= 1,759 -> *2,202* rows (= 1,759 + the 443 formerly
   under =Potato/Sweet Potato=), now spanning *all seven waves*, and it gains a
   purchase series it did not have -- 91-16 are entirely own-production
   (1,982), the 87/88 rows are purchased (220).  =Potato= keeps 02_009 and
   stays 2016-17-only (484 rows), which is the genuine Irish-potato item.
   Country Preferred Labels 215 -> 214; bijection, 0 off-axis, 0 duplicates and
   Expenditure per wave all still hold; 32 tests pass.
3. =557 'Country milk'= left under =Cocoa (milk powder beverages)=: n=52, unit
   =Single=, median 5.50 against =Other milk= 3.00 -- suggestive of milk but
   not decisive.  Documented unsure rather than moved.
4. =Margarine= absorbs =Butter, margarine= / =margarine/butter= in several
