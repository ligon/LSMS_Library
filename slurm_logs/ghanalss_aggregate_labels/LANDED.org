* Still open, for decision

1. *RESOLVED (@ligon, 2026-09-16): GLSS1/2's conflated cereal is
   =Millet/Sorghum=.*  The raw label is =Millet, guinea corn or sorghua (grain
   or flour)= and the Preferred Label said only =Millet= -- the =Goat=/mutton
   pattern.  Confirmed it is an EARLY-ROUNDS-ONLY item: from GLSS3 the
   instrument asks =Millet=, =Millet flour= and =Guinea corn/sorghum=
   separately, so =Millet= silently changed meaning at GLSS3 for anyone using
   it as a panel.

   Named =Millet/Sorghum= rather than a three-way compound because *guinea corn
   and sorghum are the same crop* (@ligon), and millet is a close consumption
   substitute the questionnaire designers evidently grouped with it.  Done at
   the WAVE level (@ligon) -- both 87/88 tables now say =Millet/Sorghum= -- with
   a matching country row.

   Measured: =Millet/Sorghum= *884 rows* (453 + 431), 87/88 only, 721 produced
   + 163 purchased; =Millet= is now *13,094 rows over 91-16 only* and means
   millet in every wave it appears.  FCT kept at =01_095= (pearl millet):
   sorghum is =01_039= and the two are close (370 vs 345 kcal, 8.3 vs 11.5 g
   protein), so carrying millet's values for the mixture beats blanking it and
   dropping 884 rows out of =nutrition= entirely.

   *The GLSS1/2 PRICE survey is finer than its expenditure module* and prices
   millet separately (item 4).  Since the 87/88 food axis no longer names plain
   millet, that item is re-flagged =Food: own= -- which per =CONTENTS.org:3357=
   means exactly "the axis does not name this food", and is now a true
   statement -- mirroring item 3 (=Guinea Corn=), which the original curator had
   already flagged =own= for the same reason.
2. *RESOLVED (@ligon, 2026-09-16): =Potato/Sweet Potato= folded into
   =Sweet Potato=.*  GLSS1/2 ask one question, =Potato or sweet potato=, so the
   compound label was the INSTRUMENT's conflation faithfully recorded, not ours
   (=Macaroni and Spaghetti= is the same shape and is untouched).  But a
   randomly drawn Ghanaian potato is far more likely to be a sweet potato than
   an Irish one, so the honest reading of that item is sweet potato: the ruling
   is a judgement about Ghana, not about the instrument, and it is recorded
   here as such rather than inferred from the label.

   Done at the COUNTRY level, which is what the crosswalk is for -- the 87/88
   wave tables still say =Potato/Sweet Potato=, and the country table's 1987-88
   and 1988-89 cells carry that spelling against Preferred Label
   =Sweet Potato=.  FCT follows the label: *02_022* (sweet potato, pale flesh)
   rather than 02_009 (=Potato, raw=).

   Measured: =Sweet Potato= 1,759 -> *2,202* rows (= 1,759 + the 443 formerly
   under =Potato/Sweet Potato=), now spanning *all seven waves*, and it gains a
   purchase series it did not have -- 91-16 are entirely own-production
   (1,982), the 87/88 rows are purchased (220).  =Potato= keeps 02_009 and
   stays 2016-17-only (484 rows), which is the genuine Irish-potato item.
   Country Preferred Labels 215 -> 214; bijection, 0 off-axis, 0 duplicates and
   Expenditure per wave all still hold; 32 tests pass.
3. *RESOLVED (@ligon, 2026-09-16): =557 'Country milk'= -> =Milk (fresh)=.*
   Ghana's "Country milk" (branded Countre Dairy) is a bottled, shelf-stable
   UHT milk drink imported from Belgium -- so it is liquid milk, not a cocoa
   beverage.  =Milk (fresh)= is the liquid-milk label on the 2016-17 axis and
   carries FCT *10_029* ("Milk, cow, whole, raw, 4.5% fat"), which is what UHT
   milk is nutritionally.  Note =Other Milk Products= would have been wrong:
   its FCT is 10_028, *cheese*.  Delivered: =Milk (fresh)= now 7,270 rows
   including 746 in 2016-17.

   This is the fact the price probe could not supply -- n=52 at unit =Single=
   was suggestive of milk but never decisive, and it was correctly left
   =unsure= rather than guessed.

   conflates them, =Margarine= where it does not.*  Unlike potato and millet
   this is *non-monotonic* -- the instrument went back and forth, so no
   "early rounds are coarser" rule works and it had to be settled per wave:

   | wave | raw label | margarine alone? | rows |

   | 91-92, 98-99 | =Margarine= | *yes* | 577, 835 |
   | 05-06, 12-13 | =margarine/butter= | no | 1,114, 1,887 |
   | 16-17 | =Margarine= | *yes* | 353 |

   91/98 corroborate themselves: those waves put dairy butter explicitly
   elsewhere, in =Other milk products (Including butter & cheese)= (code 70),
   so code 33 =Margarine= is not quietly carrying it.  Delivered:
   =Margarine= *1,765* rows (91/98/16), =Margarine/Butter= *3,493*
   (87/88/05/12).  FCT stays *11_006*: margarine 728 kcal / 937 ug vitamin A
   against butter 739 / 838, close on everything.

6. *NEW, found while checking margarine: GLSS1/2 also fold SHEA BUTTER into
   PALM OIL.*  Code 329 is =Palm oil and shea butter= -> =Oil (palm)=; from
   GLSS3 =Shea Butter= is its own item and a large one (*36,058* rows against
   =Oil (palm)='s 88,800).  Same meaning-shift, bigger item, and north/south
   consumption differs sharply.  Split as =Oil (palm)/Shea Butter= (87/88,
   *4,239* rows); =Oil (palm)= is now clean over 91-16 (*88,800*).

   *Its FCT is deliberately BLANK, unlike the other composites.*  Energy would
   have justified keeping palm oil's 11_004 (898 vs shea butter's 900 kcal),
   but *vitamin A is 5,720 ug against 0* -- and vitamin A is precisely what
   this food group is read for in Ghana.  Carrying 11_004 would assert 5,720
   for a mixture roughly a quarter of which has none, invisibly; the repo's
   own rule for Vitamin K applies ("omitted, NOT zero-filled -- a zero would
   assert these foods contain none").  Cost: those 4,239 rows drop out of
   =nutrition=.  Reversible if someone would rather have approximate energy
   than no row.

7. *The GLSS1/2 and GLSS5/6 price surveys are again FINER than their
   expenditure modules*, as with millet.  Price items =Palm oil= (87/88,
   weighed, median 0.79 kg) and =margarine (canned, sachet)= (12-13) name the
   specific good, so they are re-flagged =Food: own= with a note -- true, since
   those axes no longer name it.  *Three separate times now the
   =test_price_items_share_the_food_vocabulary= guard has caught a rename
   before it shipped*; it is the most valuable test in this file.

5. *RESOLVED (@ligon, 2026-09-16): guinea corn + sorghum reconciled with a
   named reducer.*  In 91/98 ONLY, purchased and produced sorghum sat under
   DIFFERENT labels.  =Guinea corn/sorghum= is =Code_9b=1= (purchase side, =Code_8h=
   empty), while =Sorghum= (=Code_8h=4=) and =Guinea Corn= (=Code_8h=7=) are
   own-production.  From GLSS5 on the instrument puts =9b=1= and =8h=4= on ONE
   row, so the later waves already do it right.  Since guinea corn IS sorghum,
   those two waves serve one crop under three labels, split by module:
   purchased 765 rows, produced 5,948 + 207.

   *Not a free rename*: 115 colliding keys (80 in 1991-92, 35 in 1998-99) --
   households that filed both own-production lines -- 58 of them disagreeing on
   Price.  Resolved with =ghanalss.reduce_duplicate_food_rows= (Quantity SUMS,
   Price becomes the QUANTITY-WEIGHTED MEAN, the =Goat= precedent at
   =CONTENTS.org:3280=), called through =reconcile_after_crosswalk=.

   *WHERE the reduction happens was the whole design question, and the first
   answer was wrong.*  The merge was first done by renaming the two 8h
   Preferred Labels in the WAVE tables, which forced =Price= into the
   pre-unstack groupby key so two farmgate prices would not be summed -- and
   that restructure perturbed 1991-92's row count by *+637 for reasons having
   nothing to do with sorghum* (it also silently resurrected 262 rows with a NA
   =u= that the old =dropna=True= had dropped).  Backed out.

   The merge now happens at *Lcp*: the wave tables keep =Sorghum= and
   =Guinea Corn= as their own labels -- those really are two lines on that
   questionnaire and rewriting a round's vocabulary is not ours to do -- and
   the country table's per-wave columns map both onto Preferred Label
   =Guinea Corn/Sorghum=, which is exactly what those columns are for.  The
   reducer then runs once, after the crosswalk, on the canonical grain.  The
   wave pipeline is untouched.

   Measured against a rebuild of the TRUE prior state (not an assumed
   baseline): 1991-92 *682,627 -> 682,547*, exactly *-80*, matching the 80
   collisions measured in that wave; corpus *-100* rows total; Expenditure
   identical per wave in all seven, which is what the quantity-weighted mean
   guarantees (Q*P = sum(q*p)).

   A =min_count=1= bug in the first version of the reducer turned all-NA groups
   into recorded zeros and added *+32,040 phantom rows*; caught before commit
   by the row count, not by a test.
