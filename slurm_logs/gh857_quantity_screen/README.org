#+title: GH #857 -- the quantity plausibility screen: raw records
#+date: 2026-09-09

Everything here exists so the rule in =lsms_library/quantity_audit.py= is
*re-checkable rather than merely asserted*, the same way
=slurm_logs/null_read_guard/= backs =_NULL_FRACTION_TRIGGER=.

Both scripts are read-only and **glob** =data_root()= for every country holding
a warm L2-country =crop_production= parquet -- no hardcoded country list.  The
first version of =measure_firing.py= had one, and five countries were published
as though they were the corpus while this README already claimed a glob (red
team, 2026-09-09).  Neither script calls =Country()= or builds anything, and
each prints the =lsms_library.__file__= it imported so a worktree/main mix-up is
visible rather than silent (CLAUDE.md, scrum-master addendum 3).

| file | what it is |

| =sensitivity_sweep.py= | the K / quantile / floor sweep the rule was chosen from.  It calls the library's OWN =_cell_reference= and sweeps by monkeypatching the module constants, so it cannot drift from the rule it justifies. |
| =sensitivity_2026-09-09.txt= | its output. |
| =hash_probe.py=, =hash_before.json=, =hash_after.json= | the cache-invalidation measurement: 12 =Country._table_cache_hash= values and 5 =build_transforms_fingerprint= values.  =before= is the development tip =5bec5e79= (extracted read-only with =git archive=, so it has no Site Q); =after= is this branch.  *Byte-identical* -- Site Q costs no rebuild. Reproducing the exact numbers: the shared cache was being re-warmed by a
concurrent workstream throughout, so Uganda's parquet appeared and disappeared
several times.  The committed outputs were taken against a stable snapshot
(=LSMS_DATA_DIR= pointed at a copy of every warm =*/var/crop_production.parquet=,
Uganda taken post-#861).  Point =LSMS_DATA_DIR= at any such snapshot to repeat
them.

* Findings for filing (not fixed here)

** Niger 2011-12 ships a =999999= harvest sentinel -- eleven rows, unfixed

Exactly *11* rows of =Quantity == 999999=, all in 2011-12, all =u='Unknown'=:
Mil, Sorgho, Niébé/Haricots secs and Riz Paddy, households =13317= and =20607=.
They fire at *x14,285.7* on the =t= rung -- the largest ratio in the corpus --
and are 11 of Niger's 28 firings.

This is the same class as Uganda's =99999= (GH #861, now fixed at source), and
the contrast is the interesting part: Uganda keyed the sentinel 3,097 times, so
it *became the 90th percentile* of most cells it appeared in and only seven
rows ever fired; Niger keyed it eleven times, so it cannot saturate anything and
every one fires.  *Whether a distributional screen sees a sentinel is an
accident of how often it was keyed.*  Stripping it is a source-side job with its
own issue, exactly as #861 was.

** Four Uganda 2010-11 rows still fire against a reference of one unit

=u='Others specify'= pools every crop under one junk label; its p90 was 0.6,
which =QUANTITY_REFERENCE_FLOOR= lifts to 1.0.  That removed four of the eight
firings the red team found; Rice 300, Ground Nuts 150, Sugarcane 150 and
Pumpkins 105 remain.  The defect there is the *unit label*, not the quantity,
and the next lever (excluding the unknown-unit family from the =(t,u)= rung)
is deliberately not pulled here.

* The one thing worth re-reading before touching =QUANTITY_OUTLIER_K=

The ratio distribution has *no discontinuity*: over the 595,811 rows that have
a reference, p90 = 1.0, p99 = 3.6, p99.9 = 21.0, p99.99 = 233.7, max = 14,285.7.
100 is an argued tolerance, not a measured boundary, and this sweep is what
makes that statement checkable rather than a hedge.  Tanzania's 4,000,000 kg
Timber row sits at x173.3 and is the binding constraint from above -- loosen K
past ~170 and the row GH #857 names stops firing.
