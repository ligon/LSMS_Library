* Why two different row totals

The firing count is five countries (427,551 rows, 85 fired); the sensitivity
sweep is four (293,868 rows, 49 fired at the shipped K).  Uganda's
=crop_production= parquets were being cleared and rebuilt by a concurrent
workstream throughout, so the two readings caught it in different states.  The
five-country reading is the one quoted in =quantity_audit.py='s docstring and
in CLAUDE.md; the four-country sweep is what the threshold was *chosen* on.
Re-run either after an Uganda re-warm and both will agree at five.

* The one thing worth re-reading before touching =QUANTITY_OUTLIER_K=

The ratio distribution has *no discontinuity*: p90 = 1.0, p99 = 3.7,
p99.9 = 20, p99.99 = 174, max 11,029, smoothly.  100 is an argued tolerance,
not a measured boundary, and this sweep is what makes that statement checkable
rather than a hedge.  Tanzania's 4,000,000 kg Timber row sits at 173.3x and is
the binding constraint from above -- loosen K past ~170 and the row GH #857
names stops firing.
