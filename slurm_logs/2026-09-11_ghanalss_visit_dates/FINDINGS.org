** 2.1 GLSS5 2005-06: the 2nd-visit own-production quantity is DROPPED  [defect]
=2005-06/_/food_acquired.py= reads =s8hq4..s8hq12= and skips =s8hq3=, on the
strength of the Stata label "number of units consumed" and the ledger's
reading of it as a total (=.coder/ledger/ghanalss-visit-offset.md= s5;
CONTENTS.org l.1284-1288 "own production only from the 3rd").  Both are
wrong:
- The questionnaire (=G5QPartB.pdf= p.17, printed 8.11) heads columns 3..12
  "2nd 3rd ... 11th" and each asks "How much of own produced ... was
  consumed by the household since my last visit?".  Column 3 IS the 2nd visit.
- The data agree: =s8hq3 < sum(s8hq4..s8hq12)= in 27,314 rows -- a total
  cannot be smaller than the sum of its parts -- and =s8hq3= behaves exactly
  like =s8hq3= in 1991-92/1998-99/2012-13, where it is labelled the 2nd visit.
- Loss: 21,493 positive records; 1,959 (household, item) pairs vanish
  entirely; 57 households lose all own-production; 13.4% of the wave's
  summed own-production quantity (native units, indicative).
Consequences: the "10 / 9" consumption-occasions cell for GLSS5 in
CONTENTS.org l.1261 should read 10 / 10; the "weakest first-ask step
(1.02x)" remark at l.1467 is an artefact; and GLSS5 is NOT the counter-example
the ledger used to argue that =visit= must be the calendar number rather
than a per-module ordinal (the two modules DO share a first occasion).  The
calendar-number decision still stands on its other merits.
Third corroboration: =s8hq3= is positive in 21,493 rows against 18,336 for
=s8hq4= -- the first-ask excess CONTENTS.org documents at visit 2 in every
other wave, at exactly the column argued here to be visit 2.
Fix: read =s8hq3= as visit 2 (map q3..q12 -> 2..11), rebuild, re-derive the
first-ask table.  One-line script change, but three things cascade:
- =tests/test_ghanalss_visit_alignment.py:73= pins 2005-06 produced at
  =range(3, 12)= and l.269 excludes 2005-06 from a shared-first-occasion
  check "on purpose".  The test currently guards the wrong answer and goes
  red on the fix until corrected.
- =.coder/coverage/food_recall.csv:22= / =GhanaLSS/_/recall.yml= record
  2005-06 as =within_wave_variation: by-s= with "10 purchased / 9 produced"
  asks; both modules are 2..11, so the flag falls (and the spacing is now
  established at 3 days from =secb2=, see part 1).
- CONTENTS.org l.1261, l.1284-1288, l.1467 and the ledger s5 need the
  correction quoted, not silently overwritten.

** 2.2 GLSS1/GLSS2 1987-88, 1988-89: the produced side is a value PER EATING OCCASION, not a recall total  [defect]
Both wave scripts emit =Y12B.VFOODCPD= as the "per-recall value" of own
production (docstrings call it "produced_value per day" -- a claim that
entered in =c404b7fb2= with no source) beside =Y12A.CFOODBLV= ("amount spent
since my last visit").  Section 12B has no since-last-visit question at all
(questionnaire PDF p.68 / printed 43a; Interviewer manual printed p.71; BID
printed p.2: "Section 12B asks the amount consumed and market value of foods
grown or raised by the household in the last twelve months"): it asks the
months consumed in the past 12 (=MFOODCLY=), how many times per
day/week/month/quarter/half-year/year (=TFOODC= with time-unit code
=UTFOODC= 3..8), and "How much would it cost to buy the amount they ate each
time?" (=VFOODCPD=).

*Verified against GSS's own construction, not by elimination.*  The BID
(printed p.22) defines =EXPEND.HPFOOD= as "the value of home produced food
consumed in the last year calculated from Section 12B".  Summing
=VFOODCPD x TFOODC x (30.4 / days_per_unit) x MFOODCLY= per household
reproduces =HPFOOD= with median ratio 1.020 (IQR 1.015-1.03; the residual is
30.4 vs 30 days per month) in both waves (n = 2,253 / 2,437 households);
treating =VFOODCPD= as a per-DAY value gives 2.1-2.5x; the raw served value
is 1.3-1.7% of =HPFOOD=.  So the served produced row is one meal's worth
while the purchased row is ~14 days' worth.  Scale, summed over the wave: a
14-day equivalent (=VFOODCPD x TFOODC x 14/unit_days=) is 4.5-5.1x the
served value inside a consuming month and 2.3-3.0x averaged over the year;
the served own-production share of food is 17-18% and would be 33-52% on a
like-for-like window.

Fix: construct the produced value on the purchase window (or annualise
both sides; =EXPEND.PFOOD=/=HPFOOD= are the GSS annual constructions and are
shipped) in the two wave scripts; correct CONTENTS.org l.1242 / l.1258
(both modules are NOT "since my last visit") and the two docstrings.  Also
record the manual's rule that 12A falls back to "the past two weeks" when
the R1->R2 gap is <7 or >21 days (~300 households).

** 2.3 Instrument-level gaps (real, not the library's)
- Purchases carry NO quantity or unit before 2016-17: 12A (1987-89), 9B in
  GLSS3/4/5/6 ask "how much was spent" only (questionnaire pages quoted in
  the wave files).  =u='Value'= is the right encoding.  The recovery route is
  the wave's own price survey (=community_prices=), GH #770 step 2.
- Own production carries no VALUE before 2016-17 (only quantity, unit and a
  farmgate unit price); =Expenditure= NaN on produced rows is correct.
- GLSS3 rural households have 7 asks over 14 days vs urban 10 over 30 days
  (already in CONTENTS.org); 11 loc2-urban clusters ran the rural schedule,
  so a =loc2=-based rescale is wrong for them.
