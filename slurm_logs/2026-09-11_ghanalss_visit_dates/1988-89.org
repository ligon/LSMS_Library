* Visit protocol

*Two interviews ("rounds"), two weeks apart; Sections 12A/12B asked at the
SECOND; paper; no diary; no CAPI.*

- BID printed p.2 (PDF 4): "The household questionnaire was completed in two
  interviews two weeks apart: Sections 0-8, 16A, 17A and 17C were conducted
  in the first interview.  Sections 9-15, 16B and 17B were conducted in the
  second interview."  Section 12 (FOOD EXPENSES AND HOME PRODUCTION) is
  listed under "II. SECOND INTERVIEW"; Section 9 is headed "(BEGINNING OF
  SECOND INTERVIEW)" (printed p.4).
- BID printed p.15 (PDF 17): "Round 1 covered Sections 0 through 8 and Round
  2 covered Sections 9-17.  The interview for each of the two rounds ... were
  expected to take 2-3 hours.  Each team conducted 32 complete interviews in
  each four week period, 16 for each of the two interviewers.  Round one was
  conducted in one cluster the first week.  Then the team moved to a second
  cluster for the second week.  The third week, the team returned to conduct
  Round Two ... in the first cluster.  The second round was completed in the
  second cluster in week four."  Data entry "in regional offices in the week
  between the two rounds"; the supervisor "randomly revisited 25 percent of
  the households to verify the answers to some key questions".
- Questionnaire: PDF 40 (block =8=, printed p.29) ends =END OF ROUND ONE=;
  PDF 41 (printed p.29a) is the =ROUND TWO= divider ("EXPLAIN AGAIN THE
  OBJECTIVES OF THE SURVEY AND THE CONFIDENTIALITY OF THE DATA.  READ THE
  LIST OF HOUSEHOLD MEMBERS."); 12A begins at PDF 64 (block =12A1=, printed
  p.41a); PDF 75 (Section 15C) ends =END OF SECOND ROUND=.
- Interviewer's manual (=1988-89/Documentation/technical document/
  GHA_1988_GLSS_Interviewer_EN.pdf=, 75 pp, image-only; PDF = printed + 7 for
  printed pp.2-12): printed p.5 (PDF 12) "conduct interviews with households
  at the rate of eight per week ... Interviews may require one or several
  visits to complete, depending on whether or not all the respondents are
  available at the time of the first visit"; printed p.7 (PDF 14) "The data
  entry operator will enter Round One data between Round One and Round Two
  ... You must reask these questions at the beginning of Round Two"; printed
  p.12 (PDF 19) "For Round Two you must fill out the box entitled SECOND
  ROUND OF SURVEY.  The date should be the date you agreed on with the
  household at the end of the Round One interview."
- Recall phrase, 12A (PDF 64, printed p.41a), verbatim: col.2 "Have the
  members of your household bought any ...[...]... since my last visit?"
  col.3 "How much have they spent since my visit?"  12B (PDF 68, printed
  p.43a) is a 12-month module: col.3 "During which months in the past 12
  months (since..) did your household consume the ..[ ].. it produced?",
  col.4 "How many times during these months did they eat home-produced
  ...[ ]...?", col.5 "How much would it cost to buy the amount they ate each
  time?"  -- i.e. 12B has NO "since my last visit" question; its recall is
  months-in-the-past-12-months x times-per-unit x value.
- *What "since my last visit" means, per the manual* (Interviewer's
  manual printed p.70, PDF 67, Section 12 DEFINITIONS), verbatim: "SINCE MY
  LAST VISIT means since the last visit of the interviewer to the household
  during Round One.  In theory this period is two weeks; but, as this is
  not always the case, you must emphasize the time that has elapsed since
  Round One.  NOTE: If you make several visits or if the time since the
  last visit is less than one week or greater than 3 weeks, you must ask
  for information on food expenditure and consumption in the past two
  weeks."  And on 12A col.3: "Question 3.  This question refers to the
  total amount spent on a given food item since the Round One visit.  So,
  if the item has been bought several times in the interval, you must add
  up the individual amounts."  Respondent: "the person identified in
  Section 7 of Round One as the one best informed about food
  expenditures."  So the recall window is the MEASURED round-1 -> round-2
  interval where that interval is 7-21 days, and a nominal 14 days
  otherwise.  Printed p.71 (PDF 68) glosses 12B only for the months/times
  coding; there is no "since my last visit" in 12B.
- Diary: none (no diary form in the questionnaire; none mentioned in the
  Section 12 instructions).  Mode: paper, decentralised keying on a PC in
  the regional office (BID printed p.14; Data-entry manual).

*Contradiction with CONTENTS.org / food_acquired.py -- quote both sides.*
CONTENTS.org l.1258 tabulates 1988-89 as "visits 1 / single recall / single
recall / consumption occasions 1", and =food_acquired.py= writes "this 1980s
wave has a single, degenerate 'since my last visit' recall".  That is right
about the NUMBER OF CONSUMPTION ASKS (one), and =visit = 1= is a documented
convention, not a claim about calendar contacts.  But the household was
contacted TWICE and 12A was asked at the SECOND contact (BID p.2; PDF 41
divider precedes 12A at PDF 64; =Y00B= section-12 first-visit date equals
the section-9 date in 3,156 of 3,186 households and the section-1 date +
~14 days).  So "since my last visit" is a MEASURED per-household window:
round-1 interview date -> round-2 interview date, median 14 days (see
below).  Two consequences: (a) =1988-89/_/data_info.yml= wires
=interview_date: Int_t = DAY1/MO1/YR1=, the ROUND-ONE date -- the date on
which the food module was actually asked is =DAY2/MO2/YR2= (with the caveat
in Idiosyncrasies s1); (b) CONTENTS.org's "no recall-period metadata slot"
caveat does not bind this wave: the window is recoverable per household
from =Y00A=.  In calendar terms GLSS2's food ask sits at the same position
(second household contact) that later waves label =visit = 2=; recorded,
not adjudicated.

* Idiosyncrasies and contradictions

1. *The cover-page round-2 date is wrong for 12 whole clusters (269
   households): =Y00A: DAY2/MO2/YR2= equals =DAY1/MO1/YR1=.*  Clusters
   2557 and 2558 (32 HH each), 2065, 2121, 2358, 2361, 2629, 2638, 2645,
   2678, 2749 (16 each) and 2774 (15 of 16).  Three independent clocks in
   the same households say round 2 happened ~14 days later: =Y00B=
   section-9 first-visit date minus section-1 date (median 12-14 in those
   clusters; 0 in only 3 rows), =DAY8 - DAY3= (round-2 minus round-1 data
   entry: cluster medians 7-36), =DAY2S - DAY1S= (supervisor verification:
   5-17), and =DAY5 - DAY1= (proposed date, cluster medians 4.5-14).  So the cover-page
   round-2 date, not the fieldwork, is the defect -- the round-1 date was
   transcribed (or keyed) into the round-2 box for those clusters.
   *Recommendation*: for this wave take the round-2 interview date from
   =Y00B= (=SECTION == 9=, =DAY1/MO1/YR1=), which agrees with =Y00A: DAY2=
   in 2,845 of 3,186 households and is right where =Y00A= is not.
   =EXPEND: MO2/YR2= ("month and year of the second interview") is derived
   from the cover page and carries the same 269 errors.
2. *=interview_date= in =1988-89/_/data_info.yml= wires the ROUND-ONE date*
   (=DAY1/MO1/YR1=).  For the roster (Section 1, round one) that is the
   right date; for =food_acquired= (Section 12, round two) the interview
   date is the round-2 date -- see s1 for which variable to use.  Neither
   =data_info.yml= nor CONTENTS.org says which round a table's interview
   date refers to.
3. *=Y00C= is not block 0C* (see above).  The BID's Appendix B naming is
   misleading; CONTENTS.org does not mention =Y00B= or =Y00C= at all.
4. *Year-digit typos*: 11 negative and 4 > 60-day R1->R2 gaps are all
   88<->89 slips around January (e.g. HID 250105 R1 1988-01-19 vs proposed
   1989-02-01; 250106 R1 1989-01-19 vs R2 1988-02-01; 240907 R1 1988-05-16
   vs R2 1989-05-30; 272612 R1 1989-12-14 vs R2 1989-02-28).  =Y00B= HID
   214222 section 12 has =DAY2=0, MO2=0, YR2=1= (unparseable).  Filter
   before computing spans.
5. *0B's per-section dates are per-round dates.*  3,164 of 3,192 households
   carry one identical date across sections 1-8 and 3,142 of 3,186 across
   9-15.  The grid does not tell you that Section 12 was asked on a
   different day from Section 9 -- it tells you both were asked at the
   round-2 contact.  The "several visits to complete" the manual allows
   (printed p.5) show up only as the 345 households with a CHECK-UP VISIT

6. *No urban/rural difference in the protocol* (unlike GLSS3): both strata
   are on the 14-day two-round schedule.  What differs is publicity: rural
   clusters were announced by letter to the chief and by a courtesy call;
   urban households got letters delivered by the interviewers 1-2 weeks
   before Round One (Supervisor's manual printed p.7, PDF 10; BID printed
   p.14).
