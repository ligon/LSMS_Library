* Visit protocol

One line: paper questionnaire, 8 visits at 2-day intervals (rural workload) or 11
at 3-day intervals (urban workload); 8H, 9A2 and 9B asked at every visit except the
first with the recall "since my last visit" (first ask: "since my first visit");
urban households keep a daily expenditure diary that the interviewer transcribes
into 9A2/9B; data keyed in the regional capital in Rode-PC in two rounds.
CONTENTS.org already records all of this; verified, and extended below.

- Schedule.  =pdf/G3Intman.pdf= s1.9, PDF p.7 (printed 7): "There will be 8
  interviewer visits at 2-day intervals to each rural household and 11
  interviewer visits at 3-day intervals to each urban household in a cycle.
  Thus a workload for the 16 days in a rural cluster is 10 households, while a
  workload for the 33 days in an urban cluster is 15 households."  Rural
  workload = 2 batches of 5 households interviewed on alternate days; urban = 3
  batches of 5 on days 1,4,7,...,31 / 2,5,...,32 / 3,6,...,33.  Same text in
  =pdf/G3Supman.pdf= s1.9, PDF p.7, and in the DDI "Data collection notes", PDF
  p.9.  The data show the batching: 236 clusters have exactly 2 distinct
  visit-1 dates and 94 have 3 (=S0B.DTA= =vd1/vm1/vy1=).
- Appointment.  =G3Intman.pdf= PDF pp.12-13: "there will be 8 (rural) or 11
  (urban) repeated visits to the household and ... each interview will last no
  longer than 1 hour"; "DATE OF NEXT VISIT ... This should be exactly 2 days
  apart in the case of the rural household and 3 days apart for urban
  households."  =G3Supman.pdf= s4.3, PDF p.13: on the arrival day interviewers
  "set the day and time for the interview" -- the time is agreed orally; no
  instrument field records it (see next section).
- Which sections at which visit.  =G3Intman.pdf= s3.7, PDF p.13, and the
  preprinted SECTION column of the Summary of Survey Results page
  (=G3QPartA.pdf= PDF p.4, printed 04):

  |-------+----------------------+--------------------|
  |     1 | 1,2,6,7              | 1,2,6,7            |

  So Section 0 first-visit data (S0A) and the roster are visit 1; the
  consumption modules run visits 2..8 (rural) / 2..11 (urban); 9A1 (less
  frequent purchases, 3- and 12-month recall) is the LAST visit only.
- Recall phrase.  =G3QPartB.pdf= PDF p.20 (9B, Food expenses): q1 "How much was
  spent on ... since my first visit?", q2..q10 "How much was spent on ... since
  my last visit?".  8H (PDF pp.9-12): q3 "How much was consumed by the
  household since my first visit", q4..q12 "... since my last visit".  9A2 (PDF
  pp.16-19) same pattern, q1..q10.  =G3Intman.pdf= PDF p.42 (printed 42): "Part
  H is to be administered on every visit except the first"; PDF p.43: 9A2
  "Responses ... will be solicited during all visits with the exception of the
  first visit.  In other words for urban it will be 10 times and 7 times for
  rural areas.  For each visit, you will ask for how much was spent by the
  household since your last visit."  =G3Supman.pdf= PDF p.16: "interviewers
  should always use the reference period 'since my last visit'."
- Reference period, in GSS's own words.  =pdf/G3report.pdf= Appendix 1, PDF
  p.129 (printed 111): "Since no consumption and expenditure data are collected
  on the first visit to each household, the reference period for rural areas
  would therefore be 14 days (ie. 7 x 2), and for urban areas 30 days (ie. 10 x
  3)."  PDF p.128 (printed 110) gives the reasoning (Scott-Amenuvegbe recall
  loss; monthly pay-day in towns).
- Diary: urban only, paper, daily.  =G3Intman.pdf= PDF p.7: "A dairy [sic] of
  daily consumption and expenditure will be used to support the urban
  interviews.  During the first visit a literate person already identified in
  each urban household will be trained to record all subsequent expenditures
  made by the household and to submit the dairy to the interviewer on his next
  visit for entry into the appropriate sections.  Where a household has no
  literate member the supervisor will make daily visits".  =G3Intman.pdf= s13.3
  PDF p.43: the interviewer sums the diary entries "since your last visit" and
  writes the total in the visit's column; "Transfer of entries into the
  questionnaire will start from your second visit."  =G3report.pdf= PDF p.20
  (printed 2): "All urban households were given a special diary, and requested
  to record on a separate page each day all the expenses they incurred ... In
  the case of illiterate households the supervisor or the supplementary
  interviewer visited them and did the recording."  The DDI (PDF p.10) lists
  "diaries which respondents had failed to fill in" among the field problems.
  The diary form itself is reproduced NOWHERE in the shipped documentation
  (searched all 14 text layers: the only Part-B hit for "diary" is "Other
  dairy" in 8H's item list).
- A second, longitudinal diary programme.  =G3Intman.pdf= s1.10, PDF p.7: after
  the team leaves, a locally trained interviewer keeps daily expenditure
  diaries for 3 rural households per cluster (rotating 1-3, 4-6, 7-9 by cycle)
  or all 15 urban households, "until the end of the year", "to capture the
  monthly variations in the expenditure patterns".  No file in =1991-92/Data/=
  corresponds to this and no other document mentions it.
- Paper, not CAPI.  Data entry in Rode-PC on PCs in the eight regional
  capitals, two rounds: Part A (sections 0-7) keyed after the 5th visit, Part B
  (sections 8-12) after the last visit (=G3DEOman.pdf= s2, PDF p.6; DDI PDF
  p.11; =G3Intman.pdf= s2.3, PDF p.9).  There are therefore no automatic
  timestamps of any kind.
- Fieldwork window: 30 September 1991 to September 1992 (DDI PDF p.9;
  =G3report.pdf= PDF p.20).  Visit dates in =S0B= run 1991-02-21 .. 1992-11-08
  once the obvious typos are excluded (see Idiosyncrasies).

Conclusion on TIME: no field on the household questionnaire, the diary, the
summary page or the data-entry system records a time of day or an interview
duration.  The manual states an expected duration ("no longer than 1 hour",
=G3Intman.pdf= PDF p.12) and the supervisor's observation form may note section
start times for the handful of observed interviews.  A request for time-of-day
data for GLSS3 has nothing to attach to; the request is for DATES that exist on
paper but were not keyed, and for the diaries.

* What shipped: instrument field -> distributed variable

Two Section-0 files ship.  The DDI (=ddi-documentation-english-12.pdf=) does NOT
document either: its file inventory starts at "Household roster -s1" (PDF p.15)
and no S0A/S0B variable appears in its 453 pages.  The only description is one
line each in =pdf/g3usersg.pdf= PDF p.8: "S0A: Survey information.  S0B: Survey
information continued", and the report's Appendix 2 (PDF p.134): "0 Household
Identification -- Religion of head.  Primary language used by respondent.  Date
and outcome of interviewer visits."  The Rode-PC format list (=G3DEOman.pdf= s3.2,
PDF p.8) settles which pages were keyed: Round 1 format 1 = "SEC 0 : ID of HH"
(page 01a) and Round 2 format 1 = "SURVEY RESULTS" (page 04).  Pages 02, 03 and
05 have no format, i.e. were never keyed.

* The NSO request for this wave

GLSS3 already ships visit DATES (day/month/year) for every visit of 4,230
households (4,194 with a full 8- or 11-visit schedule), the visit RESULT code for
about 3,120 of them (1,108 dated households have no result code at visit 1, and
about the same number at every later visit), and check-up revisit dates, all from
the Summary of Survey Results page.  It ships NO time-of-day and NO duration, and none exists on

* Idiosyncrasies and contradictions

- *The fieldwork stratum is not =loc2=, and CONTENTS.org's 30-day/14-day rule is
  stated in terms of the wrong variable.*  CONTENTS.org l.1289ff says "urban:
  10 asks x 3 days = 30 days; rural: 7 asks x 2 days = 14 days" and GH #851
  proposes rescaling by stratum.  Measured on =S0B= dates joined to
  =POV_GH:loc2=: 9 clusters with =loc2= = 1 (urban) were interviewed on the
  RURAL schedule -- 10 households each, 8 dated visits, median gap 2.0 days:
  clusters 3012, 3263, 3531, 3540, 3559, 3560, 3612, 3794, 3796 (90
  households); and 2 further =loc2= = 1 clusters of 10 households have no dates
  keyed at all (3551, 3554 -- counted here from their rural take of 10, since
  there are no dates to confirm the schedule).  That is 11 clusters / 110
  households, which is
  exactly what the Main Report describes, Appendix 1 PDF p.130 (printed 112):
  "This shortfall can be approximately made up by counting 11 urban areas as
  'rural' for fieldwork purposes (ie. 11 x 10 = 110) and then reassigning them
  back to urban at the analysis stage."  So for those 110 households the
  recall window is 14 days, the diary was (per the manuals) not used, and a
  365/30 rescale keyed on =loc2= would understate their annualised food
  expenditure by 2.14x.  No =loc2= = 2 cluster was interviewed on the urban
  schedule.  The correct stratum for the recall window is the FIELD stratum,
  recoverable per household from =S0B= (dated slots 9-11 non-empty, or median
  gap 3 vs 2), not =loc2=.  A further 16 =loc2= = 1 households have only 5-7
  dated visits at 3-day gaps (truncated cycles) and 1 has 10.
- *292 households (6.5%) have no visit dates: 26 whole clusters plus parts of
  6.*  All 292 have 9B data, so the Summary page was simply not keyed.  Whole
  clusters: 3331, 3340, 3344, 3347, 3348, 3352, 3354, 3355, 3403, 3464, 3466,
  3471, 3479, 3551, 3554, 3626, 3627, 3628, 3632, 3634, 3739, 3119, 3330, 3346,
  3474, 3476 (the last five are 6-9-household clusters, unkeyed in full);
  partial: 3056 (1 of 10), 3071 (2 of 20), 3332 (10 of 20), 3343 (4 of 10),
  3707 (1 of 10), 3714 (4 of 20).  All but 3403 (region 6), 3707 (9) and 3714
  (10) carry =POV_GH:region= = 4, and the supervisor codes are 1, 5 or 11, i.e.
  this is a team-level omission concentrated in one region, not random.  CONTENTS.org l.1310 ("urban records 11
  dates ... rural records 8") is true of the households that have any, and
  silent on these.
- *30 households are in =S0A= but not in =S0B=; 29 of them are in no other file
  checked (=S9B=, =POV_GH=).*  Clusters 3743 (10 of them) and 3786 (7) account
  for most.  =S0A= has 4,552 rows -- the user guide's "actual achieved sample
  was 4552" -- while =S0B= has 4,522 and =POV_GH= 4,523.  These look like
  replaced or abandoned households whose page 01 was keyed in Round 1 before
  the replacement (page 01's "THIS HOUSEHOLD WILL BE REPLACED BY NO" box is not
  shipped, so it cannot be confirmed).
- *The ledger is stale on labels.*  =.coder/ledger/ghanalss-visit-offset.md= s6
  says GLSS3's q-to-visit offset "has none of the evidence the other four waves
  have" because =S9B.DTA= carries no labels.  The DDI does carry them, PDF
  pp.68-69: =s9bq1= "How much spent on item since first visit", =s9bq2= "How
  much spent on sincce third t visit" [sic], =s9bq3= "How much spent on 4th
  visit" ... =s9bq10= "How much spent on 11th visit"; =s9a2q1= "How much spent
  on first visit", =s9a2q2= "How much spent on last visit 2" ... =s9a2q10=
  "... 10".  Read with the questionnaire (q1 = "since my first visit", asked at
  visit 2) these give q_k -> visit k+1, which is the mapping CONTENTS.org's
  table already uses (q1..q10 -> 2..11).  The =s9bq2= label's "third" is the
  DDI's own inconsistency, not evidence of a different offset.
- *=S0A= and =S0B= header codes disagree.*  =intv= differs in 164 households,
  =opcode= in 88, =sup= in 51 (of 4,517 with both).  The Round-2 header was
  keyed from the same page 01 after the cycle; where it differs the S0B value
  is the later keying.  Not a bug to fix, but do not join the two files on
  =intv=.
- *Date typos in =S0B=.*  Two =vy= = 95 (cluster 3343 hh 4 visit 2; cluster 3519
  hh 12 visit 8); 9 impossible day/month combinations; 71 households with a
  negative inter-visit gap; visit-1 dates as early as 1991-02-21 against a
  fieldwork start of 30 Sep 1991.  Any =melt_visit_intervals=-style wiring of
  =S0B= into =interview_date= should validate against the household's own
  median gap rather than trust every slot.
- *=1991-92/_/data_info.yml= declares =sample:= twice* (lines 13 and 71).  The
  first block carries =cluster_features=-shaped content (=v: clust=, =Region=,
  =Rural=) under the =sample= key; YAML last-wins so the second, correct block
  is what loads.  Not edited here (brief forbids); worth a one-line fix.
- *The CONTENTS.org first-interval claim holds.*  CONTENTS.org l.1467: "the
  S0B.DTA visit dates give a median gap of exactly 3.0 days (urban) / 2.0 days
  (rural) at every step, including visit 1 -> 2".  Verified for the median,
  and -- once the typo gaps are excluded -- for the mean and the modal share
  too (see the gap table above): the first interval is neither shorter nor
  more dispersed than the rest.  The raw per-step means (2.61 urban, 2.57
  rural at step 1) that a naive computation gives are typo artefacts, not a
  first-visit effect; do not carry them into CONTENTS.org.
