* Visit protocol

Seven visits per household at five-day intervals (visits on cycle days 1, 6,
11, 16, 21, 26, 31), 35-day cycle plus one travelling day, ten cycles over the
survey year (24 Oct 2016 - 17 Oct 2017), 15 households per interviewer per EA,
interviewed in five batches of three households on cycle days 1..5.  9B
(frequently purchased items) and 8H (own produce) are asked at visits 2..7,
i.e. six recalls, with the recall phrase "since my last visit"; the diary is
used in ALL EAs; the wave is CAPI (CSPro on Windows tablets) with paper
questionnaires and forms also carried.

- Main Report, PDF p. 305 (printed p. 283), "Interviewer Workload":
  "An interviewer visited each household in the EA assigned to him/her every
  sixth day i.e. after every five days. In other words, the interviewer
  visited each of the 15 selected household seven (7) times in order to
  complete the cycle."  Same page, "Survey Period and Team Composition":
  "10 cycles of 35 days each and one travelling day ... Interviewers in rural
  and urban areas all used the diary method as far as possible".  PDF p. 306
  (printed p. 284): the diary paragraph CONTENTS.org quotes (literate member
  trained at the first visit; otherwise daily interviewer visits to fill the
  diary).
- Manual, section 1.8 "INTERVIEWER WORKLOAD", Table 3 "SCHEDULE OF VISITS" (docx

** The one shipped date is the FIRST-visit (intake) date -- measured

=g7sec0.dta= gives one date per household and its label does not say which
visit.  The within-cluster structure settles it.  For the 15 households of a
cluster, the Manual's Table 3 puts visit 1 on cycle days 1..5, three
households per day; visits 2..7 would then span days 6..35.  Measured on the
14,008 parseable dates:

| statistic | value |
|---|---|
| distinct dates per cluster | 5 in 663 of 1,000 clusters (4: 80; 6: 178; 7: 42; 3: 13; 8: 11; 9: 5; 2: 5; 1: 3) |
| households per (cluster, date) | 3 in 3,312 cells (2: 826; 1: 649; 4: 288; 5: 60; larger: 47) |
| offset of a household's date from its cluster's earliest date | 0: 2,890; 1: 2,757; 2: 2,625; 3: 2,396; 4: 1,990; 5: 170; 6: 69; ... (>= 9: 8.1% urban, 7.0% rural) |
| cluster start date, day-within-a-36-day-cycle counted from 24 Oct 2016 | day 0 in 865 of 1,000 clusters; day 35 in 37; the rest scattered |
| cluster start dates by survey week | spikes at weeks 0, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 36, 41, 46 (94, 83, 98, 91, 92, 89, 92, 82, 99, 92 clusters) = ten cycles |
| day of week | all seven days used (Wed 2,264 ... Sun 1,605) |

A 30-day spread of per-household intake would be impossible if the date were
the last visit or the interview month; five consecutive days with three
households each is the intake batch structure of Table 3 exactly.  So
=interview_date.Int_t= is the date of VISIT 1 (roster, sections 1, 2, 5, 6A, 7
and diary training) -- NOT the date of any consumption recall.  Under the
protocol, visit k was nominally =Int_t + 5*(k-1)= days; the survey's own
non-adherence to that (the >= 5-day offsets above, the 218 non-completers,
the batches that restarted late) is precisely what the un-shipped A0.2 dates
would measure.  The median gap between successive visits therefore CANNOT be
computed for this wave; the nominal protocol gap is 5 days and the measured
intra-cluster intake spread is 4 days.

=100000= 27, =111100= 22, =110000= 20, =011111= 12, =111000= 10, =111111= 8,

** Variable dumps of every Section-0-like file (names, types, labels)

and none is a visit field.  =g7sec9b_small.dta= carries NO variable labels;

item), NOT per-visit anything; =freqcd= is the food code (one row per
household x item); =index= is a row counter from the 3.2 GB file.

* The NSO request for this wave

The wave ships ONE date per household (visit 1) and NO time of day, duration,
per-visit result code, interviewer id or supervisor verification.  Ask GSS
for the following, named as the instrument names them, for all 14,009
households (=clust=, =nh= / =hid=), and for the 991 selected households that
were not interviewed:

1. Page A0.2 "SUMMARY OF SURVEY RESULTS" of the household questionnaire
   (Module A / Module B cover), all seven rows: INTERVIEWER VISITS =DATE
   (DD MM YEAR)=, =RESULTS (COMPLETE 1 / PARTIAL 2 / DISCONTINUED 3)=,
   =DURATION (HR MIN)=; CHECK-UP VISITS =DATE=, =RESULTS=; =SUPERVISOR
   (SATISFACTORY 1 / TO BE COMPLETED 2 / TO BE REDONE 3)=.  This single page
   holds the visit date, the result and the duration of every visit and is
   the core of the request.
2. Page A0.1 "SURVEY INFORMATION": the =DATE= and =INTERVIEWER= of the
   SECOND .. SEVENTH VISIT boxes; the =SUPERVISOR=, =DATE= and =REINTERVIEW
   BY SUPERVISOR?= of the seven VERIFICATION OF QUESTIONNAIRE boxes; the
   interviewer and supervisor code numbers of the first-visit box; and
   =REASONS FOR NOT COMPLETING THE QUESTIONNAIRE= (1/2/3) for the 991
   non-interviewed households, which would let the =hhstatus= 0/1 collapse
   be undone and the Table A5b non-response be decomposed.
3. The per-visit date strips at the top of Sections 8H ("2nd .. 7th / day
   month") and 9B ("VISIT day month" x 6), and 9A's "Date of this visit", as
   entered on the tablet.  If item 1 is supplied these are redundant, but
   they are the dates physically attached to the consumption recalls and are
   the check on item 1.
4. Section 1 =Start Time= (visit 1).
5. CSPro CAPI paradata, if the application recorded it: case creation and
   last-modification timestamps, per-section or per-field entry times, the
   operator (interviewer) id and tablet id per case, and the synchronisation
   /submission times to the server.  No GLSS7 document describes such a log;
   the community file proves the application had clock-time fields
   (=g7comSEC0.dta: intst_hh/mm=, =intend_hh/mm=, the latter empty), so the
   request is "whether such records exist, and if so a case-level extract",
   not an assertion that they do.  The Manual's 2.3 (Part A submitted after
   the third visit, Part B at the end of the EA) means the submission log
   alone would date visits 3 and 7 for every household.
6. The household diaries ("DIARY OF CONSUMPTION AND EXPENDITURE", one sheet
   per household with a header date and a per-line =Date= column), or their
   keyed form if they were ever keyed.  The Report says diary entries were
   transferred into 9B/8H at the next visit, so GSS may hold only the paper
   sheets; if they exist they are the only per-DAY record of expenditure in
   any GLSS wave and the most valuable single item here.
7. Supervisors' paper forms: the per-cycle verification form, the interview
   observation form (which notes "the time at which he/she began each
   section"), and the end-of-cycle supervisor's report ("household numbers
   and the date of the data collection of the cycle").  Lower priority; they
   would give cycle dates per EA and section start times for observed
   interviews only.
8. For the community questionnaire, the reason =intend_hh/mm= (end time) is
   empty in all 892 records.

Explicitly: the wave does NOT ship complete visit dates, so the request is
for dates (visits 2..7 and the check-up visits) as well as for time of day
and duration.

* Idiosyncrasies and contradictions

- The shipped date is the intake date, and =data_info.yml= does not say so.
  =interview_date: Int_t: [ddate, mdate, ydate]= is served as "the" interview
  date; it is the date of visit 1 (proof above).  The consumption recalls in
  =food_acquired= (visits 2..7) are dated nominally =Int_t + 5*(visit-1)=,
  and nothing in the API records that offset.  Worth a comment in the YAML.
- 8 clusters have intake dates before the Report's fieldwork start.  Report
  PDF p. 25 (printed p. 3): fieldwork "commencing on 24th October, 2016";
  Preface (PDF p. 2) and PDF p. 18: "22nd October, 2016".
  The data: 8 clusters start in the weeks before 24 Oct 2016, the earliest
  on 1 Oct 2016 (during the 11 Sep - 5 Oct training), and =g7comSEC0=
  carries one community date of 2016-01-06.  Either pilot EAs kept in the
  file, or date entry errors.  Not adjudicated.
- Manual Table 3 is the correct schedule and CONTENTS.org does not say so.
  CONTENTS.org (lines 1509-1540) records that the Manual's 3.7 says "eleven
  visits" and its Table 2 schedules eleven visit-days three days apart.
  Both true.  But the Manual's Table 3 "SCHEDULE OF VISITS", the next table
  in section 1.8 (pandoc shows it as =[TABLE]=), is the 35-day, five-day,
  three-households-a-day, seven-visit schedule, and the intake dates in
  =g7sec0= reproduce it (5 dates per cluster, 3 households each).  The
  Manual contradicts itself within one page; the data side with Table 3.
- "Five interviews per day" is wrong by the survey's own numbers.  Report
  PDF p. 305 (printed p. 283): "Each interviewer conducted five interviews
  per day"; Manual 1.8: "each interviewer would conduct five interviews per
  day".  Supervisors' manual line 80: "each interviewer is expected to do
  three interviews per day", Table 3 and the measured 3 households per
  (cluster, date) agree with three.  (Five per day x three days would give a
  2-day intake spread; the data show 4.)
- The Manual's 9B instructions are stale in the same way as its Table 2.
  Section 9 Part B: "it will be administered 10 times in all. In your second
  visit you will ask for how much was spent by the household since the last
  3 days" -- ten asks and a 3-day first recall are the GLSS3-urban / GLSS5
  eleven-visit design, beside a questionnaire with six 9B columns headed
  "since my last visit".  CONTENTS.org records the Table 2 / 3.7 staleness
  and not this passage; same lineage, one more instance.
- The Manual's Part A submission timing is written in 3-day arithmetic.
  Section 1.8: "At the end of the 5th visit (day 15th) the interviewer should
  have completed Part A ... submitted to the Supervisor on day 17th"; section
  2.3: "At the end of the third visit you will have to submit Sections 1-7".
  Visit 5 on day 15 is the 3-day schedule; under the five-day schedule day 15
  is visit 3 (sections 1-7 complete after visit 3 per the 3.7 table).  The
  two passages agree only if the interval is 5 days and "5th visit" is stale.
- The Report is now in the repository; CONTENTS.org line 845-848 says it is
  not.  The population entry for 2016-17 ("Concrete next step ...: acquire
  the GLSS7 Main Report ... It is not in this repository's
  =2016-17/Documentation/= and was not consulted") predates the food_acquired
  entry at lines 1476-1499, which quotes it.  =Documentation/GLSS7 Main
  Report.pdf= (8.2 MB, 343 PDF pages, text layer) is present.  The population
  entry is stale, not wrong when written.
- The Supervisors' manual is NOT stale on the visit schedule.  It says seven
  visits, every fifth day, three interviews a day, 35-day cycles, throughout
  -- so the eleven-visit boilerplate is specific to the interviewers' Manual,
  and the "GLSS7 Final Manual is stale" note should not be read as "the
  manuals are stale".
- The diary form is a GLSS5 form.  =DIARY.doc= Word metadata: title
  "GLSS-5A", author "N. B. MENSAH", keywords "FIELD OPERATIONS".  The unit
  code list on it (None 00 .. Tubers 26, 25 codes) is not the 9B unit
  vocabulary that =_/food_acquired.py= decodes from the tablet file; a
  diary-to-9B transcription therefore involved a unit re-code by the
  interviewer.
- The community CAPI file shows the application's time fields were real and
  partly unfilled: =g7comSEC0.dta= start time 892/892, end time 0/892.
  Expect the same pattern (present in the application, not necessarily
  entered) for any household DURATION field GSS supplies.
- =g7loc_upd.dta: month= carries month-NAME value labels while =g7sec0.dta:
  mdate= carries numbers; a naive ===  between them is 0% agreement, the
  values are identical.  =hhstatus= likewise labelled in one and raw in the
  other.
- 85 households in =g7sec0= have no rows in =g7sec9b_small= (67 of them
  =hhstatus=0=, 18 =hhstatus=1=), and 3 =hhstatus=1= households have visit 2
  empty in 9B: "All 7 visits completed" is a fieldwork-status code, not a
  data-presence guarantee.
- No DDI content: =ddi-documentation-english-97.pdf= has "No content
  available" under Sampling, Questionnaires, Data Processing and Data
  Appraisal, and its Data Collection page is blank.  Nothing about dates,
  visits or CAPI can be cited from the DDI.
- Word-97 files: =DIARY.doc= is fully recoverable with =strings -n 6=
