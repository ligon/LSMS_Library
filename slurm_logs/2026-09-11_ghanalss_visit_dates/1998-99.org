* Visit protocol

One line: seven interviewer visits per household at exactly five-day
intervals over a 36-day cycle (35 interviewing days + 1 travel day), paper
questionnaire in two booklets (Part A sections 1-7, Part B sections 8-12),
keyed in the field on IMPS 3.1 by a team data-entry operator; 9B / 9A2 / 8H
asked at visits 2-7; diary urban-only.  CONTENTS.org's row for 1998-99
(7 visits, q->visit map, 6 occasions) is confirmed; nothing below contradicts
it, but several things are not in it.

** What SEC0B and SEC0C ARE (the wave-note question)

The User Guide (PDF p.26) calls SEC0B "Survey information for part A and B"
and SEC0C just "Survey Information" -- quoted, because it is the only
description in circulation and it is vaguer than the data.  Read against the
handover schedule and the dates themselves:

- *SEC0C is the Part A summary grid, keyed for rows FIRST..THIRD only.*
  Exactly 5,996 households x 3 rows = 17,988; no duplicates.  Its row-1 date
  equals =SEC0A= =dd/mm/yy= for 5,898 of 5,996 households (the other 98 differ
  by keying slips: -5..-122 days).  Rows 4-7 of the Part A grid could never be
  filled because the Part A booklet is handed to the DEO "at the beginning of
  the 4th visit" (DEO manual printed p.6) -- so those rows were not keyed,
  and the Part A data-entry program presumably had three visit records.
  (Two households in SEC0A, =4405-2= and =4809-24=, have no SEC0C rows.)
- *SEC0B is the Part B summary grid, keyed for all seven rows, plus the
  Part B cover's first-visit and first-verification fields repeated on every
  row.*  The Part B cover's "FIRST VISIT" date (=s0day/s0month/s0year=) is
  =SEC0A= first visit + 5 days for 4,827 of 5,987 households and equal to it
  for 1,029: most interviewers wrote the date of the first visit AT WHICH
  PART B WAS USED (calendar visit 2) in the Part B cover.  Likewise its
  supervisor-verification date is SEC0A's + 5 for 2,319 and equal for 1,454.
  =s0interv= equals =SEC0A.intv= for 5,382 of 5,988 (the rest: relief
  interviewer or slip).

Why =visitcd= 1 has 1,940 rows and =visitcd= 7 has 4,079 in SEC0B: the
interviewers used two conventions in the Part B grid.  Per-household
classification by the median of (SEC0B row k date - SEC0C row k date) over
k=1..3:

| convention | HH | rows 1..7 present | what row k holds |
|------------+------+-------------------+------------------|
| calendar numbering, row 1 blank (no Part B section is asked at visit 1) | 4,074 | 2..7 | calendar visit k |
| SHIFTED by one row (row 1 = first Part-B visit) | 1,287 | 1..6, row 7 blank (11 exceptions) | calendar visit k+1 |
| calendar numbering, row 7 blank | 627 | 1..6 | calendar visit k; 7th-visit date NOT RECORDED |
| calendar numbering, all seven rows | 10 | 1..7 | calendar visit k |
(12 further households have no SEC0C match and fall into the first bucket
by default; 8 rows have null =visitcd=.)

Evidence: has-row-1 x has-row-7 is 4,064 / 1,902 / 14 / 18 (neither);
SEC0B row k minus SEC0A first visit has median 5(k-1) for the calendar group
and 5k for the shifted group (row 1: 1,246 households at +5, 555 at 0; row 6:
+25 vs +30).  For the 627 "row 7 blank" households the 7th visit DID happen:
=SEC9B.s9bq6= ("7th visit amount spent") is positive for at least one item
in 618 of 627 (98.6%; reference group 99.5%) and =SEC8H.s8hq8= in 58.9% (vs
65.3%).  Only its date is missing.  Nothing in the User Guide or the
questionnaire says which convention was intended; the printed grid says
FIRST..SEVENTH, and the interviewer manual (printed p.15) says "for each visit
you should show the date on which you asked the questions", which favours
calendar numbering.

Also in SEC0B: 148 households (in 111 clusters) have two rows under the same
=visitcd= (298 rows) -- typically two consecutive visit dates keyed under one
code, e.g. =4005-16= row 3 on 20/6/98 and 25/6/98 -- and 8 rows have null
=visitcd=.  Ten household ids in SEC0B have no SEC0A counterpart and vice
versa in the same ten clusters (=4102-13= vs =4102-14=, ...): the household
number was keyed differently in the Part A and Part B batches.

* Idiosyncrasies and contradictions

1. *SEC0B row shift (above).*  1,287 households' Part B grids start at the
   first Part-B visit, 627 omit the 7th, 4,074 use calendar rows.  Any use
   of =SEC0B.visitcd= as the calendar visit is wrong for 21% of households
   until de-shifted against SEC0C / SEC0A.  Not in CONTENTS.org.
2. *SEC0B's cover "first visit" is calendar visit 2 for 81% of households*
   (=s0day/s0month/s0year= = SEC0A + 5 for 4,827 / 5,987).  Do not wire it as
   the interview date; SEC0A's is the first visit.
3. *CONTENTS.org l.1436-1450 vs this measurement.*  CONTENTS says "Bin GLSS4
   households by measured exposure and total spend is flat while the implied
   daily rate spans 2.7x, entirely from the denominator", without naming the
   file the intervals came from.  Measured here: 97-99% of successive gaps
   are exactly 5 days; the residual variation in raw SEC0B is dominated by
   the untreated row shift (a 0- or 10-day step where the two conventions
   meet) and keying slips (-360, -86, -26).  Quoted side by side, not
   adjudicated: CONTENTS's input file is not named, and this measurement
   is of the raw SEC0B / SEC0C files as shipped.
4. *CONTENTS.org's first-ask discussion* ("the 'last visit' it refers back to
   is the intake visit, which need not be five days earlier") -- the realised
   first interval IS five days (98.9% exactly 5), but the PROTOCOL window for
   the first ask is six: Interviewer manual printed p.50, "since the past 6
   days, starting from the day prior to your first visit", and the 9A2 / 9B
   first column prints "since my first visit?".  So a 6/5 = 1.20 window
   ratio is built in by design (the 1998-99 first-ask row ratio in CONTENTS
   is 84,253 / 73,701 = 1.14).  Additive evidence, not a contradiction.
5. *Section-by-visit table*: both manuals put 9A1 at the 7th visit, 10 at
   the 5th and 11 at the 6th; the printed grid (p.03 / 8.03) puts 9A1 at the
   5th, 10 at the 6th, 11 at the 7th.  8H / 9A2 / 9B at 2-7 in all.
6. *Diary is urban-only in GLSS4* (as GLSS3), with daily supervisor visits to
   illiterate urban households; CONTENTS.org's GLSS4 row does not say so.
7. *The DDI documents no SEC0* file*; the User Guide's SEC0B / SEC0C entries
   are the only description, SEC0B's says "part A and B", and its SEC0C entry
   mislabels =S0SUPNTV= and omits =S0CHKRST= (above).
8. *The data-entry manual does not enumerate keyed fields* (37 / 42 screens
   only), contrary to what the wave notes expected of it.  Which cover-page
   fields were keyed is established here from the files, not from a document.
9. *Keyed in one booklet, not the other*: the FIRST-VISIT replacement
   fields (REPLACES / REPLACED BY / REASON) are printed on both covers and
   keyed only from Part A (=SEC0A.hhrplacs/hhrepby/s0reasns=); the FIRST-VISIT
   dwelling/head/language/interpreter fields likewise.  Not a request --
   the information is already shipped.
10. *Ten household-number mismatches* between the Part A batch (SEC0A / SEC0C)
   and the Part B batch (SEC0B) in ten clusters; 148 duplicate (household,
   visitcd) rows in SEC0B; one =s0result= = 4 in SEC0C; four impossible date
   triplets.
11. *Wiring suggestion (not done; brief forbids edits)*: =interview_date=
    could carry all seven dates on a =visit= level via =melt_visit_intervals=
    from SEC0C (1-3) + de-shifted SEC0B (4-7); no GhanaLSS wave currently
