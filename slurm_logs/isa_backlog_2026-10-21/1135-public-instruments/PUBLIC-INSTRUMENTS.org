* Interpretation and remaining work

The W4 manual (PDF pages24-25) and W5 manual (page20) distinguish nearby moves from staying at the dwelling recorded in previous waves or listing.
W5's sample description (page5) explicitly starts from households interviewed in W4.
This strengthens a prior-wave interpretation of the movement flags; it does not prove which round supplies the prefilled address or complete movement histories between visits.
The W5 manual also retains older date examples (page19) and has an apparently inconsistent routing instruction after the movement answer (page20); this is a reason to retain the questionnaire/data alignment checks.

The questionnaires confirm the movement response meanings (W4 page1; W5 page3).
They do not fill the twenty unknown W5 statuses, establish the physical identity of every changed cluster group, or turn the prefix-only112/5067 into a verified net-of-movers share.
No numeric boundary record, formatter implementation, new policy ruling or data promotion follows from this note.
The source-backed86/6/20 partition and all original limitations retain their own frozen evidence pins.
