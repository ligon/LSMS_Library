*2018-19 numbering mismatch (content matches):* the printed form numbers the
purchase-at-home qty/value CEB04/CEB05 ... in-kind CEB10/CEB11; the shipped
=GSEC15B.dta= calls the same columns CEB06/CEB07 ... CEB012/CEB013 and splits
the market price into CEB14a/b/c.  The wave script reads the .dta names, whose
labels carry the same 7-day questions; the window is unaffected.

*The =[RECALL]= fill.*  2010-11..2015-16 food labels carry the un-substituted
=[RECALL]= / =[PERIOD]= placeholders, the template Uganda/_/CONTENTS.org
discusses for the NON-food GSEC15C.  For food, every printed form fixes the
window at 7 days, and the days-consumed column never exceeds 7 in any of the
five waves -- an item-specific window longer than 7 days would push daily
staples past 7.

So the overlap holds for every column the build reads, and fails for exactly
one column, =h15bq3b=, which carries the only window statement on this wave's
own data.  Its =q3b= numbering follows 2010-11's 3B.

*Days consumed.*  =h15bq3b= is 8 on 2 of 37,645 answered rows (items 104
and 139) and 0 on 5; every other row is 1..7.

*Asks.*  One row per (hh, itmcd) over 2,929 households, with no visit
column, so =n_asks= is 1.

*Rung.*  The record is =variable-label=, not =questionnaire=.  Of the four
pieces of evidence, the =h15bq3b= label is the only statement of the window
made about this wave's data.  The 2005-06 form belongs to another survey
(UNHS) and lacks a column that 2009-10 has.  The DDI is catalog metadata.  The
maintainer statement is an assertion.  Either rung converts under =period==.
