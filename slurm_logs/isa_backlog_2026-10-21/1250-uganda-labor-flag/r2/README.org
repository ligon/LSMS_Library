* Ruling and rule
"Serve wage-only hired rows, following Malawi's precedent: a hired row is kept
on its reported wage, with =PersonDays= NaN."  Malawi's rule is
=Malawi/_/malawi.py:3766-3767=:
#+begin_src python
out = out[out['PersonDays'].notna() & (out['PersonDays'] > 0)
          | (out['source'] == 'hired') & out['Wage'].notna()]
#+end_src
Uganda adopts the wage disjunct only.  =plot_labor_for_wave= now keeps
=PersonDays.notna() | (source == 'hired' & Wage.notna())=.  Malawi's
=PersonDays > 0= half is not adopted, because Uganda serves a "No" flag's zero
as a reported value (#1250).  Family and other rows keep the days-only rule;
they carry no wage.
