* Notes per derivation

** D-FA-01 -- =u='---'=
The brief asked for a =u='Value'= encoding; Uganda has none.  What Uganda does
have is a sentinel that carries less information than =Value= would: ='---'=
means "the unit code was missing OR the code exists but the org table gives it
no label".  The two cases are indistinguishable in the served index.  The raw
code is recoverable only from the source file.

** D-FA-02 -- purchased Price is a median of three market prices in 2018-19 / 2019-20
The 2018+ GSEC15B asks the market price separately for food consumed at home,
away and from own production (CEB14a/b/c).  =food_acquired= collapses them
with a row-wise median (=df.filter(regex='^market').median(axis=1)=) into one
=market= column, which becomes the served =Price= on purchased rows.  Earlier
waves ship one market price.  So =Price= means "the survey's market price" in
six waves and "the median of up to three market prices" in two; nothing in the
frame says which.  Raw inputs recoverable only from the source file.

** D-FA-03 -- home + away folded into purchased
The home / away split is a CONSUMPTION-LOCATION split, folded into one
acquisition source.  Expenditure-only rows (away-from-home meals with no
quantity) are kept with NaN Quantity.  Documented in the DESIGN doc and
data_scheme.yml.

** D-FA-05 -- shared finalizer
Duplicate canonical keys (e.g. two "Other (Specify)" items under one label)
are summed; =Price= is averaged.  A row's =Price= may therefore be a mean of
two survey prices with no flag.

** D-NF-01 -- item-specific recall windows summed
CONTENTS.org:2672-2700 establishes that the source labels carry a CAPI
=[RECALL]= fill that VARIES BY ITEM and that no wave ships the window.  The
served =Expenditure= sums purchased + own-production + gifts over that unknown
window, per item.  This is the closest Uganda case to the GhanaLSS 12B
motivation: two households' rows for different items are on different
windows and nothing in the frame says so.  The gap is recorded as WAITING.

** D-CF-02 -- 2005-06 GPS borrowed from 2009-10
=2005-06/_/data_info.yml= joins =2009_UNPS_Geovars_0910.dta= (the 2009-10
geovar) on HHID and serves its =lat_mod= / =lon_mod= as the 2005-06 cluster
location, on the stated assumption that "cluster location is geographically
stable".  This is an imputation from a different wave, four years later,
of jittered coordinates.  Only the YAML comment says so; CONTENTS.org mentions
2005-06 cluster_features only as a phantom-row TODO (:1763).

** D-SM-01 -- synthetic =v=
An INDEX VALUE computed from GPS by rounding to a 0.05-degree grid.  The
=@= prefix is the only disclosure in the served frame.  Recoverable from the
geovar file, not from the served table (rounding).

** D-HR-01 -- Age, and why the fractional branch cannot fire
=age_handler(age, d, m, y, interview_year=INTERVIEW_YEAR)= is called with no
=interview_date=, so =final_interview_date= stays None and =dob_age= is never
computed (local_tools.py:2871-2930).  The decision tree that remains is:
plausible reported age -> =int(age)=; else valid year of birth ->
=INTERVIEW_YEAR - year=; else NaN.  Consequences: (a) the served Age is always
an integer for Uganda -- CLAUDE.md's "fractional years when DOB is available"
does not apply here; (b) the day and month of birth are cleaned by
=_age_helpers= and then unused; (c) the fallback uses the wave's nominal year,
not the household's interview date, so a person born in December and
interviewed in the following calendar year gets an age one year too high
relative to the interview.  A =Derivation= key could name the rule ("reported
else year-difference") but not which branch fired per row.  This is an
inference from reading the call path; it was not executed.

** D-PF-01 / D-PF-02 / D-PF-03 -- Area in hectares, AreaUnit says acres
=Area = area_acres * 0.404686= and =AreaUnit = 'acres'= are written in
adjacent lines (uganda.py:920-924).  The docstring states both facts
("Area (hectares, float), AreaUnit (str, always 'acres')") without noticing
they contradict.  A consumer who trusts =AreaUnit= is off by 2.47x.
=SelfReportedArea= is also in hectares and has no unit column at all.  Which
of the two raw candidates (GPS vs estimate) produced =Area= is not flagged.
The 2 500-acre clamp is a judgement call with one motivating observation.

** D-PF-06 -- PlotOwned is three rules
Acquire-code buckets differ by wave (the 2018+ merged code list adds 6/7 owned,
8/9 not), AGSEC2B parcels are False by construction, and a certificate
overrides everything.  Raw inputs recoverable only from the source.

** D-PF-07 / D-PF-08 -- ErosionProtection derived two ways
Numeric-code waves: 8 -> False, else True.  String waves (2009-10, 2010-11):
non-placeholder -> True, placeholder -> NA, never False.  So a "no facility"
answer is False in 2011+ and NA in 2009-11.  Same column, two meanings.

** D-CP-01 -- 99999
Exact-value strip, wave-scoped by the colmap.  3 077 of the 3 097 sentinel
rows carry no other measure and are dropped by the "no measure" filter, so
the served effect is mostly a row count change.  CONTENTS records the
before/after.

** D-CP-02 -- 2015-16 x100
The only SCALE correction in Uganda.  Defensible and measured (three
independent lines of evidence in the script block), but the correctness
of the served number depends on a build-time assertion about the raw
median, i.e. on the file the World Bank happens to ship.  The registry
design needs a slot for "the rule is conditional on the input's state".

** D-CP-03 -- KgFactor
Served raw except =0 -> NaN=.  Garbage values (=2018=, 260 000 on a Kg row)
are left in the served column by design; the framework's
=_screen_reported_factors= rejects them at read time inside =harvest_kg=
without altering the served column.  Wired for 2018-19 only, although every
wave ships the field (CONTENTS.org:1865-1898) -- so =KgFactor= is NaN for
reasons that differ by wave (not wired vs not reported).

** D-CP-05 -- intercropped
The served boolean is =seed-use == No=, not intercropping.  CONTENTS records
48-53% agreement with the true crop-stand question and leaves it as a TODO
because rewiring moves data.  Of every derivation in this file this is the one
whose served meaning is furthest from its name.

** D-CP-08 / D-PI-05 -- three reducers in one row
When duplicate index tuples collapse, one row can carry a summed Quantity, a
=first()= KgFactor (a RATE, so summing would be wrong) and a
=reduce_to_agreed= label that was blanked.  The derivation differs PER CELL,
not per row.  Null =plot_id= rows are deleted outright by the groupby.

** D-PI-02 -- fertilizer unit assumed Kg
No unit is asked for organic / inorganic fertilizer; the builder stamps
='Kg'=.  The served =u= is indistinguishable from a reported Kg.

** D-LS-02 -- ValuePerAnimal
A head-weighted mean across roster lines that map to one species (e.g.
exotic and indigenous cattle), falling back to a plain mean, of a question
that changes from "if you sold one today" (2009-11, ~96% non-null) to
"average value of each sold" (2011+, ~21% non-null).  Neither the
weighting nor the concept switch is visible in the served frame; the
data_scheme comment discloses the switch, the docstring the weighting.

** D-AN-01 -- the =<= 0 -> NA= guard reaches Age_months
=_anthro_num= is applied to =Age_months= too, so a 0-month-old is served NA.
Not mentioned anywhere.

** D-EA-01..03 -- Earnings
Cash + in-kind, first + second job, summed over all persons in the household,
over a 7-day window that is stated only in a script comment, and only for
2005-06 .. 2015-16.  2018-19 / 2019-20 sum =s8q78 + s8q80= with no comment on
window or cash/in-kind coverage; whether those fields are the same concept is
not established anywhere in the repo.  =data_scheme.yml= declares
=Earnings: float= and nothing else.  Second-strongest 12B analogue after
D-NF-01: a monetary column whose window is undisclosed at the API and may
differ by wave.

** D-EI-01 -- profits / losses
=max(revenue - costs, 0)= and =-min(..., 0)= after summing each component over
enterprises.  A household with one profitable and one loss-making enterprise
has the loss netted into the profit before the clip.  =data_scheme.yml=
declares no columns for this table.

** D-IN-01 -- income
Sum of two derived tables; zero totals are DELETED (no row), so absence of a
household means "no income" or "not surveyed" indistinguishably.

** D-SH-01 / D-SH-02 -- shock Year and Onset
The 2009-10 .. 2011-12 questionnaires record a shock's month and duration but
no year, so the year is INFERRED by a branchy rule from the interview date and
the duration; =Onset= (months since the shock started) is then a difference of
two dates both forced to the 1st of the month.  None of this is documented
outside the scripts; CONTENTS.org has no shocks entry.  The rule is identical
across the three waves (copy-pasted), and from 2013-14 the year is reported.

** D-MF-01 / D-MF-02 -- MonthsInadequate
Two source shapes (letter string vs long Yes/No rows) reduced to one count;
households absent from the month file get 0, not NA, in 2011+.

** D-NU-01 -- nutrition
A matrix product over every kg-row of =food_quantities= (native-unit rows are
dropped without notice) and an FCT whose missing nutrients were set to 0.
The served nutrient is therefore a lower bound in two independent ways.  The
raw inputs are the whole food table; no per-row provenance is possible.

** What would not fit a per-row =Derivation= column + registry

1. Per-CELL, not per-row, derivations.  A collapsed =crop_production= row
   carries a summed Quantity, a =first()= KgFactor and a =reduce_to_agreed=
   Unit_sold at once (D-CP-08); =plot_inputs= sums, maxes and firsts in one
   row (D-PI-05); =livestock= sums heads and weight-averages the value
   (D-LS-01/02).  One key per row cannot name three rules.
2. INDEX VALUES.  =u='---'=, =u='Unknown'=, =condition='unknown_condition'=,
   =j='n/a'=, =v='@+0.35,+32.60'= are derivations of the KEY, not of a column;
   a column keyed to the row cannot annotate the row's own address.
3. Per-row branch selection with no per-row trace.  Age (reported vs
   year-difference), Area (GPS vs estimate), Height (lying vs standing),
   ValuePerAnimal (weighted vs plain mean), purchased Price (single vs median
   of three) -- a registry key names the rule; recovering WHICH candidate fired
   needs a second per-row field or the raw file.
4. Inputs that are a SET OF ROWS elsewhere.  =shocks.Onset= needs the
   household's GSEC1 interview date; =income= needs two other tables;
   =nutrition= is a matrix product over every food row and an FCT; 2005-06 GPS
   comes from a different wave's file.  "A callable that retrieves the exact
   raw inputs" is fine, but the inputs are not a row.
5. Conditional-on-data rules.  The 2015-16 x100 is applied only if the raw
   median is under 10 000 (D-CP-02); the recall window of D-NF-01 is unknown
   per item.  The registry needs to say "conditional" and "unknown" as first-
   class states, not just name a formula.
6. Derivations that are DELETIONS.  Zero incomes, zero asset values, null
   plot_id rows, no-measure rows, rows whose raw code is missing from an org
   table (D-XX-01): the served frame has no row to hang a
   =Derivation= on.
