* 2. Notes where one line was not enough

** Rows 1-2: the EHCVM purchased row is a 12B-like pair
The EHCVM s07b module asks, per item: q03 total quantity consumed in the last
7 days, q04 the part of it from own production, q05 the part received as a
gift, q06 when the item was last bought, q07a/b the quantity and unit of THAT
last purchase, q08 what was paid for it.  The three countries wire Quantity
= q03, Produced = q04, Expenditure = q08.  =food_acquired_to_canonical= then
serves, on the same row, =Quantity = q03 - q04= (a 7-day consumption residual
that also contains gifts, because q05 is never read) beside =Expenditure = q08=
(the value of one purchase of an unstated size, made at an unstated time).
=food_prices= = Expenditure / Quantity over that row is therefore the ratio of
two different quantities.  The raw inputs (q07a, q05, and the un-clipped q03)
are recoverable only from the source file.  Nothing in the served table, the
three CONTENTS.org files or the data_scheme comments says so.

Evidence status: CONFIRMED from the source files.  The variable labels of the
three s07b .dta files (read as metadata from the local DVC cache; no script
run) say q08 is 'Valeur (en FCFA) du [PRODUIT] achete la derniere fois' and
q07a 'quantite ... achete la derniere fois', while q03a is the 7-day consumed
total; the Togo questionnaire sheet is headed 'Consommation alimentaire des 7
derniers jours et achat des 30 derniers jours'.  So the served row pairs a
7-DAY quantity with a LAST-PURCHASE (up to 30 days back) value of an
unrecorded quantity.  In-repo readings of the same field conflict: Senegal's
script and the retired Togo script read it as the labels do; Burkina Faso's
scripts and CONTENTS read it as 'total expenses', and the add-feature skill
template wires it as Expenditure without comment (Togo/_/recall.yml also cites
the retired Togo script as its questionnaire evidence).  The Burkina Faso
reading is the one the labels refute.  A second, smaller selection on the same
row: u is s07bq03b (unit) alone; the size modifier s07bq03c ('Taille', e.g.
sachet size) is not read, so units of different sizes share one u.  Other EHCVM
countries (Senegal, Mali, Niger, Burkina Faso, CotedIvoire) are outside this
group but wire the same YAML.

** Row 3: age_handler is one shared derivation invoked by three countries
The country only decides the sentinel convention (-1 vs 9999) and supplies the
DOB components; the selection rule (fractional DOB age when it agrees with the
report, else integer report; 15th of the month when the day is missing;
year-arithmetic fallback) is the library's.  The reported age is not
recoverable from the served Age once the DOB branch fires.  A registry key
would have to name the branch taken PER ROW (reported / dob-fractional /
year-math), which is exactly the case the brief flags as not fitting a
single-key design.

** Row 14: the Panama 1997 threshold is applied to QUANTITIES too.  =>= 9999 ->
NaN= runs over ga106a and ga110a as well as the money field ga106c, so a genuine
quantity of 9,999 or more in a small unit (grams, millilitres) is nulled along
with the 9999.x sentinels.  CONTENTS:885-900 reports sentinel SHARES per
column, not a check that no real value sits above the threshold; 2003/2008 use
99999 and are less exposed.

** Rows 16-17 vs 27: Panama and Guatemala resolve the same instrument shape
in opposite ways.  Both surveys give ONE non-purchased quantity and several
yes/no source flags.  Panama emits the whole quantity once per flag (so a
two-flag item is served twice) and defaults an unflagged row to 'produced';
Guatemala emits it once under a priority order and defaults to 'other'.  A
=Feature('food_acquired')= over the two therefore pools quantities that are
double-counted in one country and single-counted in the other, with the
default source pointing the opposite way.

** Row 31: Cambodia's quantity split is an imputation that manufactures two
numbers from one.  The survey records one quantity and two values; the
served purchased/produced quantities are that one quantity times a value
share.  The raw total is recoverable as the row-sum over s, but the split
itself is invented, and Expenditure for s='produced' is the survey's valuation
of own consumption (s05q06), not money spent.

** Rows 33-37: Serbia.  The served Quantity/Expenditure are 7-day sums of
daily diary entries; Price is a median of daily unit values (an extra reducer
that the country writes although food_prices is API-derived).  'dinar' rows are
relabelled u='Value' WITHOUT the Panama/GhanaLSS =Quantity := Expenditure=
convention (Quantity stays NaN), so the corpus has two shapes under one
sentinel.  panel_weight is wired to popweight, which the DDI defines as
hhweight x hhsize, i.e. a person-level weight; after API-time normalisation the
two weight columns differ by the household-size gradient.

** Row 42 / 47: instance and acquisition sums.  Iraq 2012 sums Purchase Price
over physical instances, so the column is a household total for the item, not a
price; 2006-07 assets stay on the YAML path unsummed -- the same column means
different things in the two waves.  Guyana sums per-acquisition rows whose
semantics (increment vs restated stock) the source does not settle; 372 of 658
multi-row blocks carry identical quantities and are over-counted if they are
restatements.

** Row 48: MODE reducers.  Guyana and Peru project household rows onto the
cluster by majority (with tie -> NA).  This is a selection rule deciding which
household's value represents the cluster; it is the only place in the group
where a reducer other than first/sum is declared, and it is documented in both
countries.

** Rows 59, 41, 53: constants served as data.  EthiopiaRHS weights = 1,
Rural = 'Rural', strata = v; Iraq 2012 MonthsAway = 0; Kosovo interview year =
2000.  Each is a constant chosen by the maintainer rather than a survey value.
They are honest (all documented in docstrings / scheme) but nothing in the
served frame distinguishes them from a recorded 1.0 or 0.

** Rows 62-65: EthiopiaRHS serves several SOURCE-side derivations verbatim
(pre-converted hectares with a native-unit label beside them; crop production
already summed over plots; herd value and TLU built from head counts with
IFPRI's factors; consumption aggregates and a poverty flag).  These are not
library derivations, but a per-row Derivation column would need a way to say
"derived upstream, factors unknown to the library", and GH #737 shows the
library's own TLU factors disagree with the source's.

** The Woreda -> PA broadcast (row 63) means a "PA price" can be the same
number for every PA in a Woreda; a consumer joining community_prices to
food_acquired on v will see spatial variation only at Woreda level for R1-R4.

** The cases where the served number is furthest from the raw answer
1. EHCVM food_acquired purchased row (rows 1-2, Togo/Benin/Guinea-Bissau):

   included, clipped at 0) and Expenditure is the value of one last purchase of
   an unrecorded size (confirmed from the .dta variable labels).  A price
   ratio over that row divides two different things.  Undisclosed anywhere a
   user would look, and read two different ways by two country configs.

   Expenditure is a valuation, not spending.  Disclosed in scheme/docstring.

   the wrong unit).  Disclosed.

   replaces the reported integer when they agree; which branch fired is not

** What would not fit a per-row =Derivation= key + registry
- age_handler (row 3): the branch differs per row and depends on the
  agreement test; the key would have to carry the branch AND the tolerance.
- Cambodia's value-share split (row 31): the derivation is a function of two
  sibling rows (purchased and produced share one raw quantity); a key on one
  row cannot express that the other row is its complement.
- Panama's per-flag duplication (row 16): the derivation is "this row is one
  of k copies"; the multiplicity is a property of the group, not the row.
- Reducers over duplicates (rows 22, 28, 35, 42, 47, 48): the served value
  depends on how many source rows collapsed and which reducer ran; a key can
  name the reducer but not the n, and for Guyana the semantics of the sum is
  itself unknown.
- Source-side derivations (rows 62-65): the library cannot name the factors
  (IFPRI TLU weights, price vectors, Landconversion.SPS) because it never sees
  them; a registry entry would be "derived upstream, method unknown".
- Constants served as data (rows 41, 53, 59): representable, but the
  registry needs a kind for "not a survey value at all", which is not in the
  brief's list.
- Row 2 is the pure 12B shape: no arithmetic at all, just two raw fields
  with different windows on one row.  A Derivation column keyed on
  Expenditure could name it ("value of last purchase, <=30 days, quantity
  q07a not served"), which is probably the strongest argument for the column
  being nullable per CELL rather than per row.
