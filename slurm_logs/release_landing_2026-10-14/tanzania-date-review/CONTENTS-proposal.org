* Known warm interview-date loss (GH #798, 2026-10-15)

The four =2008-15/= UPD rounds store interview dates as space-separated
timestamp strings in the raw country parquet; 2019-20 and 2020-21 store
ISO timestamps with a =T= separator. On development =3914d8804= the raw
cache contains 22,433 rows across six waves, including six originally
null dates. Cold output serves 22,427 rows; an ordinary warm read
serves only 16,534, losing all 1,184 valid dates from 2019-20 and all
4,709 from 2020-21.

Read-only replay shows normalization and the sample-=v= join preserve
every raw row. Whole-column declared datetime coercion infers the older
format and nulls those 5,893 later-wave dates; the all-null-row filter
deletes them as well as the six original nulls. Mixed-format/per-wave
parsing reproduces the complete cold result after the same null filter.
This is not the Extended/Refresh sub-panel split, UPHI back-casting, or
a new visit-key interpretation.

The same two missing waves were reported in #798 before v0.14.0; the
demonstrated normalization, join, coercion, finalization and writing
functions are unchanged across v0.14.0 and this release. Exact older
counts and dependency environments were not rebuilt. The raw cache
and its six-wave footer remain complete and unchanged. Keep #798 open
and distinguish raw-cache coverage from served warm API coverage; no
parsing-policy or panel-grain change is implemented here.

On a separate complete warm-cache copy, concatenating ordinary
=interview_date(waves=[wave])= calls for all six waves, using
=Country('Tanzania', preload_panel_ids=False)=, reproduced the exact
22,427-row cold result without changing cache files. This is a bounded
workaround for the verified cache/environment, not an all-wave fix.
