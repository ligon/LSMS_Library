their combined behaviour, then release from =master=. Proposed version:

branches are not merge instructions. The initial release train is 18 active

release gates below pass; GitHub Release and PyPI contain the intended version;

records final SHAs, results, remaining issues, and cache status.

the archived lane reports for reviewed heads, actual merges and validation.
