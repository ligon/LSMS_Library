** 2007 plot identity and area-unit provenance (GH #879)

The 2007 land roster combines r1m12a1 (owned, 3,685 plots), r1m12a2
(rented/borrowed in, 269), and r1m12a3 (rented/lent out, 20).  The source
=plotcode= restarts within each subsection: 231 =(hhid, plotcode)= keys
collide between owned and rented-in sections.  =2007/_/plot_features.py=
therefore prefixes plot_id with tenure (=owned-1=, =rented_in-1=, ...),
preserving 3,974 plots with unique =(t, i, plot_id)=.  Do not strip the tag.

The source area is in sotka (=1/100 hectare=).  The script has served
=Area = q3 / 100= in hectares since GH #879 (=dedb1c7a7=, before v0.14.0);
=AreaUnit='sotka'= preserves the ORIGINAL survey unit, not the unit of
served Area.  This follows =lsms_library/data_info.yml='s canonical contract.
The country registry's comment "Area is in sotka (1/100 ha)" contradicts
that implementation and must not be read as the served-unit contract.

The October 2026 cold corpus reproduced 3,974 rows with Area total
882.38 hectares; the prior shared country aggregate held total 88,238,
while its wave parquet already matched the new country frame exactly by
fingerprint.  This is evidence of an older stale aggregate, not a new
conversion introduced by the v0.15.0 train.  The total is a validation
checksum, not a nationally weighted land estimate.
