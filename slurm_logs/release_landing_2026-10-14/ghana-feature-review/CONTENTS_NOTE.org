** Feature assembly drops null-key food rows separately from visit summation (2026-10-14)

=Country('GhanaLSS').food_acquired()= retains the integer =visit= axis
and is unique at that grain.  =Feature('food_acquired')(['GhanaLSS'])=
removes =visit= to reach its canonical cross-country grain, then sums
Quantity/Expenditure over the resulting duplicate keys.  That ordinary
visit aggregation is distinct from the null-key deletion below; it does
not standardize the recall windows documented above.

On frozen source =7fa05179=, the country call serves 5,340,606 rows and
the Feature call serves 1,704,880.  Of the reduction, 3,622,254 rows are
combined through additive aggregation of non-null keys.  Another 13,472
rows are deleted because the grouping keys contain nulls:

| wave    | null key | deleted rows | Quantity checksum | Expenditure checksum |
|---------+----------+--------------+-------------------+----------------------|
| 1987-88 | v        |           54 |              8370 |                 8370 |
| 1988-89 | v        |           38 | 5182.017543859649 |    5182.017543859649 |
| 2016-17 | u        |        13380 |            3379.8 |              6720.55 |

These are raw-column *audit checksums*, not kilograms or comparable
economic totals: units, currencies and recall windows differ.  The
GLSS7 set includes price-only produced rows; a deleted-row count is not
a count of non-null quantities.  The country-native records remain
available.  Independent in-memory replay from the raw food/sample
parquets reproduces the mask and Feature row count; =math.fsum= of the
retained input and output agrees for both additive columns.

The collapse mechanism predates v0.14.0: =feature.py= and the relevant
row-selector source are identical at that tag, pre-train =452302ad= and
=7fa05179=.  Exact row counts on a rebuilt historical Country snapshot
were not measured.  GH #847 explicitly scoped its change to reporting
this class, not correcting every country.  Its closure does not mean
these GhanaLSS rows are preserved in Feature assembly.

This also qualifies Trap 9's "armed and waiting" description: no new
Country duplicate is needed to activate the Feature collapse, because
removing =visit= creates duplicate keys.  The 22 null-unit rows discussed
under the 1998-99 wave reducer are a separate upstream loss, not the
cohort counted here.  Choosing a source-supported unit/cluster treatment
or changing assembly's null-key policy requires its own reviewed fix;
this note changes neither identifiers nor measures.
