* Known warm interview-date loss (GH #798, 2026-10-15)

Mali combines EAC-I (2014-15/2017-18) and EHCVM (2018-19/2021-22).
The complete raw country =interview_date= cache stores EAC-I dates as
=YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS= strings and EHCVM dates as =YYYY-MM-DD= strings.
On development =3914d8804=, cold output contains 24,939 rows across all
four waves, but an ordinary warm read serves only 12,194 EAC-I rows.
Whole-column declared datetime coercion infers one format and nulls
exactly the 6,602 and 6,143 EHCVM dates; the subsequent all-null-row
filter removes them. Index normalization preserves every raw row.
Per-wave and mixed-format parsing reproduce the full cold result in a
read-only diagnostic; no parsing-policy change is implemented here.

This is the same warm loss recorded in #798 on September 9, before
v0.14.0. Its October 14 cold-only "no longer reproduces" narrowing was
premature. The raw parquet remains complete and unchanged; raw cache
coverage and served warm API coverage must be reported separately.
Keep #798 open. The =data_scheme.yml= comment claiming EHCVM household
visits across multiple vagues and =groupby.first()= also conflicts with
this file's one-vague-per-grappe convention and the signed current
reducer. That documentation tension is separate from the demonstrated
datetime loss; it does not authorize a visit-key or reducer change.
