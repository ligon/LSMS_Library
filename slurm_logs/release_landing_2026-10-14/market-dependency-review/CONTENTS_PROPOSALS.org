* Append to GhanaSPS/_/CONTENTS.org

** Region-market dependency remains missing (2026-10-14 release audit; GH #140)

Cluster identity is available through =sample()=, as documented above; it is
not a geographic =Region= lookup. The current =sample= exposes no =Region=
column (=2017-18='s source =region= is mapped to =strata=), and neither the
country scheme nor its wave configs declares =cluster_features=. The frozen
release audit at =7fa05179= records =market='Region'= calls for
=food_expenditures=, =food_prices= and =food_quantities= raising
="cluster_features returned no data for any wave."=; =Feature= warns and
excludes GhanaSPS from those variants. It records the same exception for
=household_characteristics(market='Region')=. Ordinary declared-table calls
succeed. The relevant market helpers and declarations are unchanged since
=v0.14.0=; this is a retained dependency gap.

The earlier =other_features.py= TODO says, "For now, all of 2013-14 has Ghana
set as its region." That is not an implemented fallback in the current API.
It remains a historical claim to reconcile, alongside the documented wave-2
source geography gap. Do not recreate the earlier false claim that cluster
identity is unavailable, infer Region from =strata= without source review, or
fabricate a national market to hide the dependency failure. GH #110 was closed
as superseded by the still-open umbrella [[https://github.com/ligon/LSMS_Library/issues/140][#140]],
whose plan already includes =cluster_features=.

* Append to Panama/_/CONTENTS.org

** Region-market lookup is not wired (2026-10-14 release audit)

The current =sample()= supplies =v= from =upm= in all three waves, but exposes
no =Region= column; neither the country scheme nor the wave configs declares
=cluster_features=. At frozen source =7fa05179= the Feature audit records
=food_expenditures=, =food_prices= and =food_quantities= with
=market='Region'= failing for Panama with
="cluster_features returned no data for any wave."=. =Feature= warns and
excludes Panama from those variants, while ordinary declared-table calls
succeed. The relevant helpers and declarations are unchanged since =v0.14.0=.
This is a missing geography dependency, not missing cluster identity or proof
that the raw files lack geographic variables.

Any future mapping needs a source-backed definition of Region. In ENV-97 the
planning-region domains exclude indigenous and hard-to-access areas, which
have their own domains, and their boundaries "do not coincide with the
Collection Regions created ad hoc for fieldwork" (quoted in the universe
section above). Neither provinces, study domains nor fieldwork regions should
be silently substituted for one another. The retained =panama.regional_data=
helper does not register a live Region table.
