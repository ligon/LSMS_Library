## 2. Idiosyncrasies (Benin, from `_/CONTENTS.org` and found here)

**From CONTENTS, all bearing on this change:**
- **EHCVM key.** `i = (grappe, menage)`, and each grappe is fielded in one vague. 2018-19 `i` = `benin.i` = grappe + menage:03d.
- **The `E2_` prefix.** 2021-22 `i` is `E2_`-prefixed (`2021-22/_/mapping.py:ID_PREFIX`, GH #1697) because 6,994 of its 8,032 keys recur. This prefix is what makes the crosswalk free of chains and rename-onto-occupied by construction, so I kept it.
- **`ehcvm_individu.age` was set from 2018-19 for panel members.** The age checks therefore use the raw answers: `s01q04a`, else the interview year less `s01q03c`.
- **2021-22 `s01` lists 6,138 people who have left** (`s01q00aa` = non). They are excluded from the household-size comparison, as from `household_roster` (#1565 is still open).
- **`s00q07d` "separates nothing".** Confirmed: it is Oui on all 6,878 linked keys, on the 116 recurring Non-panel keys and on 2 new keys.
- **All 670 grappes recur**, with the same GPS fix.
- **`panel_weight` = `poids`.** No longitudinal weight is shipped, and a family ruling is pending. I left it unchanged.
- **`s16bq05` is the gift question in both waves.** CONTENTS recorded the 2018-19 defect; it is now corrected and the passage carries a dated note.
- **`interview_date`'s `visit` level is `int64` cold and `Int64` warm.** This is the known CLAUDE.md quirk and is identical on base.
- **Contradiction check:** CONTENTS's figures reproduce exactly (6,889 flagged Panel, 11 with no key, 116 recurring Non-panel, 94.8% head sex). CONTENTS gave 61.5% for "sex and age 1-6 older"; my +1..+5 window gives 59.4%. That is a different window, not a contradiction.

**New, added to CONTENTS:**
- **Benin had no `_/Makefile`.** Adding one changes the build route: `Wave.grab_data` then runs `make -s ../<wave>/_/<table>.parquet` for every script table, so every wave script needs a rule. The skill and AGENTS.md now say so.
- **No 2021-22 file records a 2018-19 id.** The cover has `PanelHH`, `s00q07d` and `s00q07e`, and no `s00q07f` or `grappe_EHCVM1`. `hhid` in `ehcvm_menage`, `ehcvm_welfare` and `ehcvm_individu` is a 2021-22 id. What does point back is the pre-loaded roster: `s01q00aa`, with `membres__id` equal to 2018-19's `s01q00a`.
- **The 116, the 11 and the 96.** The 116 recurring keys flagged Non panel have pre-loaded rosters. The 11 flagged Panel answer `s00q07d` = Non. 96 of the 1,027 new keys have pre-loaded members.
