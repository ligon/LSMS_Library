| =api/catalog/{idno}=                        | 200                | 200                 | 200             |
| =api/catalog/{idno}/data_files=             | 200                | 200 (128: 3 files)  | 200             |
| =api/datasets/{id}=                         | 400 ACCESS-DENIED  | 400 IDNO-NOT-FOUND  | 400 ACCESS-DENIED |
| =api/resources/{idno}=                      | 400 ACCESS-DENIED  | 400 ACCESS-DENIED   | 400 ACCESS-DENIED |
| =catalog/{id}/download/{rid}= (web)         | 200, file          | 200, file           | --              |

So: the key is *validated* (a real key gets a different refusal from a
bogus one), but it gates only metadata; =api/resources= is refused even
to a keyed user, so =data_access._find_stata_zip_url= cannot be used on
this host; and every *download* is anonymous.  Nothing in the library
consumes the key today, and nothing should until the access-class
design (=SkunkWorks/holder_gated_access.org=, design only) lands.

* The unlisted files, and why they are legitimate
The catalog pages link only two AHIES resources on entry 128 (513, the
2024 Stata zip; 544, the study documentation PDF) and one on 119 (465).
The download endpoint =catalog/{id}/download/{rid}= is not scoped to
the catalog id: a scan of rids 440-599 served 114 files, most from
unrelated GSS studies (2021 PHC, MICS, DHS, IBES, tourism surveys), and
among them the remaining AHIES files.  The six files used are exactly
the six the v2.1 study documentation lists under "Files Description"
(3 x =AHIES Q1-Q4_Rev_20250827= person files, 3 x =SEC567_edt= household
files), so the scan recovered the documented dataset, not stray copies.

Resolved the same day: EL's *logged-in* view of entry 128's "Get
Microdata" tab lists 21 data-file entries -- the six datasets in each of
STATA / SPSS / CSV, plus the 2022 person file twice under STATA (rid 512
as a zip, rid 508 as the bare =.dta=, identical size) -- i.e. rids 505-514
and 545-556 exactly as the scan found them.  The 2024 STATA file (513) is
the odd one out: GSS filed it under "Other Materials", which is why it was
the only data file visible without login.  Nothing was hidden; the web
listing is login-gated while the download endpoint is not.

* Survey design (from the study docs and the files)
- 10,800 households in 600 EAs (18 per EA; 304 urban / 296 rural EAs)
  drawn from the 2021 PHC frame; quarterly panel, Jan 2022 - Dec 2024;
  CAPI; 16 regions.
- Person files: Sections 1-4 (demographics + migration, education,
  health, economic activity) plus derived labour-status dummies, =BMI=,
  =region=, =urbrur=, =regdist=, and per-quarter =hh_weight= /
  =pop_weight=.  Column prefixes: s1a 19, s1b 11, s2a 101, s2b 5, s3a 32,
  s3b 7, s3c 6, s4a 130, s4b 41, s4c 15, s4d 14, s4e 24, s4f 10, s4g 272,
  s4h 107 (2024; the three years share the 838-column layout).
- SEC567 files: Section 5 (FAO FIES, 8 items, 12-month recall),
  Section 6 (respondent identification), Section 7 (housing: dwelling,
  tenure, rent, utilities, dwelling characteristics).  2022's carries
  extra =WTPP*= / =WTHH_*= weight columns (2022Q4 only; =WTHH_2= equals
  the person file's =hh_weight=) and 16 Section 10/11B columns
  (=s10q1-3=, =s10bq1-2= transfer screens, =s11bq1-11= miscellaneous
  income amounts) populated 2022Q1-Q3 only -- the one sliver of
  Sections 8-13 that IS shipped, corrected 2026-09-17 from an earlier
  "16 =s1*= columns" misreading; 2023's carries no weight at all;
  2024's carries =hhwt_q9=, =wtpp_q10=, =hhwt_q10=.
- Keys.  =(cluster, HholdID)= is the stable panel household id (600
  clusters x 1-18); =hhid= is quarter-scoped (=qtr= + 3-digit cluster +
  2-digit household; 6 digits for qtr 1-9, 7 for 10-12).  =qtr= runs
  1-12 over the panel (2022Q1 = 1 ... 2024Q4 = 12); =quarter= is the
  string =YYYYQn=.  =new_pid= is the person id (=personid= agrees 99.8%);
  0 duplicates and 0 nulls on =(cluster, HholdID, quarter, new_pid)= in
  all three files.  Q1 x Q2 2024 join on =(cluster, HholdID, new_pid)=:
  45,890 matched of 47,963; sex agrees 100%, age within 1 year 99.8%.
- Panel retention (quarters observed per =(cluster, HholdID)=): 2022
  {4: 10,042; 3: 534; 2: 154; 1: 42}; 2023 {4: 9,782; 3: 288; 2: 90;
  1: 81}; 2024 {4: 9,637; 3: 222; 2: 28; 1: 28}.
- Weights.  =hh_weight= is constant within household-quarter and sums
  to ~8.2M households per quarter (2021 PHC: 8.35M); =pop_weight= sums
  to ~32M.  *Anomaly:* 2023Q3 =hh_weight= mean is 402.3 against 720-854
  in every other quarter -- half scale.  Recorded, not corrected; the
  library's within-wave mean-1 normalisation would hide it, so check
  the raw parquet, not the API.
- =s1aq2= relationship-to-head has 17 codes including =Househelp= (16)
  and =Not member anymore= (17); =s1aq2x= is a corrected duplicate.

* What is NOT distributed
Sections 8-13 of the instrument -- own-produce consumption (8),
household expenditure, less-/frequently purchased items (9), transfers
(10), miscellaneous income and expenditure (11), assets and durables
(12), non-farm enterprises (13) -- appear in no published file.  The
survey's namesake content is absent.  The questionnaire in hand is the
C4 evidence for an =asked-not-distributed= verdict on =food_acquired=
and the other consumption features; EL is asking GSS for these
sections.

A candidate "AHIES consumption" file (=231030impact_consumptiondata.zip=)
was checked and is Togo (Savanes / Tandjoare, Novissi =nov_*= columns,
9,511 respondents), not Ghana.  Not used.

* Decisions taken (EL, 2026-09-17)
- Country name =GhanaAHIES= (sibling of =GhanaLSS=, =GhanaSPS=).
- Grain: =t= is the quarter, =YYYYQn= (12 values); wave folders per
  calendar year (=2022/=, =2023/=, =2024/=) each holding four rounds
  via =wave_folder_map= (the Tanzania =2008-15= / Nigeria pp-ph
  pattern; script path).  Forced by =sample=: one =weight= per
  =(i, t)=, and =hh_weight= differs by quarter, so a year-grained =t=
  would collapse four weights to one.
- =i= = ={cluster}-{HholdID}= (stable across quarters); =pid= = =new_pid=.
