* What this is

Section 3 of =PLAN_871.md=.  Measures, per =(country, table, wave)= cell, the
difference between what core serves *today* -- a per-column
=groupby(...).first()= composite -- and the proposed row-coherent
=groupby(...).nth(0).sort_index()= selection, at the two *build*-path collapse
sites:

- *Site 1* =country._normalize_dataframe_index= (declared-index collapse)
- *Site 2* =country._collapse_to_cluster_grain= (household -> cluster projection)

*Site 3 (=feature._collapse_duplicate_index=) was NOT run* -- the optional,
non-blocking item in the plan, skipped for time.  Its groups are cross-wave and
are formed after =droplevel=, so nothing below sizes it.

Script: =bench/grain_census_871.py= (read-only; no DVC, no network, *no build*).
Run: =PYTHONPATH=<worktree> LSMS_BUILD_WORKERS=1 python bench/grain_census_871.py=,
=multiprocessing.Pool(40)= over files, largest-first.  Wall time 5.7 s.

* Method, and what it can and cannot see

The stamped =lsms_grain_audit= (read with =local_tools.read_parquet_grain_audit=
off ={cache}/{C}/var/{table}.parquet=) carries counts only -- no per-column
and no per-row detail -- so it can give a cell list, never the diff.  The diff is
computed on the *pre-collapse* wave parquet
={cache}/{C}/{wave}/_/{table}.parquet= (written by =Wave.grab_data= on the
YAML route, by =local_tools.to_parquet= from =_/{table}.py= on the script route;
both are pre-Site-1 and pre-Site-2).

*A cell with no pre-collapse wave parquet is reported =uncensused=, never
rebuilt.*

Cells censused: *every cached pre-collapse wave parquet* (915 files), plus every
=household_roster= and =cluster_features= cell the config declares whether cached
or not -- that is where the =served_na_new= cost lives.  Sweeping the wave
parquets rather than the stamps is deliberate: only 388 =var/= parquets exist,
Uganda's =var/= holds two tables, and a cell whose =(country, table)= has no
=var/= parquet would otherwise be *invisible* rather than uncensused.  The stamp
index is still read (55 reports, 51 of them on cells the census
also diffed) and is used as the *check* described below.

Five deliberate choices in the diff, each of which changes the numbers:

1. *The build's per-wave =t= filter is replayed* (=grab_data= country.py:1646,
   =build_wave= :3743-3748).  Without it a script-path parquet shared by the
   quarters of one folder is counted whole, once per quarter.
2. *Missing-value spelling is normalised before comparing.*  =first()= and
   =nth(0)= can return the same missing value in different clothes (=None= vs
   =pd.NA= vs =nan=) on an object column.  A naive =astype(str)= diff -- the
   audit's own stringify -- then calls every such row "changed": corpus-wide that
   is *rows_changed_raw = 1,211,233* against a true
   1,025 (=Burkina_Faso/shocks/2014= alone: 193,115 of
   194,246 rows, in a cell whose audited destruction is one row).  =isna()= is the
   ground truth; the census masks on it.  Both numbers are in the CSV
   (=rows_changed=, =rows_changed_raw=).
3. *Declared reducer columns are excluded, not counted.*  =_ADDITIVE_MEASURE_COLUMNS=
   (=food_acquired: Quantity, Expenditure=; =assets: Value=) and a =Price=
   re-derived from summed totals are summed today and stay summed under the
   proposal, so they cannot change.
4. *The build's residual re-audit is mirrored* for those same tables
   (country.py:5992-6006), so =destroyed= means what the stamp means.
5. *Site 2 is diffed on the frame with the household level already dropped*, as
   =_collapse_to_cluster_grain= does -- otherwise every multi-household cluster
   looks destructive (~100% false positives).

Not reproduced between the wave parquet and Site 1: =id_walk= and
=_augment_index_from_related_tables= (the latter only fires for a declared =m=).
=id_walk= re-keys =i=, so a cell where transitive re-keying *creates* duplicates
would be under-counted here.  The reconciliation below is what bounds that.

** The frame check: census =destroyed= vs the stamp

The stamped =destroyed= is the only instrument for "is the frame I diffed the
frame the build actually collapsed?".  Compared on every cell that is both
stamped and diffed:

| cells compared | agreeing | mismatched |
|-
| 51 | 51 | 0 |

*51 of 51 agree exactly*, and after fixing (1)
and (4) above there is no residual mismatch -- so for the stamped cells the
census frame is the build frame.  Unstamped cells carry no such check; they are
the ones a reader should discount if =id_walk= is in play.

No record is =unauditable= (0 of 1005).

* Two cells checked by hand

** =Niger/household_roster/2014-15=, Site 1, declared index =(t, i, pid)=

The group =('2014-15', '101008', '1')= -- two rows, one of them entirely blank:

#+begin_example
--- input rows in that group:
                         Sex   Age    Relationship  MonthsSpent
t       i      pid
2014-15 101008 1        <NA>   NaN            <NA>          NaN
               1    Masculin  45.0  Chef de menage         12.0
--- first() serves:
2014-15 101008 1    Masculin  45.0  Chef de menage         12.0
--- nth(0) serves:
2014-15 101008 1        <NA>   NaN            <NA>          NaN
#+end_example

This is =served_na_new= in the flesh, and it is the worst-looking case for the
proposal: today the household head's record survives; under =nth(0)= the served
row is the blank one, and =_finalize_result='s =dropna(how='all')= would then drop
the person entirely.  45 rows / 180 column cells in this cell.  Note what the
*pre-collapse* frame says, though: =pid= is not unique within the household, i.e.
the identifier is broken -- exactly the #323 verdict ("duplicates on a declared
index mean the IDENTIFIER IS BROKEN").  =first()= is repairing a broken key by
assembling a person out of two rows.

exchange for the guarantee that the served row is an observed one.
