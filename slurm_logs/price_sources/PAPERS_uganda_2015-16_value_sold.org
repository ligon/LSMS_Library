* 0. What is being traced, and the discriminator used

GH #829 establishes that in the *live* World Bank release =UGA_2015_UNPS_v02_M=
(catalog 3460), =AGSEC5A.a5aq8= / =AGSEC5B.a5bq8= ("What was the value? UShs")
are *divided by exactly 100*: the sole int32->double storage-type change among
the 432 variables common to the 2013-14 and 2015-16 AGSEC files; =a5aq8 x 100=
is an exact integer on 100.00% of 6,906 sale records; median implied price on
kilogram-sold records is 10 UShs/kg against 1,000 in 2011-12 and 2013-14 and
1,300 in 2019-20; the questionnaire text is unchanged; and the file we hold is
byte-identical to the distributed release.

** HEADLINE

*The World Bank's own harmonised product already corrects this, silently.*
=LSMS-ISA_Ag= -- the WB LSMS team's harmonised panel -- multiplies =a5aq8= by
100 for the 2015-16 wave and for no other wave, in every released version,
with no comment and no documentation.  *EPAR's* competing harmonised product,
which is the more widely used one, does *not* correct, and ships Uganda
2015-16 crop values ~100x too small in its public estimates.  Details in §1
and §2.  This turns the upstream report from an assertion into a
reconciliation: the Bank's own code already agrees with GH #829.

** The discriminator (applied in the "plausible effect" column)

A constant x1/100 confined to one wave does *not* damage every quantity that
touches it.  Three cases, and each item is placed in one of them from its own
stated construction:

1. *Cancels.*  Ratios whose numerator and denominator both descend from
   =a5aq8= -- most importantly the commercialisation share (sales value /
   production value, where production is valued at a sale-derived median
   price).  *Confirmed empirically in §2*: EPAR's =proportion_cropvalue_sold=
   is the one crop-value indicator NOT displaced between W4 and W5.
2. *Absorbed.*  Log-value specifications with wave (or year) fixed effects: a
   constant within-wave scalar is swept into the FE.  This holds only if the
   sample is *pooled with the FE present*; a wave-specific level, a growth
   rate, or a cross-wave difference is not protected.
3. *Broken.*  Levels; cross-wave growth or trends; any sum or difference
   mixing =a5aq8=-derived money with money from another module (the crop-income
   subtraction is the sharpest instance -- production falls 100x while input
   costs do not, so net income goes *negative*); income shares; and
   productivity denominated per hectare or per person-day.

A fourth case appears once in practice and is worth naming because it looks
like case 2 but is not: a *log of a SUM* of an affected and an unaffected
component (Morrissey et al.'s =log(crop sales + livestock sales)=).  The
scalar does not factor out of a sum, so it re-weights the two components
rather than shifting a constant.

=EPAR_UW_Uganda_UNPS_W5.do= -- no =* 100=, no drop, no flag:

: 476: clonevar sold_value= a5aq8

: 694: ren value_harvest value_crop_production
: 702: gen proportion_cropvalue_sold = value_crop_sales / value_crop_production

latter because the harvest is valued at a median price whose numerator is
=sold_value=.  =crop_income= then subtracts input costs that do not.

independent confirmation: that series is volatile across every wave pair
(W7/W5 = 2,000, W8/W5 = 429), so its W4/W5 ratio is not evidence of a x100 on

The two rows that behave differently are the §0 discriminator showing up in
published output:
- =w_proportion_cropvalue_sold= is the *only* crop-value indicator undisturbed,
  because numerator and denominator both carry the 1/100.
- =w_crop_income_1ppp= does not merely shrink, it *changes sign*: production
  falls 100x while =crop_production_expenses= does not, so the median Ugandan
  farm household is published as *losing* money on crops in 2015-16 alone.  Its
  knock-on, =w_share_crop=, is published as a median of *exactly zero* for that
  wave against 0.353 in 2013-14 and 1.000 in 2018-19.

*Reach.*  Any user of AgQuery, of the estimates spreadsheet, or of
=Uganda UNPS W5.zip= inherits this without touching a raw =.dta=.  These are
"download and analyse" products; the defect is three layers below where such a
user would look.
