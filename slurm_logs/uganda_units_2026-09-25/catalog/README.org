#+title: Uganda food-unit catalog audit, GH #962

* Scope and evidence

Sue; 2026-09-25; baseline =4c2223aa119381ebcd8aea05fc2f9d26c3026028=.
The audit reads the eight raw food modules with =get_dataframe= and retains only code/item/wave/source aggregates, never household identifiers or microdata frames.
The [[file:proposed_mapping.csv][143-row mapping]] records the exact preferred labels, their source strings, canonical equivalents, and observed raw-row counts.
The [[file:raw_blank_usage.csv][raw-use counts]] and [[file:source_summaries.json][source inventory]] identify the observations supporting those counts.

The baseline catalog has 289 codes and 143 blank preferred labels.
Of the 134 blanks with a source string, 131 specify a unit or size and three name only an item (=153001=, =154001=, =160001=).
All 134 strings match the 2011-12 Stata unit metadata exactly; the other seven wave codebooks omit them.
Promoting 131 labels therefore uses one wave's codebook, not independent agreement across waves.
The mapping reuses 111 canonical equivalents and preserves the recorded sizes and named forms of the other 20, without guessing containers for labels such as =Soda (500 ml)=.

Only =123060= among the 131 occurs in the raw modules: one 2015-16 fish purchase, quantity 4 and expenditure 8,000.
Its existing canonical equivalent =Fish Cut piece(Above 2  kg)= is parsed as 2 kg per piece (8 kg here), despite the label's lower bound and the legacy curated 3 kg factor.
That range-endpoint policy is already pinned in =tests/test_food_kg_inference.py= and is unchanged.
The fraction parser from GH #963 reads the half- and quarter-packet labels as 0.5 and 0.25 kg.
