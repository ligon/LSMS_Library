* Tag checks (reported / imputed / aggregated), 10 per file

BRIEF.md defines three tags: =reported= (carried from the instrument as
asked), =imputed= (filled or replaced by a rule), =aggregated= (summed /
collapsed / indexed to a coarser grain).  *Two whole classes of EPAR
construction fall outside all three*, and every file trips over at least one:

- *A filter / selection* (rural-only, priority-crop list, sub-population).  It
  removes rows; it neither fills, replaces, nor collapses.
- *A scalar rescale of an already-reported value* (x52 annualization, PPP
  conversion, population-calibrated weights, unit-factor multiplication).  It
  changes no row's provenance and creates no new information.
- A borderline third: *a ratio of two reported values* (=price_unit =
  value/qty=).  Nothing is imputed and nothing is aggregated, yet the number is
  not what the instrument asked either.

Recommendation for the synthesis: add a fourth tag (=derived= or =rescaled=)
or mark these explicitly "not a data transform".  The mislabels below are
mostly *consequences of the missing tag*, not carelessness -- except where two
files tag the SAME construction differently, which is a real inconsistency.
