* 1. The hook -- PASS

** CONCERN B: eight firings have a reference below one unit

The ledger argues the =reference > 0= condition exists to stop "a 2 kg cassava
row named as implausible, which is exactly the false positive that discredits
a guard".  A p90 of 0.6 clears that condition.  Measured: 8 of the 162
firings (all Uganda 2010-11, rung =t,u=, =u='Others specify'=, p90 = 0.6):

: Rice 300 (x500) | Ground Nuts 150 | Sugarcane 150 | Pumpkins 105
: Maize 99 | Cassava 90 | Sugarcane 80 | Sugarcane 66

These are ordinary harvests.  The =(t,u)= rung pools every crop sharing a
junk unit label, so the reference measures nothing.  22% of Uganda's firings
and 5% of the corpus's are this class.  A =reference >= 1= floor, or excluding
the =U_UNKNOWN=/"Others specify" family from the =(t,u)= rung, would remove
them; either is a judgement call for the author.  Stated precisely: the MECHANISM
(strict positivity) does deliver what it was written for -- it kills the
ratio-=inf= firings the ledger measured -- but the PURPOSE the ledger gives it
("not naming a 2 kg cassava row") is not achieved, because a junk-unit =(t,u)=
cell has a small POSITIVE p90 and sails through.

** CONCERN C: the unjudged rows are silent AND uncounted
