** CONCERN 1 -- the plausibility ceiling is wave-inconsistent by a factor of 43

The ceiling is "the largest field this wave actually measured by GPS".
Measured, per wave:

| wave    | GPS max = ceiling | GPS p99 | GPS p999 | GPS median |
|---------+-------------------+---------+----------+------------|
| 2011-12 |          *9.91* ha |   0.936 |    4.405 |     0.0478 |
| 2013-14 |        *126.41* ha |   1.253 |    7.374 |     0.0455 |
| 2015-16 |        *425.71* ha |   1.104 |    4.599 |     0.0403 |
| 2018-19 |         *19.81* ha |   1.023 |    2.852 |     0.0330 |
| 2021-22 |          *4.93* ha |   1.007 |    2.486 |     0.0315 |

The five ceilings span 4.9 ha to 425.7 ha because each is set by a *single*
GPS outlier -- and every wave's p99 is ~1 ha, so the ceiling is 5x to 425x
the 99th percentile depending on which wave you are in.  Consequence,
measured: the three rows refused in 2011-12 (13.7 / 18.0 / 28.1 ha) would all
have been *accepted* in 2013-14 or 2015-16; conversely 2013-14 serves a
33.22 ha converted field and 2015-16 a 12.14 ha one, both above 2011-12's
ceiling.  Converted rows exceeding 9.9142 ha (the 2011-12 ceiling) by wave:
2011-12 *0*, 2013-14 *3*, 2015-16 *1*, 2018-19 *0*.

The docstring's defence -- "the ceiling is the wave's own data rather than a
magic constant, and it is deliberately generous" -- is true but incomplete:
it is not merely generous, it is *non-comparable across waves*, so "refused"
does not mean the same thing in 2011-12 as in 2015-16.  =count, never clip=
is respected either way and no number is wrong; this is about the *screen*,
not the data.  A wave-invariant reference (e.g. the wave's GPS p999, which is
2.5-7.4 ha across all five and would flag 38/3/3/2 rows) would make the
counts comparable.  *Suggest an issue; do not block on it.*
