* Coverage (their waves vs ours; what they build for this scope)

EPAR ships a dedicated, standalone "Nonstandard Unit Conversion Factors"
folder for exactly two countries: Uganda UNPS (7 waves pooled into one
master table) and Malawi IHS (multi-source, multi-wave). For Nigeria GHS,
Ethiopia ESS and Tanzania NPS there is no such folder; conversion-factor
logic (where it exists at all) is inlined in each wave's ~300 KB do-file.

* Construction decisions by feature area (each tagged reported/imputed/aggregated, with .do:line)

** Uganda (=Uganda UNPS/Nonstandard Unit Conversion Factors/=)

- *Grain*: (crop_code, unit_code, region) for the regional table; (crop_code,
  unit_code) for the national fallback. =condition= is tracked through the
  per-wave extraction (=conditionharv=/=conditionsold=) but the =by()= clause
  of every final =collapse= has it COMMENTED OUT
  (=Master_Conversionfactor_table.do:248,260,272,287=: "/*condition_harv*/"),
  so the shipped table pools fresh, dry and shelled reports of the same
  crop/unit/region into one factor. *aggregated* (median), condition
  dropped.
- *Pooling scope*: appends all 7 waves (2009-2019+) into one panel BEFORE
  collapsing (lines 234-237), so one national/regional factor is used for
  every year. Weighted by the wave's own sample weight
  (=[weight=weight]=, line 248) -- a design choice with no analogue in our
  unweighted =_survey_median_factors=.
- *Fallback ladder*: reported per-row =conv_fact= (source: instrument, wave
  1-7 raw AGSEC5A/5B, =A5?q6d=/=a5?q6c=) -> regional median -> national
  median (region-blind). Two levels, both *imputed* medians over *reported*
  values -- no unit-label parsing, no price-ratio inference anywhere in this
  file.
- *Harvest vs sold*: two independent tables/unit columns from the moment the
  raw files are read (=unitharv=/=conv_factharv= vs
  =unitsold=/=conv_factsold=, first appearing wave 3, line 77-102). This
  IS our #824 concern, already solved on their side by construction.
- *Screening*: none observed beyond one hardcoded drop of a sentinel value
  (=drop if conv_fact==18888 | conv_fact==.=, line 101) and forcing
  =s_conv_factor_sold=1 if sold_unit_code==1= (i.e. Kg forced to 1 post
  hoc, line 276) -- narrower than our =_screen_reported_factors= (which
  also catches >250 kg and year-like integers) and applied only to the
  SOLD table, not harvest.
- *Provenance disclosure*: none. The final =.dta= tables carry only
  (crop, unit, [region], factor, nobs) -- no column says which layer
  (reported vs regional-median vs national-median) produced a given row's
  factor. *This is the single largest disclosure gap relative to our
  design* (our =KgFactorSource= column, =transformations.py:1899=).
- *Measured spread* ("high uncertainty", quantified from
  =UG_Conv_fact_harvest_table.dta=, 2181 rows, 544 crop x unit cells with
  >=2 regions): median (IQR / median) across those cells = *0.228* (23%);
  the worst cells run 10-70x median-scale IQR (e.g. crop 510/unit 87:
  median 3.5, IQR 250.25, i.e. region medians spanning 2 to 1000).
  Overall factor range 0-1500 across 2089 populated rows.
  =reported= tag: the pre-collapse rows; =imputed= tag: every shipped
  cell (median of >=1 reports).

** Malawi (=Malawi IHS/Nonstandard Unit Conversion Factors/=)

- *Sources* (=README.md=): (1) IHS5 2019-20's own released conversion
  files, borrowed for ALL earlier waves in the absence of a wave-specific
  file -- =ihs_seasonalcropconversion_factor_2020.dta= (857 rows: region x
  crop_code x unit x condition x collectionround) and
  =ihs_treeconversion_factor_2020.dta= (153 rows, no condition axis --
  trees have none); (2) a PDF scrape of the IHS3 (2010-11) food-conversion
  document (=Malawi_IHS3_Food_Item_Conversion_Factors.pdf=, hand-keyed
  triples =cf1/cf2/cf3= per crop x unit x condition,
  =EPAR_UW_conversionfactors.do:29-101=, three parallel columns whose
  meaning -- three raw values? three region estimates? -- is not
  documented in-file); (3) =caloric_conversionfactor.dta= (142 items,
  cal/100g + edible%) -- a FOOD/nutrition table, not a kg-conversion
  table, kept in the same folder but semantically distinct from (1)/(2).
- *Grain*: region x crop_code_long x unit_code x condition. Crop condition
  IS an axis here (unlike Uganda) -- =condition_label 1 SHELLED 2 UNSHELLED
  3 N/A= (line 331).
- *Shelled/unshelled interpolation* (our =condition= axis, exactly): a
  per-crop ratio =shell_unshelled= is computed once
  (=collapse (mean) shell_unshelled, by(crop_code)=, line 389, saved
  standalone as =MWI_IHS_shell_unshelled.dta=, 8 crops) and then used
  BIDIRECTIONALLY to fill a missing condition from the other:
  =conversion2 = conversion1/shell_unshelled if conversion2==.= /
  =conversion1 = conversion2*shell_unshelled if conversion1==.= (lines
  401-405, repeated 424-430). *imputed*, ratio applied at national grain
  (not region), a genuinely different axis-filling technique from
  anything in our code (we have no cross-condition ratio anywhere).
- *Fallback ladder, in the order the do-file applies them* (each step
  stamps a =source= string that survives to the final =Malawi_IHS_cf.dta=):
  1. reported region x crop x unit x condition value, =source= blank
     (=food_cf= / =IHS 2020= at ingestion, lines 286, 318);
  2. shell/unshelled ratio of the SAME region's other condition
     (=source = "Shelled/unshelled ratio"=, line 402/405);
  3. regional mean across... itself? -- =bys region crop_code unit_code:
     egen mean_conv=mean(conversion)= then filled where missing
     (=source = "Regional mean"=, line 413);
  4. national mean, region-blind (=source = "National mean"=, line 420);
  5. shell/unshelled ratio AGAIN (second pass, line 424-430, for whatever
     the regional/national mean still left empty);
  6. crop-family borrowing: tuber crops (sweet potato/Irish
     potato/cassava, ids 28/29/49) get the MEAN of the other tubers'
     factor for that region+unit (=source = "mean of tuber crops"=, line
     482); cowpea (36) factors are copied onto two OTHER crop codes
     (46, 35) verbatim, no ratio (=source = "cowpea conversions"=, line
     486);
  7. anything still missing is dropped (=drop if conversion==.=, line
     490).
  Six *imputed* layers plus the *reported* base -- the deepest fallback
  chain either side of this comparison, and the ONLY one on either side
  that names crop-family substitution (tubers, cowpea) as a defined step.
- *A "more conservative" merge rule*, food-conversion file only: when both
  the PDF-scrape food_cf value and the IHS-2020 =cf= value exist and
  disagree, EPAR keeps whichever is SMALLER
  (=replace conversion=cf if (conversion==. | cf<conversion) & _merge==3=,
  line 244) -- an explicit, documented preference for the lower estimate
  with no stated justification beyond the inline comment "taking the
  more conservative conversion factor between files". *imputed*, and a
  tie-break rule with no analogue (and, we'd argue, no obvious
  justification) in our design.
- *Harvest vs sold*: not constructed in this folder at all -- Malawi's
  conversion tables only ever key on the HARVEST unit; no sold-unit
  variable appears anywhere in =EPAR_UW_conversionfactors.do=.

** Ethiopia (grep sweep, W1 and W3 do-files; not EPAR's own folder -- ESS has none)

=EPAR_UW_Ethiopia_ESS_W3.do:143-146,948-990=. The crop conversion factor
file is NOT EPAR's construction from raw survey rows (unlike Uganda/
Malawi) -- it is a WB-DISTRIBUTED raw file per wave, =Crop_CF_WaveN.dta=
(present for W2-W5 in our DVC tree: =Crop_CF_Wave2.dta.dvc=,
=Crop_CF_Wave3.dta.dvc=, =Crop_CF_Wave4.dta.dvc=, =crop_cf_wave5.dta.dvc=;
no sidecar found for W1 2011-12, matching the do-file's own
=capture confirm file= / "not present" fallback warning, line 990/1034).
EPAR reshapes it long over region (with the three atypical regions
Somalie/Harari/Dire Dawa each hard-assigned the SAME =mean_cf99= column,
line 956-960 -- a likely shortcut bug, not investigated further), fills
missing crop x unit x region cells from a national mean (=nat_cf=), then
missing units from a crop-BLIND generic-unit mean (=collapse (mean)
gen_conversion=conversion, by(unit region)=, line 974 -- explicit comment
"this isn't perfect but it's a rough estimate... when the crop code is
unknown"), forces kg-unit to 1 and a specific unit (code 3) to 100
post-merge (lines 1054-1055), and drops one hand-identified implausible
row (=drop if crop_code==74 & unit==62 & conversion>5=, line 985) --
*this last step is a manual analogue of our =_screen_reported_factors=*,
but ad hoc (one crop x unit pair, not a general rule) rather than a
declared threshold. Layers: reported (WB file) -> region mean ->
national mean -> generic-unit mean (crop-blind) -> hardcoded overrides;
all *imputed* except the WB file's own base numbers, whose provenance
(survey-reported vs WB desk estimate) is not stated in the do-file.
AREA conversion (local plot units -> hectares) is a SEPARATE file
(=ET_local_area_unit_conversion.dta=, present in DVC for 2013-14/
2015-16/2018-19/2011-12) and a separate axis from crop weight -- not
followed further, out of scope.

** Nigeria (grep sweep, W1 and W3 do-files)

No crop-weight/nonstandard-unit-to-kg conversion construction found in
either wave's do-file. The only "conversion" machinery under that keyword
is AREA: a hardcoded zone x area-unit -> hectare lookup taken verbatim
from the "LSMS-ISA Nigeria Wave 2 Basic Information Document"
(=EPAR_UW_Nigeria_GHS_W1.do:309-357=, =W3.do:241-293=; heaps/ridges/stands
each get a zone-specific hectare factor, e.g. =conversion = area_size *
0.00012 if area_unit==1 & zone==1=). *reported* (survey area unit) fed
through an *imputed* (externally sourced, not data-derived) constant
table. Consistent with #824's own finding that Nigeria's crop_production
ignores the sold-unit variable =sa3q11b= entirely -- there is no sold-side
OR harvest-side kg-conversion machinery to ignore it with.

** Tanzania (grep sweep, W1 and W3 do-files)

No crop-weight/nonstandard-unit-to-kg conversion construction found. The
only conversion machinery under the keyword is currency PPP/USD/TSH
(=ccf_loc=/=ccf_usd=/=ccf_1ppp=/=ccf_2ppp=, e.g. =W3.do:5016-5024=). Two

nonstandard units". *Declined*, not imputed -- EPAR's own stated
practice here is closer to our "none" layer than to inference.

* Ranked learnings (what we could take, why, what it would touch, and the risk)

1. *Carry per-row layer provenance the way Malawi's =source= column does
   (we already do this for crop harvest, not for food).* Malawi's
   =Malawi_IHS_cf.dta= ships a =source= string alongside every factor
   (=EPAR_UW_conversionfactors.do:482,486=, six distinct values). Our
   =harvest_kg_factors= already does exactly this
   (=KgFactorSource=, =transformations.py:1899=) for crop_production, but
   the FOOD side (=food_quantities_from_acquired= /
   =_get_kg_factors=/=conversion_to_kgs=) returns only the resolved
   number, with no equivalent of "which layer served this row".
   *Touches*: =transformations.py= (=_get_kg_factors=, =conversion_to_kgs=
   return shape) -- a real API change, not additive-only, since callers
   read a plain dict today. *Risk*: medium (widens two public function
   signatures used across every country's food derivation); high value,
   because #850 already wants a parallel generalisation for crop and the
   two could land together.

2. *Add the item axis (=j=) to the food-side price-ratio inference
   baseline.* GH #850 asks for =j= in the CROP baseline group as a new
   feature; measured here (Design decisions §Ethiopia, finding 4 in the
   register) the FOOD side has the identical gap TODAY, in production,
   in both the static =Ethiopia/_/conversion_to_kgs.json=
   (=groupby('unit_cd')= only) and the live =conversion_to_kgs=
   (=index=['t','m','i']=, =transformations.py:782=, no =j=). A "bunch"
   of two different food items gets one pooled factor. *Touches*:
   =transformations.py:782,1051= (default =index=), plus every country's
   cached food_quantities/food_prices output would move. *Risk*: high --
   #850's own §2 already names exactly why (maize and coffee prices per
   kg differ by orders of magnitude; the food analogue is bananas vs.
   groundnuts in a "Cup"). This is arguably the single highest-leverage
   item on this list because it is not hypothetical -- it is live and
   silently wrong for every country whose food price-ratio inference
   fires.

3. *Do NOT adopt EPAR's "kg unit need not equal 1" tolerance.* Measured
   directly against our own screen: of Uganda's 128 kg-unit region-medians
   in =UG_Conv_fact_harvest_table.dta=, 6 deviate from 1 by more than 1%,
   two backed by nobs>=10 (crop 620/region 3: factor 120, n=10; crop
   741/region 2: factor 13, n=14) -- i.e. EPAR's own pooled data would
   fail our =_screen_reported_factors= kg-unit check
   (=transformations.py:1706-1708=) at a rate our design treats as a
   data-entry-error signature. This is evidence FOR keeping our stricter
   rule, not a learning to import -- listed here because the brief asks
   for evidence on both sides and this is a case where EPAR's own
   pooled table corroborates the LIBRARY's existing design decision
   rather than suggesting a change. *Touches*: nothing (no action). *Risk*: none.

4. *The Malawi shelled/unshelled cross-condition ratio is a genuinely new
   technique our =condition= axis does not have.* We carry =condition= as
   an index level on =crop_production= (Uganda/Mali/Niger/Tanzania/Togo
   per =data_info.yml=) but nothing today INFERS one condition's factor
   from the other's via a per-crop ratio the way
   =EPAR_UW_conversionfactors.do:381-390= does (=shell_unshelled=,
   computed once as a per-crop mean and cached to its own file). This
   could become a fifth layer in =harvest_kg_factors= ("ratio from the
   SAME row's other reported condition") for countries that report both
   conditions for the same crop/unit/wave. *Touches*: a new function in
   =transformations.py=, a new entry in =KG_FACTOR_LAYERS=, and the
   =min_reports=-gated computation of the ratio itself (needs its own
   N threshold, undecided). *Risk*: medium-high -- it is a real
   construction decision (which crops the ratio applies to, at what
   grain) EPAR encodes as a hardcoded crop-id allowlist
   (=shunsh_na= at line 442-444), which is exactly the kind of "magic
   list" this repo's CLAUDE.md warns against (cf. the GPS-aggregation
   "no exception" precedent) -- would need a principled, not copied,
   version.

5. *Malawi's crop-side (harvest) EPAR source files are ALREADY in our DVC
   cache, unused.* =ihs_treeconversion_factor_2020.dta= and
   =ihs_seasonalcropconversion_factor_2020.dta= sit under
   =Malawi/2019-20/Data/Cross_Sectional/= with =.dvc= sidecars (confirmed
   by directory listing) but no =.py= in the Malawi tree reads either
   (grep across =lsms_library/countries/Malawi/= finds only
   =ihs_foodconversion_factor_2020.dta= wired, in
   =2019-20/_/food_acquired.py:17=, for the FOOD side). Building Malawi's
   =harvest_kg= support could start from acquisition-complete raw
   material -- no WB download needed. *Touches*: a new
   =Malawi/_/crop_production.py= (or an addition to the existing script)
   plus =data_scheme.yml= (add =condition=, optionally =KgFactor= if a
   per-row factor is findable in the raw harvest files, which these
   region-level tables are NOT -- they're already-aggregated factors,
   so they'd feed the =inferred= layer, not =reported=). *Risk*: medium;
   this is real, scoped work, not a design question, and
   =Malawi/_/CONTENTS.org= already half-flags it (see contradiction
   below) but with an incomplete source inventory.

6. *Ethiopia's crop-side WB =Crop_CF_WaveN.dta= files are likewise
   already in DVC and unused* (=Crop_CF_Wave{2,3,4}.dta.dvc=,
   =crop_cf_wave5.dta.dvc=), while =Ethiopia/_/data_scheme.yml:120=
   states outright "NO unit->kg conversion" for =crop_production=. Same
   shape as learning 5, one country over. *Touches*/*Risk*: same as 5.
   Filed together they would be two new =data-gap= issues, not one --
   the fallback ladders EPAR uses (region -> national -> generic-unit)
   differ enough between the two countries' source material that a
   single generic loader is unlikely to serve both.
