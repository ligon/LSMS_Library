  =food_acquired=) flagged as "future enhancement (not yet wired)".
  CONTENTS.org:93-95.

  question (=secta3ii=) is hh-crop grain, not plot-crop grain, and our build

  positive-sale rows; the build assumes they're equal and never reads

- Universe/exclusions: W3 conflict-area oversample (+5 HH/EA); W4 excludes

We hold all five EPAR-covered waves; EPAR holds no Nigeria wave we lack.
Only W3, W4, W5 ship a =readme.md=; W1/W2 have only placeholder
=Final DTA files/README.md= / =Raw DTA files/README.md= stubs ("Final .dta
files will be saved here") with no Known-Issues section, so those two waves'
caveats had to be read out of the =.do= file bodies directly rather than a
readme.

EPAR builds ~40 wide, mostly household-level indicator tables per wave
(TLU, gross crop revenue, crop expenses, monocropped-plot file, livestock
income, fish income, self-employment income, wage/off-farm income, farm
size, farm labor, vaccine usage, disease incidence, plot-manager input-use
rates, ag-extension contact, mobile/financial-services ownership,
milk/egg productivity, per-hectare costs, fertilizer application rates,
women's decision-making/asset/diet indicators, HDDS, poverty indices,
consumption aggregates, household assets) plus a shared summary-statistics
pipeline. We build item-level tables at survey grain (=crop_production=,
=plot_inputs=, =plot_labor=, =livestock=, =plot_features=, =community_prices=,
=food_acquired=, ...) and derive ratios/indices/valuations at query time via
=transformations.py=. None of EPAR's ~40 output tables is a 1:1 target for
one of ours; the comparison below is at the level of individual
constructions inside sections that map to our features.

* Construction decisions by feature area (each tagged reported/imputed/aggregated, with .do:line)

** PLOT AREAS
- =reported=: farmer-reported area + unit (=s11aq4aa=/=s11aq4b= in W4), GPS-measured plot area in m^2 (=s11aq4c=). =EPAR_UW_Nigeria_GHS_W4.do:339-347=.
- =imputed=: standard-unit-to-hectare conversion (plots x0.0667, acres x0.404686, sqm x0.0001) -- =EPAR_UW_Nigeria_GHS_W4.do:388-390=; zone-specific heaps/ridges/stands factors from the BID table -- =EPAR_UW_Nigeria_GHS_W4.do:391-397=.
- =reported= (preferred over imputed): GPS measurement overwrites the farmer estimate when present -- =EPAR_UW_Nigeria_GHS_W4.do:399=.

** ALL PLOTS / crop values (the key price question)
- =reported=: hh-crop sale quantity/unit/size/condition/value from =secta3ii= (=sa3iiq5a/1c/1d/1b/6=, W4) or plot-crop sale (=sa3q11a/11b/12=, W1/W2). =EPAR_UW_Nigeria_GHS_W4.do:685-694=; =EPAR_UW_Nigeria_GHS_W1.do:479-482=.
- =imputed=: geographic median-price ladder (household -> EA -> LGA -> state -> zone -> country, N>=10 observations at each level before it's trusted) built from the sale transactions above, then applied to the FULL harvest quantity (sold or not) to produce =value_harvest= -- =EPAR_UW_Nigeria_GHS_W4.do:695-706=, W1 equivalent =EPAR_UW_Nigeria_GHS_W1.do:653-712=.
- =imputed=, then abandoned: =sa3q18=-as-direct-valuation, commented out in W2 -- =EPAR_UW_Nigeria_GHS_W2.do:644-679= (live code instead at lines 686-707).
- =imputed=: per-row survey conversion factor (=sa3iiq1_conv= etc.) preferred over a computed crop x unit x size x condition median factor table, itself partly literature-sourced (e.g. sugarcane, pickup-truck-load constants attributed to "Kormawa and Ogundapo") -- =EPAR_UW_Nigeria_GHS_W4.do:576-649=.

** GROSS CROP REVENUE
- =aggregated=: =value_crop_sales= = sum of hh-crop =sales_value= over the wave (=EPAR_UW_Nigeria_GHS_W4.do:982-985=).
- =aggregated= + =imputed= reconciliation: =value_crop_production= = imputed =value_harvest= from ALL PLOTS, OVERRIDDEN by =value_crop_sales= where reported sales exceed the imputed harvest value (=EPAR_UW_Nigeria_GHS_W4.do:988=).

** CROP EXPENSES / FARM LABOR
- =reported=: hired and non-hired labor counts/days/wage by gender, separately for the planting (=sect11c1b=) and harvest (=secta2b=) rounds, tagged =season='pp'|'ph'=. =EPAR_UW_Nigeria_GHS_W4.do:1043-1075=.
- =aggregated=: hired person-days and wage summed ACROSS pp+ph into one wave-level number per household x gender (child labor dropped from the wage calc: "Not keeping track of hired labor for some reason", =EPAR_UW_Nigeria_GHS_W4.do:1096=). =EPAR_UW_Nigeria_GHS_W4.do:1090-1108=.
- =imputed=: median agricultural wage by geography (same ladder pattern as crop prices), used to value UNPAID/non-hired labor. =EPAR_UW_Nigeria_GHS_W4.do:1113 ff.=

** TLU
- =reported=: livestock head counts by species code, split "at start of season" vs "as of today" (=s11iq6=/=s11iq2a=). =EPAR_UW_Nigeria_GHS_W4.do:1866-1879=.
- =imputed=/=reported-as-fixed-constant=: species-code -> TLU coefficient map (cattle-group 0.5, code 108 0.3, 110/111 0.1, 112 0.2, poultry 0.01, 122 0.7). =EPAR_UW_Nigeria_GHS_W4.do:1850-1854=.
- =aggregated=: summed to household level (=collapse (sum) tlu_* nb_*, by (hhid)=, =EPAR_UW_Nigeria_GHS_W4.do:1893=).

** LIVESTOCK INCOME
- =reported=: weekly milk-sale earnings/quantity (=s11k2q10-14=). =EPAR_UW_Nigeria_GHS_W4.do:1948-1951=.
- =imputed=: extrapolated to season total via =earnings_week * 4.35 (wks/month) * months_producing= with no recall-window column retained on output. =EPAR_UW_Nigeria_GHS_W4.do:1948,1950=.

** Fertilizer / improved seed (PLOT MANAGERS)
- =reported=: item-level fertilizer/pesticide/herbicide kg and improved-seed flag (=s11fq3b=). =EPAR_UW_Nigeria_GHS_W4.do:2783=.
- =aggregated=: collapsed with =max()= from plot x crop grain up to household x manager grain as a binary "uses X" indicator, discarding the plot-level detail in the delivered table. =EPAR_UW_Nigeria_GHS_W4.do:2799-2809, 2843-2849=.

** HOUSEHOLD IDS / WEIGHTS
- =reported=: post-planting cover page household id/weight/rural flag (=secta_plantingw4.dta=, =wt_wave4=). =EPAR_UW_Nigeria_GHS_W4.do:216-221=.
- =imputed=: weight RESCALED (raked) to match external World Bank rural/urban population totals for the survey year -- =weight_pop_rururb=, not the raw survey weight, used in every downstream median-price/wage ladder. =EPAR_UW_Nigeria_GHS_W4.do:292-303=.

** Diet Diversity (HDDS)
- =reported=: 7-day y/n consumption per food item, both PP and PH rounds (=s7bq1=/=s10bq1=). =EPAR_UW_Nigeria_GHS_W4.do:3813-3821=.
- =aggregated=: item -> 12-food-group max(); food-group count -> per-round HDDS; PP-round and PH-round HDDS AVERAGED into one wave-level number. =EPAR_UW_Nigeria_GHS_W4.do:3838-3849=.

** Individual roster (combining PP and PH into one wave)
- =reported=, then =aggregated= by preference order: age/sex read from BOTH the planting and harvest person rosters, reshaped wide by round, then =age = age_plan; replace age = age_harv if age==.= (planting preferred, harvest fills gaps) -- one row per person per WAVE. =EPAR_UW_Nigeria_GHS_W4.do:243-249=. This is the general pattern behind "how EPAR combines the post-planting and post-harvest visits into one wave": round-tag, reshape wide, prefer one round and backfill from the other (household ids, roster, labor-days-summed, HDDS-averaged -- the reconciliation RULE differs by table, but every feature ends up with exactly one row per household/person per wave, never two).

* Ranked learnings (what we could take, why, what it would touch, and the risk)

1. *Wire the per-row crop conversion factor* (=sa3iq6_conv=/=sa3iiq1_conv=/=sa3iiiq13_conv=/=sa3iq6d_conv=) as an optional =KgFactor= column on Nigeria =crop_production=, mirroring the Uganda =KgFactor= precedent (#849) and our own =harvest_kg= layering (reported -> survey median -> library-inferred -> none, CLAUDE.md). Why: EPAR measured this is worth preferring over a computed median table; we currently have zero factor for Nigeria crop_production and rely entirely on =harvest_kg='s inferred layers. Touches: =_/crop_production.py= (add a per-row cvn read per wave/file), =data_scheme.yml= (optional column), =transformations.py: harvest_kg= (extra layer). Risk: LOW -- purely additive, optional column, same shape as #849's ask; the two should probably be worked together (candidate: generalize #850's own ask, "Generalise _get_kg_factors ... to infer crop factors from sale prices," to also prefer this reported factor first).
2. *Adopt the geographic median-price ladder to value the UNSOLD portion of the harvest* (EA -> LGA -> state -> zone -> country, N>=10), mirroring our own =median_price_valuation= transform which already implements exactly this ladder shape for other purposes. Why: this is the answer to "how does EPAR get a price when W3-W5 lack a plot-level sale price" (#843's key question) -- they don't try to attribute the hh-crop sale to a plot; they build a price INDEX from all hh-crop sales at increasingly coarse geography and apply it to plot-crop harvest quantity. Touches: a NEW transformation (not a stored column, per the design philosophy) that would take =crop_production= (plot-crop harvest qty + hh-crop sale qty/value) and produce an imputed =Value_harvest= at query time -- exactly analogous to our existing =food_prices(units='unitprice', s='produced')= for food. Risk: MEDIUM -- must stay a transformation, never a cached column (per "no aggregation in core" and "never store an imputed construction"); must document the grain bridge (hh-crop price applied to plot-crop quantity) as loudly as #843 already does, or it repairs the SYMPTOM of #843 while reintroducing the exact grain-mismatch EPAR itself waffled on (Cell 3's finding that EPAR's own =value_harvest= mixes grains silently).
3. *Do NOT copy the =sa3q18=-as-direct-valuation idea without EPAR's own caution in mind.* EPAR tried exactly what #844 proposes (rename =sa3q18=/=secta3ii= farmgate valuation straight to a value column) in W2 and then commented it out in favor of the price ladder, without leaving a reason in the code. Why this matters as a learning: before wiring #844, either (a) empirically compare =sa3q18=-derived unit values against the price-ladder values EPAR ended up trusting, to see the same discrepancy EPAR silently reacted to, or (b) accept #844's own field as a `Value_if_sold` alongside — not instead of — a ladder-based `Value_harvest`, since CONTENTS.org already frames it as "give Nigeria a farmgate valuation... recorded here as a gap rather than guessed at," i.e. exactly the caution EPAR skipped. Touches: #844 investigation itself. Risk: LOW to do the comparison, MEDIUM if #844 is closed by blindly wiring =sa3q18= as-is.
4. *Cross-check every country's TLU coefficient table against =_TLU_FACTORS=* as a standing audit, not a one-off. Nigeria (cattle 0.5 vs our 0.70) is now a SECOND independently-found instance of the #737 pattern (EthiopiaRHS 0.83 vs 0.70).
