* Shared roots --- fix these, not the symptoms

** Root A --- GhanaLSS =food_acquired= reducer discipline (#936, #832-GhanaLSS)
=food_acquired= is in =_ADDITIVE_MEASURE_COLUMNS=, so *any* duplicate index makes
core SUM and re-derive =Price = Expenditure/Quantity= over the whole frame
(=CONTENTS.org= "Trap 9").  #832's fix shipped an index-uniqueness assert in
each of the four wave scripts.  1998-99's =groupby(...).agg(Price='first')= is
the *one* collapse in the country sitting before that assert, bypassing the
existing =ghanalss.reduce_duplicate_food_rows=.  Routing it through that helper
closes #936 and completes the discipline #832 began.

** Root B --- cp1252 decoded as Latin-1 (#926, #805)
One mechanism, two layers: #805 is the reader, #926 is 73 already-committed
config cells.  *Neither fix closes the other* --- a fixed reader will go on
serving the committed artefacts.

** Root C --- nobody has read the GLSS3 (1991-92) instrument (#913, #851)
Same wave, same missing act.  #913 needs Section 8H's =u='All'= semantics; #851
needs GLSS3's stratum-varying recall window stamped per row.  One session with
=1991-92/Documentation/pdf/G3QPartB.pdf= and =G3Intman.pdf= section 1.9 feeds
both.  Implementation warning already in the #851 thread: the stratum is *not*
in =S9B.DTA=, and the window must not be inferred by counting visits present
(that conflates protocol with non-response).

* Proposed new issues --- evidence gathered, nothing filed

1. *Ghana's =Region= vocabulary is two incompatible geographies.*  GhanaLSS
   serves the pre-2018 10 regions; GhanaAHIES the post-2018 16.  Nine labels
   collide, and the collision is the point: post-2018 =Northern= excludes
   Savannah and North East, =Western= excludes Western North, =Volta= excludes
   Oti.  =market='Region'= and a two-country =Feature('cluster_features')= pool
   them with no signal.  Tier A, a day.
2. *GhanaAHIES is not on the shared Ghana =Relationship= vocabulary.*  =9ad8e04f6=
   built one vocabulary for GhanaLSS and GhanaSPS; AHIES has no =relationship=
   table and serves 16 raw GSS labels, only 2 of which match.  Several are
   literally rows in GhanaLSS's =Original Label= column, so the mapping exists
   and is simply not applied.  Tier B, hours.
3. *Promote =Roof= / =Floor= to the global categorical mapping.*  All three Ghana
   countries curate the identical 9 Preferred Labels for each, independently,
   with zero differences in any pair.  Precedent: =9ad8e04f6=.  Tier C, hours.
4. *Uganda's =v= is four different geographic entities across eight waves* ---
   community/EA code, a GPS coordinate bin (2009-10), parish code, and a
   DISTRICT/PARISH name composite.  This is *not* #942: GhanaLSS is faithful
   passthrough of a genuine renumbering, Uganda is a level whose *meaning*
   changes.  Each wave is deliberate and documented; nothing signals that a
   pooled =cluster_features= mixes three grains.  Tier B.

* Method, and its limits

with =path:line= citations, measured numbers where cheap, and merged-PR
reconciliation.  Both were required to read the relevant =_/CONTENTS.org= in
full --- LOGBOOK and WAITING entries included --- and to report contradictions

it in every dispatch brief.
