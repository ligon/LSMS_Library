* 0. Scope, method and epistemic rules

This file assembles evidence.  It does *not* make the keep-or-reacquire
decision that GH #140 asks a human for.

Conventions used throughout, and enforced:

- "the catalog says X" and "the data is X" are kept apart.  Catalog metadata is
  a claim by a cataloguer; a byte count or a Stata header is a property of the
  bits we hold.
- Every external claim carries its URL and the date it was retrieved.
- Where a fetch failed, that is recorded as a failure.  Nothing in this file is
  supplied from memory.
- Where a finding was *reproduced this session*, it says so.  Where it is
  quoted from prior work in the repository, it is attributed and marked as not
  re-verified.

* 1. Idiosyncrasies found in the country documentation

The task required naming these, not merely confirming they were read.

** From =_/CONTENTS.org=

1. *GSPS is a panel, not three independent cross-sections, and only wave 1 has
   a documented probability sample of Ghana.*  Verbatim: "Only wave 1 has a
   documented probability sample of Ghana.  Waves 2 and 3 are follow-ups of
   that sample plus households split off from it, and *no document in the
   corpus states a universe for them*."  This governs any pooled use.
2. *Wave 2 is 5009 wave-1 households + 475 split-offs = 5484 selected, 4774
   interviewed.*  The 475 split-offs "enter through the tracking rule, not
   through a probability draw from a national frame."
3. *=2013-14= carries no region and no urban/rural variable.*  Verbatim:
   "'2013-14' has no regions or urban/rural data. ... For now, all of 2013-14
   has Ghana set as its region."  CONTENTS.org is explicit that this is a
   *data-availability* fact about the shipped extract, "*not* a coverage or
   universe claim, and must not be read as one."
4. *The wave-1 sample is deliberately not self-weighting*: Upper East (16 EAs)
   and Upper West (12 EAs) were over-sampled, so "unweighted GSPS wave-1 means
   are not national means."
5. *5010 vs 5009*: 5010 sampled, 5009 interviewed; the Basic Information
   Document reconciles it ("only one sampled household could not be included
   for reason of data incompleteness").
6. *Household membership requires 6 months' residence* -- a person-level
   inclusion rule that matters for roster-derived counts.
7. *Wave-label discrepancy.*  Our directory is =2017-18=; the local instrument
   cover page reads "WAVE 3 (2018-2019)"; the IHSN entry is "Socioeconomic
   Panel Survey 2018-2019, Wave 3".  CONTENTS.org: "The identification of this
   directory with GSPS Wave 3 rests on the local instrument's cover page, not
   on the microdata."
8. *Never conflate wave 3 with the 2019 "extremely poor" northern sample* -- a
   separate purposive sample, "*not* present in =GhanaSPS/2017-18/=".
9. *No "Universe" field exists anywhere locally*; the only one is WB catalog
   metadata for wave 1, "outside the repository and revisable without notice."
10. *CONTENTS.org asserts waves 2 and 3 are not in the WB Microdata Library.*
    Verbatim (wave 2): "*not* the World Bank catalog -- GSPS Wave 2 is not in
    the WB Microdata Library at all."  *This session VERIFIES that claim* --
    see section 4.1.
11. *Wave 3's community instrument is titled "RURAL COMMUNITY INSTRUMENT"* and
    CONTENTS.org warns this "scopes the community-level module only" and is not
    a rural restriction on the household sample.
